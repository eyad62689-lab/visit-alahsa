An honest gift from Al-Ahsa comes out of the oasis itself: from its palms, its citrus and the hands of its artisans. This guide sorts out what deserves a place in your suitcase, and where to find it in the city's heritage souqs.

## Dates: The Crown of the Oasis

In the world's largest oasis, dates are the first gift without rival. **Khalas** is the master variety — golden as fresh rutab in summer, amber as dried dates at season's end — alongside storied local varieties: Shishi, Razeez, Barhi and more. Each variety's season, fresh and dried, is documented on the [Oasis Fruits](/en/fruits/) page.

## Hasawi Lime and the Oasis Pantry

The **Hasawi lime** (Bin Zuhairi) is among the most celebrated produce of Al-Ahsa's groves — fresh in its summer season, or dried to perfume Hasawi cooking all year round. From the same pantry come **date syrup**, which sweetens the local dishes, and **date bread** sprinkled with nigella seed. The [Hasawi cuisine](/en/food/) page shows the place each one holds at the table.

## Handicrafts: What Outlasts the Trip

- **The Hasawi bisht** — the cloak of ceremony, embroidered in *zari* thread coated with gold or silver water, a weave handed down through Al-Ahsa families generation after generation.
- **Dougha Al-Gharash pottery** — vessels shaped the old manual way at the foot of Jabal Al-Qarah, bought from the craft house itself after watching the stages of the work.
- **Palm-frond work** — woven from the palm's leaves, present among the local crafts in the heritage souq shops.
- **Coffee and spices** — from Qaisariyah's passages, fragrant with both.

The full craft tour — where each is made and how to see it live — is in the [living crafts guide](/en/blog/al-ahsa-traditional-crafts/).

## Where to Buy

- [Qaisariyah Souq](/en/attractions/qaisariyah/) — the heart of heritage shopping for over two centuries: bishts, frond-work, dates, coffee and spices. Entry is free, and evening is its best hour.
- [Craftsmen Souq](/en/attractions/craftsmen-souq/) — 112 shops run by the craftspeople themselves in historic central Hofuf, with a traditional coffee house and a tannour bakery.
- [Dougha Al-Gharash](/en/attractions/duqat-algharash/) — pottery at its source, by Jabal Al-Qarah.
- And the cluster of adjoining souqs in Hofuf's heart — among them [Al-Hamidiyah Souq](/en/attractions/hamidiyah-souq/), rebuilt as part of the historic downtown development.
