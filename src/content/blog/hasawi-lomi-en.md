Say "Al-Ahsa" and people think of dates — this is, after all, the largest palm oasis in the world. But the oasis's summer has a second fruit with a standing of its own in homes, souqs and kitchens: the **Hasawi lime**, known to its people as the **lomi**. A small green fruit whose fragrance reaches you before you see it, it has become Al-Ahsa's second crop after dates — celebrated so warmly that it has an entire annual exhibition to its name.

In this article: what sets the Hasawi lomi apart, when its season falls, how it fills the household pantry and flavours the local kitchen, and your guide to its annual exhibition.

## What Makes the Hasawi Lomi Special?

The Hasawi lomi — known locally as **Bin Zuhairi** — is a small, dark-green, thin-skinned fruit bursting with juice, sharply tart, with a piercing fragrance that sets it apart from all other citrus. As documented on our [Fruits of the Oasis](/en/fruits/) page, more than **100,000 bearing lime trees** spread across Al-Ahsa's groves; a single tree yields 25–30 kg a season, and in season it sells for SAR 15–20 per kilo.

## Its Season: The Oasis Summer

The lomi's season is summer, starting **from around mid-June** — arriving at the height of the heat, just as the oasis's groves are also busy with summer rutab. To meet the fruit on its tree, there is the [Hasawi Lemon Farm](/en/attractions/lemon-farm/), a citrus farm where visitors pick the famous lemon in season. For what the oasis bears in every season, take the full tour on the [seasonal fruits page](/en/fruits/).

## From Tree to Pantry: Jameed, the Sun-Dried Lime

A cherished Hasawi summer custom surrounds the lomi: in its season, families gather to press and preserve it — the practice known as **"jameed"**, or sun-dried lime — laying in a supply for the whole year. It is used fresh, as juice, and dried, perfuming the dishes of Hasawi and Gulf cooking all year round.

## In the Hasawi Kitchen

Lift the lid of a Hasawi pot and the lomi is there: [Hasawi rice](/en/food/) — Al-Ahsa's most famous dish — simmers with its spices of lomi, cinnamon and cardamom until it takes on its red-brown colour, while the heritage dish **mufallaq** is cracked wheat flavoured with lomi and crowned with dates and boiled eggs. The full tour of the dishes is on the [Hasawi cuisine page](/en/food/).

## The Hasawi Lomi Exhibition: An Annual Celebration

Such is the lomi's standing that the governorate celebrates it every year at the **Hasawi Lomi Exhibition** — Al-Ahsa's flagship seasonal agricultural event, organised by the **Al-Ahsa Chamber** in partnership with the Al-Ahsa Development Authority. The exhibition offers an interactive look at the lomi's qualities, benefits and cultivation, direct contact with the farmers, and shopping from productive-family stalls — alongside live cooking, agricultural workshops, a kids' corner and family sessions.

The **2026 edition** runs from **19 August to 13 September** (after its closing was extended) at the Al-Ahsa Expo Center on Al-Salam Street (Ain Najm Road) in Hofuf, daily from 5:00 to 11:00 PM. According to the official booking page on Evento: tickets are SAR 10 from Sunday to Wednesday and SAR 15 from Thursday to Saturday online (with SAR 5 added at the on-site box office), and entry is free for children under 5.

Up-to-date dates, the booking link and the map are always on the [exhibition's page](/en/events/hasawi-lomi-exhibition/) — the reference point if anything changes between editions.
