## Answers

(a) Cemid is a liquid. It is lime juice made by squeezing the limes, then leaving the juice in the sun and letting it stand/age in glass jars. It is kept for use all year. The text gives no duration, no added ingredients, and no precise steps. Whether the jars are in the sun for the whole time is not stated. Nothing says it is a paste or a solid.

(b) Yes. Passage 5 says dried lime ("kurutulmuş meyve", "kurutulmuş lomi") is used and flavors food all year. Passage 6 mentions "güneşte kurutma" (sun drying) as ancestral knowledge, without naming the object. Cemid itself is not dried.
