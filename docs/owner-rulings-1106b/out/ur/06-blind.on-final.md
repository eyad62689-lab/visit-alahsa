# Blind read: location line («جگہ») of the Creative Al-Ahsa Festival

Line read (identical on the event page and the list card):
«بلدیہ کے قلعے کے اندر واقع الفریج نامی روایتی محلہ»

## 1) Where is the festival held

In my own words: it is held in a traditional neighbourhood called "Al-Fareej", which sits inside the municipality's fort. The line names two places: the fort (the municipality's fort) as the container, and the neighbourhood Al-Fareej inside it. The fort is the larger place; Al-Fareej is the actual venue within it. I read it as one location, with the fort as its enclosing setting, not as two separate venues.

## 2) Stumbles in the location line

- P1. «بلدیہ کے قلعے» ("the municipality's fort"). I first read it as a fort that belongs to or is run by the municipality, which is an odd idea. I was unsure whether it is a specific named fort or a generic one. Because no fort name is given, I could not tell which fort it is or how to find it. Minor stumble: the meaning is clear, the identity is vague.
- P2. «الفریج نامی» ("named Al-Fareej"). The "Al-" form is Arabic, not natural Urdu. I first wondered whether it is a place name or a common word. "نامی" then told me it is a name, so this resolved quickly. It does not say what a "فریج" is, though «روایتی محلہ» (traditional neighbourhood) helps.
- P3. Word order. The long modifier «بلدیہ کے قلعے کے اندر واقع» comes before «الفریج نامی روایتی محلہ». I first read "inside the fort" as modifying the whole phrase, and only afterwards saw that it locates the neighbourhood. Understandable, but heavy: two genitives and "کے اندر واقع" stacked before the head noun.
- P4. Nothing says which city the fort is in. The organiser is «الاحساء کی بلدیہ», so I inferred Al-Ahsa from that line, not from the location line itself.

## 3) Readability of the location line

78/100. It is grammatical and the meaning is clear. It loses points for the stacked modifiers before the head noun, the unnamed fort, and the Arabic-looking name.
