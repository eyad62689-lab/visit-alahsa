# Blind read, on-05 (Creative Al-Ahsa Festival), Urdu location line

Location line (identical on both the event page and the list card):
"الفریج نامی روایتی محلہ بلدیہ کے قلعے کے اندر"

## 1) Where is the festival held
I read it as ONE place: a traditional neighbourhood called "Al-Fraij" (الفریج), and that neighbourhood lies inside the municipality's fort ("the fort of the baladiyah"). So the line names two things, but they are nested, not separate venues: the fort is the container and Al-Fraij is the old quarter inside it. I did not read it as two alternative venues. The event is in a single heritage quarter, which sits within a fort belonging to or named for the municipality.

## 2) Stumbles in the location line
- P1: "محلہ بلدیہ" in "روایتی محلہ بلدیہ کے قلعے". On a first read I took "محلہ بلدیہ" as one compound, a "municipality neighbourhood" (like "Mohalla Baladia"), and then "کے قلعے کے اندر" seemed to hang loose. I had to reread to see that "بلدیہ کے قلعے" (the municipality's fort) is a separate unit.
- P2: "بلدیہ کے قلعے" (the municipality's fort). I first took it as a proper name, a fort called "Baladiyah". Then I wondered whether a municipality owns a fort, or whether this is the fort that houses the municipality. The relationship is not clear. In Urdu, "بلدیہ" normally means a municipal body, so a "municipality's fort" sounds odd.
- P3: "الفریج" is written with the Arabic spelling and the Arabic "ال" prefix. I read it correctly as a name, but I did not recognise it as a place and had no clue it is a quarter of Al-Ahsa. It would help to say that it is in Al-Ahsa, or to write the name the way Urdu usually does.
- P4: "نامی" ("named") comes before "روایتی محلہ". It reads as "a traditional neighbourhood named Al-Fraij", but the order "الفریج نامی روایتی محلہ" pushes the name before the noun. I paused for a moment. It is understandable but a little stiff.

Not in the location line, but noticed: on the list card, the date and duration lines are fused with no space ("مارچ سے اپریل تکتقریباً ایک ہفتہ"). It looks like a rendering or markup artefact.

## 3) Readability of the location line
72 / 100. The idea (a heritage quarter inside a fort) comes through, but the "محلہ بلدیہ" run-on and the unclear "municipality's fort" cost me a reread.
