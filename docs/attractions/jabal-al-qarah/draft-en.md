Jabal Al-Qarah rises east of Hofuf in the middle of a green oasis filled with palms [ح-01، ح-02], and its caves, not its height, are what make it special. The mountain is a flat-topped hill of sedimentary rock carved by erosion [ح-04، ح-40], threaded with caves and passages that stay pleasantly cool at the height of summer and gently warm in winter [ح-37، ح-39]. It lies within Component 01 of "Al-Ahsa Oasis, an Evolving Cultural Landscape", inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2018 [ح-18، ح-19]; Al-Ahsa is the largest oasis in the world [ح-21].

## History

Jabal Al-Qarah was once known as Jabal Al-Shab'an [ح-10]. Four historic villages surround it, Al-Tuwaithir, Al-Dalwah, Al-Tahimiyah and Al-Qarah, and the mountain takes its name from the last of them [ح-12]. Before air conditioning spread, its caves were where people from the nearby villages escaped the summer heat, especially during the daytime in Ramadan [ح-14، ح-15]. In 2016 the [Land of Civilisations](/en/attractions/ardh-alhadarat/) cultural tourism project opened in part [ح-22], and in January 2018 it opened officially with a tourist trail, a leisure trail and an information hall [ح-23]; according to 2020 coverage, the project occupies about 10% of the mountain [ح-26].

## What you'll see

According to the Saudipedia encyclopaedia, Jabal Al-Qarah has 12 caves of different shapes and lengths [ح-27، ح-28], among them Al-Nashab, Al-Naqa (also written Al-Naqah), Al-Eid, Al-Mahyoub and Bu Saleh [ح-29، ح-30]. According to the Saudi Tourism Authority, these caves contain 28 long passages stretching 1.5 kilometres [ح-31]. In Al-Nashab cave, long, narrow, tall corridors cross one another at right angles [ح-34], and its galleries rise to the top of the mountain through openings that let daylight in [ح-35]. You find yourself between two walls that almost touch your shoulders, then the rock opens onto a high chamber pierced by a shaft of light. According to Saudipedia, the caves stay close to 20°C even when it is over 40°C outside [ح-36، ح-38]. Weathering has shaped mushroom-like forms in the mountain's rock [ح-40], and its summit looks out over the governorate's palms and the surrounding towns [ح-41].

According to the Saudi Tourism Authority, some caves are used for workshops by traditional potters [ح-42]. According to the Al-Yaum newspaper in 2023, the mountain park has a mosque, a café and a gift shop [ح-46], and lighting and a footbridge have been installed in the mountain area [ح-47].

## Best time to visit

The Al-Yaum newspaper (2023) describes Jabal Al-Qarah as a year-round destination thanks to the atmosphere of its caves [ح-52], and as a good spot for watching birds such as the hoopoe, the bee-eater and the bulbul during the migration season [ح-53].

## Getting there

The mountain is in the town of Al-Qarah, which is also home to [Dougha Al-Gharash Pottery](/en/attractions/duqat-algharash/) [ح-56]. You can find it on the [attractions map](/en/map/).

## Visitor tips

The Saudi Tourism Authority notes that guided tours are available to introduce you to the history of Jabal Al-Qarah [ح-58]. A cave researcher cautions that some of the cave routes are difficult and need experts or people from the nearby areas [ح-59], and the Al-Yaum newspaper advises anyone climbing the mountain to take care and go with a guide [ح-57]. Hours and fees are in the visit card above. Before you go, read the [guide to visiting the caves of Jabal Al-Qarah](/en/blog/jabal-al-qarah-guide/), and if you want to fold the visit into a longer trip, see [48 hours in Al-Ahsa](/en/blog/48-hours-in-al-ahsa/).
