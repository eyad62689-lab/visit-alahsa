# Blind read: terrain-map intro (de)
## 1) What the page offers and what to do
This is a relief/terrain map of the Al-Ahsa oasis showing every attraction at its real location, with a list of all sights below it. To make a place appear on the map, I press the small icon button next to its name in the list (tooltip "Auf der Karte anzeigen"). To open a place's own page, I click the name itself (e.g. "Al-Hamidiyah-Souk"). Yes, it is clear: button = show on map, name = open page. The only mild uncertainty is that the icon is unlabeled apart from its tooltip, so on touch devices I cannot see the tooltip before pressing; "die Schaltfläche neben einem Namen" is still enough to find it.

## 2) Stumbles
P1. "ReiseführerGeländekarte": the two words run together with no space or separator; I first read it as one odd compound word (probably a breadcrumb/label "Reiseführer" + page title "Geländekarte"). Minor, likely a layout artifact.
P2. "Geländekarte": understood as a terrain/topographic map; fine, though "Reliefkarte" would match "im Relief" in the next line more closely. Very minor.
P3. "Jabal al-Qarah (der Qarah-Berg)": "Jabal" means mountain, so "Qarah-Berg" repeats it, but the gloss is helpful. Fine.
P4. "jedes Ausflugsziel": "Ausflugsziel" sounds slightly like a day-trip destination, while the list heading says "Sehenswürdigkeiten"; two different words for the same thing, a tiny inconsistency, not a real problem.

## 3) Readability of the two intro lines
90/100: Natural, clear German with a precise instruction about button versus name; only the tiny wording variation (Ausflugsziel/Sehenswürdigkeiten) and the unlabeled icon keep it from perfect.
