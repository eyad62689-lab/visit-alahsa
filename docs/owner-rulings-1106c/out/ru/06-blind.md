# Blind read: terrain-map intro (ru)
## 1) What the page offers and what to do
This is a relief (terrain) map of the oasis, with Jabal al-Qara, palm groves and dunes in the east, and every sight placed on it. Below the map there is a list of sights grouped by category (e.g. historical ones). To make a place appear on the map, I press the small icon button next to its name (tooltip "Показать на карте"). To open the place's own page, I press the name itself. It is clear which to press for each: button = show on map, name = open page. The only small doubt is that the icon button is described only by a tooltip, so on first sight I might not know what the icon means until I hover.

## 2) Stumbles
1. "ПутеводительКарта рельефа" - looked like a glued-together word at first ("Путеводитель" + "Карта рельефа"). Probably a breadcrumb/heading run together in the cut text; I understood it as "Guide / Relief map". Minor.
2. "Джабаль-эль-Кара (гора Эль-Кара)" - clear thanks to the parenthetical, but the doubled name (Джабаль + гора) feels slightly redundant. Natural enough for a transliterated name.
3. "и каждая достопримечательность на своём месте" - slightly unusual/marketing-like; I first read it as "every sight is in its place" (figurative, "everything is as it should be") before realising it means each sight is marked on the map. Understandable but a little vague.
4. "пальмовые рощи и дюны на востоке" - the position of "на востоке" could attach only to the dunes or to the whole list; minor ambiguity.

Otherwise none.

## 3) Readability of the two intro lines
88/100 - Natural, clear Russian with a clear instruction (button vs. name); only the slightly figurative "на своём месте" and the glued heading hold it back.
