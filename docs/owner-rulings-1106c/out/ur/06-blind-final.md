# Fresh read on final: terrain-map instruction (ur)

## 1) What to do
To see a place on the map, I look at the list below, find the small icon next to the place name (for example next to "العقیر" or "بیت البیعہ"), and press that small icon. The place then appears on the map. To open the place's own page, I press the name itself (the name text). On a phone I could find both: the small icon sits right beside each name, and the name is the text next to it. The icon has no visible label for me (the tooltip is not shown before pressing), so I would press the small icon beside the name for the map, and the name for the page.

## 2) Stumbles
P1. "چھوٹے سے نشان کو چن کر" — "چن کر" (choose/pick) is a slightly odd verb for pressing an icon; I first understood "select/choose", not "tap". I still guessed it means tap. Also "نشان" (mark/sign) is vague: I did not know it was an icon button until I saw the list; it could be a symbol, a tick, or a pin.
P2. "ہر نام کے ساتھ والے" — fine, but it does not say where (before or after the name), so I had to look.
P3. "نام کو کھول کر" — "open the name" is a little unusual; I understood it as tapping the name. Natural but slightly stiff.

## 3) Readability of the instruction line
78/100 — Clear and understandable overall, but "چن کر" and the vague "چھوٹے سے نشان" make the pressing action less direct than a plain "دبائیں / ٹیپ کریں" would.
