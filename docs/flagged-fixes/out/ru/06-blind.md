# Observations — 7st. Speciality Coffee (/ru/)

## What I understood as a reader
- Where: a café in the Er-Rifaa ash-Shamaliya district of Al-Hofuf (Al-Ahsa), next to the Qasr Ibrahim fortress, opened in 2025; specialty coffee and espresso. Next to the old Al-Kut quarter (Qasr Ibrahim, Al-Jabri mosque, streets leading to Al-Qaisariya market), so it fits into a walk around the old town.
- Two branches: (1) 7st Эль-Байт, which is this same café by the fortress; (2) 7st Эль-Кинна, in the Es-Salam quarter in the "Золотой пояс" area. Where that second area is, or how far it is from Hofuf, I cannot tell.
- The name: the Arabic name «Макха-Шари 7» means "Кофейня «Улица 7»", which is where "7st" comes from (7 + street). I understand it as a proper name.
- The search hint (footer version): Esc closes the dialog, the arrow keys move through results, Enter opens the selected result.

## Issues
1. minor — «пишет своё название сразу в двух системах письма» sounds bookish and a little odd for a café ("writes its name"). «указывает название двумя письменностями» or «на вывеске название дано по-арабски — «Макха-Шари 7» — и латиницей — «7st»» would be more natural. Also, the page never shows the Arabic script, so a Russian reader has to take "two writing systems" on trust.
2. serious — «7st Эль-Кинна в квартале Эс-Салям в районе Золотого пояса»: nothing explains what «Золотой пояс» is (a district? a ring road? a commercial strip?) or which city it is in. "район" is also used for Er-Rifaa ash-Shamaliya, so the hierarchy is confusing. I cannot place the second branch.
3. minor — The branch names «Эль-Байт» and «Эль-Кинна» are given with no gloss, while the café name is glossed. It is inconsistent, and I don't know whether these are place names or branch nicknames.
4. minor — «7st Эль-Байт — это и есть то заведение, что рядом с крепостью» is conversational and clumsy. «7st Эль-Байт (то самое кафе у крепости)» reads more smoothly.
5. minor — Search hint at the top of the page (line 11): «Esc закрыть · стрелки — переход · Enter — открыть». The dash after Esc is missing, so the first item breaks the "key — action" pattern. The footer copy (line 73) has «Esc — закрыть», which is correct, so the two copies differ.
6. minor — In the hint the three actions use mixed forms: «закрыть», «открыть» are infinitives but «переход» is a noun. «стрелки — перейти» or «стрелки — выбор» would be more uniform. It is still understandable.
7. minor — «Дополните свой день» is a slight calque. «Что ещё посмотреть рядом» would be more natural.
8. minor — «рейтинг и часы работы подгружаются оттуда» but the page shows no rating or opening hours, so the note promises something I don't see.
9. minor — «Сохранённые места 0RU» has the counter and the language code glued together (probably a text-extraction artifact, but if it is visible like this it looks broken).

## Directed questions
(a) Yes. «Макха-Шари 7» (дословно «Кофейня „Улица 7“») reads as the café's name, "Street 7 Café", because the inner quotation marks make «Улица 7» a name. It does not read as "a street's café". Only the phrasing "пишет своё название" is a little awkward.
(b) No. «Золотой пояс» is never explained. I can't tell whether it is a district, a road or a commercial zone, or where it is relative to Hofuf (serious, item 2).
(c) The footer copy is consistent («Esc — закрыть · стрелки — переход · Enter — открыть»). The copy at the top of the page lacks the dash after Esc («Esc закрыть»). In both, «переход» is a noun among infinitives (minor).
