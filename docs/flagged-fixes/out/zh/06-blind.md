# Reader observations — /zh/restaurants-cafes/7st/

My understanding as a traveller:
- Where the café is: Al-Rifaa North district (里法阿北区), Hofuf, right next to Ibrahim Palace (易卜拉欣宫). Opened 2025. Mainly specialty coffee and espresso, plus other drinks and desserts.
- Two other branches in Al-Ahsa: 7st Al-Bait, next to Ibrahim Palace; and 7st Al-Kinnah, in Al-Salam district (萨拉姆区) inside an area called "黄金地带".
- Nearby: Al-Kut district (库特区), Hofuf's oldest quarter, with Ibrahim Palace and Al-Jabri Mosque, reaching to Al-Qaisariya Market. The café is within walking distance of the old town. Suggested next stops are Al-Hamidiya Market and Ibrahim Palace.

1. serious — The main café is described as "紧邻易卜拉欣宫", and then one of the two *other* branches, "紧邻易卜拉欣宫的 7st Al-Bait", is also next to Ibrahim Palace. Because the text says "另设两家分店" (two branches besides this one), I can't tell whether 7st Al-Bait is this café or a different shop a few metres away. If I want to go to Ibrahim Palace, which one am I going to?
2. minor — "均由店家自行命名" sounds odd and adds nothing. Of course the shop names its own branches. It reads like a translated internal note (maybe "names as the shop gives them"). I would just delete it.
3. minor — "位于“黄金地带”地区萨拉姆区内": the quotation marks plus "地区" suggest a proper place name, but "黄金地带" is also an ordinary Chinese phrase meaning "prime location". Without a transliteration or the original name, I can't search for it on a map. "萨拉姆区内" after "地区" is also a heavy stack of place words.
4. minor — "阿拉伯文（音译 Maqha Shari' 7）": I get the transliteration but not the meaning. A short gloss would help, for example "意为‘7号街咖啡馆’", if that is right. Otherwise "7st" means nothing to a Chinese reader.
5. minor — "这里以精品咖啡与意式浓缩咖啡为主打" followed later by "除精品咖啡外": the repetition is a bit clumsy. Also "意式浓缩咖啡" is a kind of coffee, so pairing it with "精品咖啡" is slightly odd, though understandable.
6. minor — The location line "里法阿北区——胡富夫" puts the small unit first. Chinese order is big to small: "胡富夫·里法阿北区".
7. minor — "评分与营业时间会随每次网站更新从谷歌地图获取", but the page shows no rating and no opening hours. I'm left wondering whether something failed to load.
8. minor — "在谷歌地图中打开 ↗（在新窗口中打开谷歌地图）" repeats itself. It's fine for a screen reader, but it looks redundant when shown as text.
9. minor — Place-name forms are mixed: "哈米迪耶" uses a Turkish-style ending, while "凯撒利亚" and "里法阿" are rendered from Arabic. It's not wrong, just inconsistent in style.
10. minor — "与咖啡馆相邻的库特区…这家咖啡馆恰好处在老城步行游览的范围之内" is one long sentence and logically fine. "恰好" is a slightly odd word choice, and "就在…步行范围内" would be more natural.
