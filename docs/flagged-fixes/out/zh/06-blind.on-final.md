# Reader observations: /zh/restaurants-cafes/7st/

## What I understood as a traveller
- Where: 胡富夫 (Hofuf), 里法阿北区, right next to 易卜拉欣宫. Opened 2025; specialty coffee and espresso, plus other drinks and desserts.
- Branches: two in 哈萨. One is 7st Al-Bait, next to 易卜拉欣宫, and the page says this is the one on this page. The other is 7st Al-Kinnah, in 萨拉姆区 in the “黄金地带” area.
- What is nearby: the 库特区 next to it is Hofuf's oldest quarter, with 易卜拉欣宫 and 贾布里清真寺, and it runs to 凯撒利亚集市. So the café sits on an old-town walking route. Suggested next stops: 哈米迪耶集市 and 易卜拉欣宫.

## Numbered observations
1. minor — “店名同时用阿拉伯文（音译 Maqha Shari' 7）和拉丁字母“7st”两种文字书写”: it says the name is written in Arabic script, then shows only a Latin transliteration. As a Chinese reader I can't see the Arabic form or work out what “Shari' 7” means (street 7?). Nothing tells me how to say “7st” either.
2. minor — “均由店家自行命名”: this is an odd, unneeded aside. “Named by the shop itself” is true of every shop. It reads like a translation leftover and makes me wonder whether the branch names are unofficial.
3. minor — “坐落在胡富夫的里法阿北区，紧邻易卜拉欣宫” and then “与咖啡馆相邻的库特区……区内有易卜拉欣宫”: the café is in one district and the palace is in the next one. That is consistent, but on a first read I paused to check that the café is not itself in 库特区.
4. minor — “位于“黄金地带”地区萨拉姆区”: the quote marks plus 地区 make it read like a translated nickname, not a place name I could type into a map. The stacked form X地区Y区 is also a little heavy.
5. minor — “以精品咖啡与意式浓缩咖啡为主打 … 除精品咖啡外”: 精品咖啡 appears twice in a short paragraph. Slightly repetitive.
6. minor — 关于本页 says “评分与营业时间会随每次网站更新从谷歌地图获取”, but the page shows no rating and no opening hours. I was left wondering whether data is missing.
7. minor — the title says “7st. Speciality Coffee” but the body says “7st 咖啡馆”. That's acceptable, but the English title has no Chinese gloss.

Overall the Chinese reads naturally. I found no serious errors.

## Directed questions
(a) Yes. “一家是 7st Al-Bait，即紧邻易卜拉欣宫的这一家；另一家是……7st Al-Kinnah” clearly says this page is Al-Bait beside 易卜拉欣宫 and that Al-Kinnah is the other branch. The only small doubt comes from “均由店家自行命名” (item 2).
(b) Partly. With the quote marks, “黄金地带”地区 reads as a named area, but more like a nickname or translated label than an official, searchable district name. I could not find it on a map with it.
