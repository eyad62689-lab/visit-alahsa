# Observations (native zh-CN reader)

## What each intro tells me to do
- 景点地图 (/zh/map/): search or filter by category, then 点击 any sight in the list or on the map to open its info card. Clear. Works with a mouse; on a phone, 点击 is the normal word for a tap too, so it works for me there as well.
- 地形图 (/zh/terrain-map/): 3D view of the oasis; 点击 any name in the list to see it on the map. Clear, and fine for both mouse and finger. There is also an "打开地形图" button and a "经典地图 →" link, both easy to understand.
- 我的行程 (/zh/plan/): the list is empty; browse sights, 点击 "收藏景点" to collect places here, then get a ready-made route. I understand it. It works for mouse and tap.
- 餐厅与咖啡馆 (/zh/restaurants-cafes/): a guide to restaurants, cafés and date-bread bakeries; each one has a photo and its Google Maps location, "点击即达"; the list keeps being updated. Mostly clear. What 点击即达 opens is a little vague (see item 3). Works for mouse and tap.

## Language issues
1. minor — 地图: "即可打开它的信息卡". "它的" reads a bit translated. More natural: "即可查看详情" or "即可打开其信息卡".
2. minor — 地形图: "绿洲立体呈现：卡拉山、椰枣林与东边的沙丘". The opening is a headline-style fragment and a bit abrupt inside running text. Something like "以立体视角呈现绿洲：……" flows better. It is still understandable.
3. minor — 餐厅: "每处均附照片及其谷歌地图位置，点击即达". "点击即达" ("click and you arrive") leaves out what I am clicking and where I end up. I guess it means it opens Google Maps for directions, but "点击即可在谷歌地图中导航" would be clearer. Practical note: Google Maps does not work in mainland China without a VPN, so many mainland visitors can't use these links from home. Abroad in Saudi Arabia it is fine.
4. minor — 行程: "再获取一条现成路线". "现成" is slightly odd here, because the route is produced from my own picks. "即可生成一条推荐路线" sounds more natural.
5. minor — 餐厅: "既有传统氛围与民间风味". "民间风味" is understandable, but "地方风味" or "家常风味" is the usual collocation for food.

No serious issues. Nothing I read was wrong or misleading.

## Directed question
For each intro, does its action verb fit both a mouse click and a finger tap?
- 景点地图: "点击列表或地图中的任意景点". Yes. In Chinese UI text 点击 is the standard verb for both clicking and tapping.
- 地形图: "点击列表中的任意名称". Yes, same reason.
- 我的行程: "点击“收藏景点”". Yes.
- 餐厅与咖啡馆: "点击即达". Yes, the verb fits both. Only its object is vague (item 3).
None of the four uses a mouse-only verb such as 悬停, 右键 or 双击, or a touch-only one such as 长按 or 滑动.
