# Blind reading report (German reader) - Gulf excerpts

## What I understood each geographic name to refer to

- **Arabischer Golf**: I take it to be the gulf east of Saudi Arabia, i.e. what I know as "Persischer Golf" (or neutral "der Golf"). I only worked that out from context (Katar, Dammam, Ostküste). "Arabischer Golf" is unfamiliar in German. It could also be confused with the Golf von Aden, the Arabisches Meer or the Roten Meer.
- **Region am Arabischen Golf / Länder am Arabischen Golf**: the Gulf region in general, and the Gulf states (the GCC) in the second phrase. It is never clear whether "Region" means the Gulf coast, the Eastern Province or the Gulf states. It is used about 20 times.
- **Al-Ahsa**: used for the oasis, the governorate ("gehört zum Gouvernorat al-Ahsa"), the wider region and sometimes the city. Salwa lies "im äußersten Südosten Al-Ahsas", 150 km from Hofuf. Hofuf is the main city inside it.
- **Nadschd**: the central Arabian highlands, the hinterland for which Al-Uqair was the "Tor zum Meer". It is never explained.
- **Halbinsel**: the Arabian Peninsula ("Herz der Halbinsel"). It is never named.
- **Ostküste Saudi-Arabiens / Küste des Arabischen Golfs östlich von Al-Ahsa**: the coast where Al-Uqair lies.
- **Jafurah-Wüste**: a dune desert west of Salwa beach.
- **Qarah-Berg**: the mountain near Dougha Al-Gharash.
- **Al-Madina** (in "Al-Madina – Al-Ahsa-Nationalpark"): I read it as Medina, the holy city. I assume it is something else.
- **Al-Khaleej-Straße**: a road in Hofuf. I only worked out that "Khaleej" means "Golf" because the rest of the page uses the translated form.
- **Dammam, Katar, Oman, Irak, Kuwait, Qatif, Riad, AlUla, Medina**: understood.

## Findings

### Names and consistency

1. **/de/attractions/al-uqair/ and nearly every page** - "Arabischen Golf" / "Arabischer Golf". I understood the Persian Gulf, but only by inference. A German reader expects "Persischer Golf" or "Golf". The unfamiliar name makes me wonder whether another sea is meant. It also appears so often that it reads mechanical.
2. **Many pages (qaisariyah, hubaish, hasawi-lomi-lime, online-stores)** - "Region am Arabischen Golf", "Länder am Arabischen Golf", "alle arabischen Länder am Arabischen Golf". The phrase is clumsy, vague and repeated. The last one is tautological ("arabisch ... Arabischer"). "Golfstaaten" or "Golfregion" would be natural.
3. **/de/trails/al-asfar-and-national-park/** - "Al-Madina – Al-Ahsa-Nationalpark". I read "Al-Madina" as Medina and was confused. I could not tell whether it is a place name, a district or a source title.
4. **/de/attractions/salwa-beach/** - "Salwa gehört zum Gouvernorat al-Ahsa, dem übergeordneten Verwaltungsbezirk." The spelling "al-Ahsa" in lower case differs from "Al-Ahsa" everywhere else and from "Gouvernorat Al-Ahsa" on /de/online-stores/. The sentence also breaks the flow, and "Südosten Al-Ahsas" turns out to mean the governorate, not the oasis.
5. **Whole site** - Al-Ahsa is used for oasis, governorate and region interchangeably, e.g. "Oase Al-Ahsa", "Gouvernorat Al-Ahsa", "Zentrum von Hofuf – Al-Ahsa", "Hofuf" vs "Al-Ahsa". As a visitor I did not know whether Hofuf is inside or beside it.
6. **/de/attractions/al-uqair/ and /de/attractions/uqair-castle/** - "das Tor Al-Ahsas zur Welt", "das Tor Nadschds zum Meer", "wirtschaftliches Tor", "Handelsader Al-Ahsas". The same port is the gateway of Al-Ahsa on one page and of Nadschd on another. Nadschd is never explained.
7. **/de/attractions/uqair-castle/ vs /de/trails/al-uqair-coast/** - "Uqair-Protokoll ... das die Grenzen festlegte" vs "Konferenz von Al-Uqair ... Grenzen von Nadschd zum Irak und zu Kuwait" vs "Verhandlungen, in denen die Grenzen der Region gezogen wurden". Protocol, conference and negotiations are three names for one event. "Grenzen der Region" is misleading, and the castle page does not say which borders.
8. **/de/attractions/uqair-mosque/ vs /de/attractions/hubaish/** - "Programm zur Wiederherstellung historischer Moscheen ... Königliche Kommission für Jubail und Yanbu" vs "Projekt von Prinz Mohammed bin Salman zur Entwicklung historischer Moscheen". It looks like two programmes or two names for one. Why a commission for Jubail and Yanbu is involved is unexplained.
9. **/de/attractions/uqair-mosque/** - "errichtet und ausgebaut" vs the tagline "ausgebaut wurden". Built or expanded is not consistent between tagline and body.
10. **/de/restaurants-cafes/sinyar/** - "Al-Khaleej-Straße" is transliterated while the rest of the site translates Khaleej as "Golf". The road name is not recognisable as "Golfstraße", and "Al-Uwaimriyah" is not explained either.
11. **/de/blog/hasawi-lime-history/** - "Bin Zuhairi" / "banzahir" / "badzahr" / "bezoar": the name appears in several forms. The Fazit lists "Bin Zuhairi" as a name of the fruit, but the etymology paragraph is about "banzahir". I did not see the link between the two.
12. **Lomi-pages (blog/hasawi-lomi-lime, events/hasawi-lomi-exhibition, blog/48-hours)** - the fruit is called "Hasawi-Limette", "Lomi", "Hasawi-Lomi", "Limette", "Zitrusfrucht", "Hasawi-Limette (Lomi)" and "Hasawi-Limette (die Limette aus Al-Ahsa)". "Die Lomi" is feminine, "die Limette" is too, but a reader does not know the terms are the same fruit until much later. The FAQ on /de/blog/hasawi-lomi-lime/ even asks how it differs from "einer gewöhnlichen Zitrone oder Limette".
13. **/de/blog/hasawi-lomi-lime/ and /de/events/hasawi-lomi-exhibition/** - "Jameed". In German "Jameed" is dried yoghurt (Jordan). Here it is "sonnengetrockneter Lomi-Saft". Juice drying in the sun and maturing in glass jars is hard to picture. I assumed an error or a different product.
14. **/de/blog/hasawi-lomi-lime/** - "Mufallaq" is introduced as a "traditionelles Gericht" with no explanation of what it is beyond "Weizenschrot, mit Lomi gewürzt".
15. **/de/blog/hasawi-lomi-lime/** - "Gewürzen aus Lomi, Zimt und Kardamom": Lomi is not a spice in my mind (it is a fruit), and the passage on the pot lid is otherwise very good.
16. **/de/online-stores/** - "Für die Hasawi-Lomi werden Limetten ... in der Sonne getrocknet": circular. The Lomi is a lime, and "Limetten" are dried for it. Later it is sold as "ganze Früchte oder gemahlenes Gewürz".

### Awkward or ungrammatical German

17. **/de/attractions/al-uqair/** - "zwei seltene Bilder aufeinander" - "Bilder treffen aufeinander" is a strange image. A "Bild" is not met. I understood the dunes and the sea, but it felt forced.
18. **/de/attractions/al-uqair/** - "das Herz der Halbinsel ... das Herz rastloser Bewegung": the same metaphor twice in four sentences. "rastlose Bewegung" is vague.
19. **/de/attractions/al-uqair/** - "Sie gehen auf den Spuren der Karawanen und sitzen auf Sand, der das Meer berührt": the "Sie" is generic and lyrical, which does not fit the practical visitor sections below.
20. **/de/attractions/salwa-beach/** - "erstreckt sich der Salwa-Strand am Arabischen Golf entlang" - "am Golf entlang" is odd for a beach on the coast. "Platz zum Meeresangeln" - "Meeresangeln" is unusual (Angeln im Meer / Küstenangeln).
21. **/de/attractions/salwa-beach/** - "am Landübergang des Königreichs nach Katar" - "Grenzübergang" is the German word.
22. **/de/attractions/hubaish/** - "Al-Rifaa-Süd-Viertel": three hyphenated parts, hard to read and to pronounce. "Der Qaisariyah-Souk" lies in "Hofuf, Al-Rifaa-Viertel", so I assumed they are neighbouring districts.
23. **/de/attractions/hubaish/** - "bietet sie etwa 90 Betenden Platz" - grammatically correct, but stiff.
24. **/de/attractions/qasr-ibrahim/** - "Kein Palast im prunkvollen Sinne, sondern eine Festung" contradicts the title "Ibrahim-Palast" and the later "Palastfestung" (/de/blog/is-al-ahsa-worth-visiting/). The correction is fine, but the naming is inconsistent. "Gehen Sie ... sehen Sie ..., und Sie lesen Schichten von Geschichte" mixes imperative and indicative awkwardly.
25. **/de/attractions/qasr-ibrahim/** - "gehört zu jenem städtischen Gefüge, dem die Oase ihre Aufnahme in die Welterbeliste ... verdankt": vague. Which urban fabric?
26. **/de/attractions/qaisariyah/** - "Die „Hasawi-Weberei“ zeichnet sich durch die Feinheit ihrer Handstickerei aus" - weaving vs embroidery. On /de/online-stores/ the Bischt is "von Hand gewebt, und zwar mit Zari-Fäden", while elsewhere the Zari threads are embroidery. A cloak is woven, then embroidered; the text blurs the two.
27. **/de/attractions/qaisariyah/** - "Neben dem Bischt finden Sie einheimisches Kunsthandwerk aus Palmblättern und Keramik": fine, but "Bischt" appears without gloss in the next sentence after the explanation, then as "Hasawi-Bischt", "Bischt", "Umhänge" - plural "seine Umhänge, auch Hasawi-Bischt genannt" (/de/blog/is-al-ahsa-worth-visiting/).
28. **/de/attractions/uqair-castle/** - "das Gebäude des Emirats" - I do not know what a "Gebäude des Emirats" is (the emir's residence?). "die Passstelle" (/de/trails/al-uqair-coast/) - passport office? unclear. "Anlegestelle" is fine.
29. **/de/attractions/uqair-castle/** - "Ende der 1990er-Jahre wurden sie restauriert. Derzeit laufen ... Restaurierungsarbeiten, und einige Bereiche können geschlossen sein": two restorations without explanation. I did not know if the site is open.
30. **/de/attractions/uqair-castle/** - "In früherer Zeit war er das Tor Nadschds": "er" refers to the port two lines back; the first sentence has "die Festung" and "Gebäudekomplex" in between, so the pronoun is ambiguous.
31. **/de/attractions/uqair-beach/** - "eines der ältesten Sommerziele am Arabischen Golf" appears twice in a row (tagline and body), and "historisch" three times in two sentences. "Einrichtungen für Besucher" is vague.
32. **/de/attractions/asfar/ and /de/trails/al-asfar-and-national-park/** - "Bestandteil der UNESCO-Welterbestätte · 1563-012" is cryptic: which site, and what is "1563-012"? The tagline "Eines der größten Gewässer der Region am Arabischen Golf" is odd for a lake.
33. **/de/trails/al-asfar-and-national-park/** - "326 Millionen m²" - the area is 326 km²; the unit is awkward for a German reader. "„Kajal des Sommers und Weiß des Winters“": "Kajal" is not explained, and the colour change is not described (what colours?). "Zugvögel rasten hier auf dem Hin- und auf dem Rückflug zwischen Norden und Süden" is clear but heavy.
34. **/de/blog/48-hours-in-al-ahsa/** - "Beenden Sie Ihre Reise an der Küste des Arabischen Golfs östlich von Al-Ahsa bei Al-Uqair": clumsy stack of prepositional phrases. The sunset recommendation conflicts with /de/trails/al-uqair-coast/ ("kehren vor Sonnenuntergang zurück"), which advises being back before sunset. Whether to watch the sunset at Al-Uqair is unclear (also "Kurz vor Sonnenuntergang" in /de/attractions/al-uqair/ "Beste Tageszeit").
35. **/de/blog/hasawi-lime-history/** - "Redlichkeit verlangt, die Grenzen klar zu ziehen" - stiff, almost archaic.
36. **/de/blog/hasawi-lime-history/** - "seit mindestens 4 Jahrhunderten", "3 voneinander unabhängigen Gründen": digits instead of words ("vier Jahrhunderten", "drei"). In running German text this looks careless.
37. **/de/blog/hasawi-lime-history/** - "Die Art ist eine Hybride ... eine örtliche Entstehung ist damit biologisch unmöglich" and in the Fazit "weder eine echte Art": "Art" and "Hybride" contradict each other. "Landsorte" (instead of "Landrasse") is unusual. "(Wikipedia: Key Lime)" inside the running text breaks the register.
38. **/de/blog/hasawi-lime-history/** - "Zwischen offiziellen Quellen und einem Teil der Presseberichterstattung gehen die Baumzahlen durchaus auseinander; wir legen hier die Zahl der offiziellen Agentur zugrunde": fine, but "Saudi Press Agency" vs "offizielle Agentur" is not linked.
39. **/de/blog/is-al-ahsa-worth-visiting/** - "das erste Freitagsgebet nach dem von Medina": ambiguous, "nach dem [Freitagsgebet] von Medina". The sentence structure hides the meaning.
40. **/de/blog/is-al-ahsa-worth-visiting/** - "dreht die Familie Al-Gharash noch immer den Ton" - "den Ton drehen" is not a normal collocation (töpfern / auf der Scheibe drehen). "AlUlas" lacks the hyphen used for Al-Ahsa. "Falaj-Kanäle" is a doublet (Falaj = Kanal) and unexplained.
41. **/de/blog/is-al-ahsa-worth-visiting/** - "Zwei Tage ... Ein einzelner ... Tag ... drei Tage": the order is odd, and "Museen, Parks und wöchentliche Souks" are listed without a hint of what they are.
42. **/de/restaurants-cafes/sinyar/** - "Das Restaurant selbst hat nicht mitgeteilt, warum es sich für diesen Namen entschieden hat": strange for a restaurant page. The long etymology of "sinyar" (ships in port, convoys, caravans, a festival in Katar) is more than a visitor wants. "versteht sich selbst" and "auf den eigenen Konten" are stiff.
43. **/de/events/kafu-nights/** - "Erwartete Saison / Während des Ramadan ... Jahreszeit / Heiliger Ramadan / Zeitraum" (empty): three labels for one fact and the last is blank. "„Kafu“ geht auf ein arabisches Verb zurück, das „belohnt werden“ bedeutet: Lohn und Lob ernten" - hard to follow. "Ghabqa" is explained as a "Mahlzeit und gesellschaftliche Sitte". "arabischen Ländern am Arabischen Golf" again.
44. **/de/events/hasawi-lomi-exhibition/** - "Hasawi-Sommerbrauch": invented compound. "25–30 kg" here vs "25 bis 30 Kilogramm" in the blog: formatting differs.
45. **/de/online-stores/** - "Mashalih" (plural of mishlah, a cloak) is never explained; "königliche Mashalih" means nothing to me. "Schneidert königliche Mashalih in verschiedenen Ausführungen" is odd. "Der letzte Beitrag liegt etwa acht Monate zurück" and "Der Account ist täglich aktiv" are time-bound statements that will age quickly. "Iqal Al-Sultan" - "Iqal" is glossed only in the FAQ.
46. **/de/online-stores/** - FAQ answers read like marketing: "mit garantierter Echtheit", "einzigartige Aromentiefe und einen authentischen Geschmack", "Wirtschaftsabkommen der Region am Arabischen Golf" (which agreement?). "Diese unterliegen Standards für Qualität ..." - "Diese" is ambiguous between Plattformen and Sorten. "Hasawi-Reis ... Er braucht viel Wasser und mehr Zeit": unclear whether growing or cooking. The question "wie wird er gekocht?" is not answered. "bei hohen Temperaturen angebaut, die ihm seine unverwechselbaren Eigenschaften verleihen" is vague.
47. **/de/online-stores/** - "Auch Kunsthandwerk wie der Hasawi-Bischt und der Iqal sticht hervor": subject-verb agreement is wrong-looking (two subjects, singular verb).
48. **/de/trails/al-uqair-coast/** - "2 / Stationen im UNESCO-Welterbe / 0": numbers and labels are detached, so I could not tell which number belongs to which label (two stations, zero in the World Heritage?). The page claims "kehren vor Sonnenuntergang zurück" (see 34).
49. **/de/trails/al-uqair-coast/** - "zu einem Hafen, ... danach zu einem Strand, an dem sich Familien erholen. Am besten nehmen Sie sich für beides einen ganzen Tag": fine, but "Der König machte den Hafen zu dem Ort, an dem er Diplomaten empfing" - which king? "König Abdulaziz, dem Staatsgründer" was just named, so it is acceptable but abrupt.
50. **Home headings** - "vier Sehenswürdigkeiten, die den Geist dieses Ortes einfangen" - "dieses Ortes" is vague (which place?).
51. **Page chrome (all pages)** - "Startseite/Sehenswürdigkeiten/Al-Uqair-StrandNaturAl-Uqair-Strand", "Plattform: Salla, über das Verzeichnis „Mahally“Spezialisiert", "الأحساءVISIT AL-AHSAEine Oase": words run together with no space (probably an extraction artefact). The Urdu switch "اردو" appears as an untranslated menu item.

## Does it read like a translation?

Yes, in places. The calques "Region am Arabischen Golf" and "Länder am Arabischen Golf", the stacked transliterated names ("Al-Rifaa-Süd-Viertel", "Al-Khaleej-Straße"), the lower-case "Gouvernorat al-Ahsa", "sticht hervor", "Meeresangeln", "Landsorte" and the unusual "Arabischer Golf" all sound like Arabic structures or terms carried over word for word. The body prose of the attraction pages is otherwise fluent.

## Count and top three

**51 findings.**

Top three:
1. "Arabischer Golf" / "Region am Arabischen Golf" / "Länder am Arabischen Golf" on almost every page: unfamiliar, repeated, vague, and mildly confusing (finding 1, 2).
2. "Al-Madina – Al-Ahsa-Nationalpark" on /de/trails/al-asfar-and-national-park/ reads as Medina, and Al-Ahsa vs Nadschd as the "Tor" of Al-Uqair is contradictory (3, 6).
3. Fruit and product naming (Hasawi-Limette / Lomi / Hasawi-Lomi / Jameed / Bin Zuhairi), plus weaving vs embroidery of the Bischt, and the sunset advice that contradicts itself between the Al-Uqair pages (12, 13, 26, 34).
