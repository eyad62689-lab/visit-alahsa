# Blind reader report (Urdu, reader from Pakistan)

Total findings: 40

## Geographic names as I understood them

"خلیج عرب" is used everywhere for the sea on whose shore Al-Uqair and Salwa lie. I worked out from context (Saudi east coast, near Qatar, Dammam) that it means the body of water that I would normally call "خلیج فارس" (Persian Gulf). On first reading, though, "عرب" next to a sea made me think of "بحیرہ عرب" (the Arabian Sea), which is the sea off Pakistan's own coast. "خلیج عرب کے ممالک" I took to mean the Gulf states (GCC), but the visa page also uses "خلیج تعاون کونسل", so I wasn't sure they are the same thing. "الاحساء" is used for three things at once: a region ("الاحساء کے مشرقی حصے"), an oasis ("الاحساء کا نخلستان") and, in places, what reads like the city ("الاحساء، ہفوف کے وسط میں"). I understood Hofuf (ہفوف) as the main city inside it. The "مشرقی ساحل" is the east coast of Saudi Arabia. The "مشرقی حصہ" of Al-Ahsa is the part facing that coast. "نجد" is the central highland region, and "مملکت" is Saudi Arabia. "الخلیج سڑک" in Al-Uwaymiriyah (a Hofuf neighbourhood) I read as a road named "Gulf", but I couldn't tell whether it is a road to the Gulf or a road with that name. "مشرقی عرب" (lime article) is a different, vaguer area. The Eastern Province is never named, so I couldn't tell which administrative area all this belongs to. Qatar, Dammam, Jubail, Yanbu, Riyadh and AlUla are known names. Jubail and Yanbu appear in the mosque text with no explanation of why they matter to Al-Ahsa.

## Does it read like a translation?

Yes, in places. I name the reasons in the findings: Arabic head-first word order in names ("جھیل الاصفر", "قلعہ العقیر", "الخلیج سڑک"), the Arabic article "ال" kept on everything, rare Arabic-influenced words ("آبی اجسام", "ترشاوہ", "معاشی دروازہ", "رطب"), and the same stock sentence repeated across pages.

## Cross-page problems that bothered me

- Spaces missing between UI strings, so words run together: "العقیرتاریخی مقاماتالعقیر", "ثقافت اور دستکاریطرفہ بن العبد", "2جھیل الاصفریونیسکو", "سیاحتی معلوماتالاحساء", "ہمیں بتائیںتمام مضامین ←". Probably page extraction, but a reader sees it as an error.
- The same sentences are repeated almost word for word across many pages ("سعودی عرب کے مشرقی ساحل کی پہلی سمندری بندرگاہ", "جہاں صحرا کی ریت خلیج عرب کی نیلاہٹ سے جا ملتی ہے", "خلیج عرب کے خطے کے قدیم ترین"), which feels mechanical. "پہلی سمندری بندرگاہ" – first in time or first one reached? Unclear.
- Names keep Arabic word order and the Arabic "ال" ("قلعہ العقیر", "العقیر کی بندرگاہ", "سوق القیصریہ"); acceptable for proper names but not consistent: "العقیر کا قلعہ"-type forms and "قلعہ العقیر" both could appear.
