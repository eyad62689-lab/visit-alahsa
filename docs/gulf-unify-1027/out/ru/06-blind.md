# Blind read (RU) - travel site about Al-Ahsa, Saudi Arabia

Reader: educated Russian-speaking traveller. Only ru.txt was read.

## What I understood each geographic name to be

- **Арабский залив / регион Арабского залива / страны Арабского залива / побережье Арабского залива**: from context (east coast of Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait nearby, Dammam port, "пески встречаются с синевой") I worked out that this must be what I know as the Персидский залив. But the name is not the one I know. My first reflex was Аравийское море or Красное море, then a political rename. "Страны Арабского залива" I took as the GCC states (Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, UAE, Bahrain, Oman), and I had to guess that.
- **Аль-Ахса / Эль-Хуфуф**: Аль-Ахса is an oasis, also a "мухафаза" (administrative region) and also a name the text uses for the whole destination. Эль-Хуфуф is the main city in the oasis.
- **Эль-Укайр**: a historic port, with a fort, a mosque and a beach, on the Gulf coast east of the oasis.
- **Сальва**: a town and beach in the far south-east, near the Qatar border, about 150 km from Хуфуф. The dunes of the **Эль-Джафура** desert lie to its west.
- **Неджд**: the central region of Saudi Arabia, with the port as its "gateway to the sea". It is not explained.
- **Королевство**: Saudi Arabia.
- **Эль-Кара / Дуга-эль-Гараш**: Эль-Кара is a mountain with caves. "Дуга-эль-Гараш" is a village or quarter with a pottery family.
- **Эль-Асфар**: a lake among dunes. **Эр-Рифаа аль-Джанубия / Эр-Рифаа**: districts in Хуфуф.
- **Дорога Эль-Халидж (Sinyar)**: a street in Хуфуф. I did not realise "Халидж" means "Gulf".
- **Эль-Улы, Эр-Рияд, Эль-Джубайль и Янбу, Эль-Катиф, Даммам, Медина**: other Saudi places. Эль-Улы I parsed as a genitive of an unknown "Эль-Ул". Only after thinking did I take it as Аль-Ула.

## Did it read like a translation?

Mostly it reads as natural Russian with a few signs of adaptation: the transliterated Arabic names without Russian conventions (Аль/Эль/Эр, "Дуга-эль-Гараш", "аль-Муджам аль-Васит"), the dates in "хиджры", Latin source names left in place, and set phrases such as "исторически первый морской порт", "страны Арабского залива" and "точек" that look carried over from another language. I do not think the sentences themselves are machine-like; the problem is the proper-name layer.
