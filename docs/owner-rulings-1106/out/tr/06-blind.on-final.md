# Blind read: /tr/trails/historic-hofuf/ intro

## 1) Paraphrase of the intro's last sentence
"Cuma günleri ise El-Kaysariye Çarşısı yalnızca akşam açılır; bu yüzden çarşıyı o gün akşama bırakın."
Place: El-Kaysariye Çarşısı (the covered market in Hofuf). Day: Friday. Time: it opens only in the evening on Fridays. A Friday visitor should leave the market for the evening and not go there in the morning or daytime.

## 2) Stumbles in the intro's last two sentences
Last two sentences: "Duraklar birbirine yakındır; en iyisi sabah ya da öğleden sonranın geç saatlerinde yürüyerek gezmektir." and the Friday sentence.

- P1. "öğleden sonranın geç saatlerinde". At first I read it as "late hours of the afternoon". It is fine on a second look, though slightly heavy. Minor.
- P2. "Cuma günleri ise ... yalnızca akşam açılır". The "ise" reads as a contrast with the morning/late-afternoon advice before it. I first wondered whether this was a warning about the whole trail. It becomes clear that it refers only to the market. Minor.
- P3. "akşam" gives no clock time. I first expected an hour. The sentence says only "evening", so a visitor cannot plan precisely. This is a content gap, not a language fault.
- P4. "çarşıyı o gün akşama bırakın". Slightly colloquial ("leave it to the evening"). It is understandable. I briefly wondered whether it meant "skip the market that day", but "akşama" settles it.
- Possible tension: the morning advice for the trail is followed by "leave the market to the evening on Friday". The reader has to work out that the rest of the trail can still be done in the morning. It is not stated.

No serious stumbles.
