## 1) Paraphrase of the intro's last sentence
"On Fridays the Qaisariyah-Souk only opens in the evening; so postpone your visit there to the evening."
- Place: the Qaisariyah-Souk (covered market, last stop of the route).
- Day: Friday.
- Time: evening only (no specific hour given).
- Friday visitor: do not go in the morning or daytime; save the souk for the evening, i.e. visit the other stations first and come to the souk last.

## 2) Stumbles in the intro's last two sentences
Sentences: "Die Stationen liegen nah beieinander; am besten erkunden Sie sie zu Fuß, am Morgen oder am späten Nachmittag." and "Freitags öffnet der Qaisariyah-Souk allerdings nur abends; verschieben Sie Ihren Besuch dort also auf den Abend."

- P1: "am Morgen oder am späten Nachmittag" -- I first read this as a recommendation for the whole route, including the souk. Then the next sentence says the souk is only open in the evening on Fridays, which clashes with "late afternoon"; I had to work out that the Friday note overrides the time tip for the souk. Minor, but there is mild tension (late afternoon vs. evening).
- P2: "Freitags öffnet ... allerdings nur abends" -- "öffnet nur abends" first read as "opens only in the evening [on Fridays, closed otherwise?]"; it is clear after a moment that it means on Fridays it opens only at evening (not that it is generally evening-only). The "allerdings" (however) is fine and contrasts with the morning tip.
- P3: "verschieben Sie Ihren Besuch dort also auf den Abend" -- a bit redundant: it repeats "abends" from the sentence before, and "verschieben" (postpone) could be read as "move to another day" before "auf den Abend" clarifies. No specific opening hour is given, so "Abend" stays vague (when exactly does it open?). "dort" is slightly clumsy but understandable.
- No stumble on "Qaisariyah-Souk" itself; the spelling is consistent with the stop card at the bottom.

88/100. Natural, polite German; long first sentence is dense but fine. The Friday warning is clear and actionable; only the missing opening time and the slight redundancy keep it from higher.
