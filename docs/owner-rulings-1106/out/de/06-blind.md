# Blind read: DE historic-hofuf trail intro

## 1. Paraphrase
The intro says the route runs through Hofuf's old centre, between the Al-Koot quarter and the Qaisariyah souk. It passes an Ottoman garrison fortress, a house where Al-Ahsa's people swore allegiance to King Abdulaziz, the first state school, and a covered market that is still lively. The stops are close together, so walk, in the morning or late afternoon. Last sentence: on Fridays the Qaisariyah souk opens only in the evening. So the place is the Qaisariyah souk, the day is Friday and the time is evening only. I infer that a Friday visitor should not plan the souk for morning or late afternoon, and should visit it in the evening or do other stops first. The text never says this outright. It states the fact and leaves the advice to me.

## 2. Stumbles
- P1. "am besten erkunden Sie sie zu Fuß, am Morgen oder am späten Nachmittag; freitags öffnet..." The semicolon chain is a bit heavy. The Friday clause reads like a separate fact, not a warning. I first read it as "mornings are fine, except the souk is a bit different on Fridays". Nothing says what to do on a Friday. A "Hinweis:" or "Achtung" would make it a clear warning.
- P2. "freitags öffnet der Qaisariyah-Souk nur abends" is ambiguous. "öffnet nur abends" can mean it only opens in the evening, or it opens only after the evening starts. It would be clearer as "erst abends" or "ist nur abends geöffnet". It also does not say whether Friday-only or every day. "freitags" makes it Friday only, which I accept.
- P3. "am Morgen oder am späten Nachmittag" suggests the souk is open in the morning, which clashes with the Friday evening-only rule. It is not flagged that the morning tip fails on Friday for the souk.
- P4. "Stationen im UNESCO-Welterbe 2" is a bare number. I read it as "2 stops in the UNESCO World Heritage", which is understandable but terse.
- P5. "den Osmanen als Sitz ihrer Garnison diente" is fine but slightly stiff. "Zu einer Festung" has no name, so I cannot look it up.
- P6. "Treueid" is fine, a bit formal. "Haus" is not named.
- P7. "Qaisariyah-Souk" and "Al-Koot-Viertel" are transliterations that are unfamiliar to Germans, but consistent.
- P8. "in dem bis heute das Leben pulsiert" is a travel cliche but natural.
- P9. Stop card: "erbaut 1238 n. H. (1822 n. Chr.)" is clear. "fiel er einem Brand zum Opfer" is natural. "vierzehn Tore" next to "15.000 m²" is fine. "Bestandteilen der Welterbestätte" is a bit bureaucratic.
- P10. No opening hours are given, so "abends" is vague: from what time?

## 3. Score
Readability of the intro: 78/100. The grammar is correct and natural. The Friday point is weakly signalled and reads like a footnote, and the bare "nur abends" gives no hour.
