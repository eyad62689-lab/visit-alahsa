# Blind read (ru) — historic-hofuf trail intro

## 1. Paraphrase
The intro says the route goes through the old centre of Hofuf, between the Al-Kut district and the Qaisariyah market. It lists four sights: a fortress where the Ottomans had their garrison headquarters; the house where Al-Ahsa residents swore allegiance to King Abdulaziz; the first state school in Al-Ahsa; and a covered market that is still lively today. The stops are close together, so it is best to walk, either in the morning or toward evening. Last sentence: on Fridays the Qaisariyah market is open only in the evening. So the place is the Qaisariyah market, the day is Friday, the time is evening only. A Friday visitor should not go in the morning; plan the market for the evening (e.g. do the other stops first, or visit the market last). The sentence states the fact but does not literally say "come in the evening" — I infer it.

## 2. Stumbles
- P1. "по пятницам рынок Эль-Кайсария открыт только вечером" is joined to the walking advice after a semicolon, so at first I read it as a continuation of "morning or toward evening" (i.e. an exception to the morning option) and was not sure it was a warning. It works, but it is tucked in as an afterthought. A separate sentence ("В пятницу ...") would be clearer.
- P2. "ближе к вечеру" (toward evening) is vague against "вечером" (in the evening) for the market; I was unsure whether the market opens late afternoon or after dark. No hours given.
- P3. "в которой османы разместили штаб своего гарнизона" is a bit bureaucratic/translated-sounding; "где располагался штаб османского гарнизона" would be more natural. Understandable.
- P4. "жители Аль-Ахсы принесли присягу" — natural enough, but "Аль-Ахса" vs "Эль-Хуфуф", "Эль-Кут", "Эль-Кайсария" mix the Аль-/Эль- article styles; slightly inconsistent. I did not misread it.
- P5. "первая в Аль-Ахсе государственная школа" — word order is slightly stiff but clear.
- P6. "крытый рынок, где и сегодня кипит жизнь" — fine, mildly marketing-like.
- P7. Last stop text: "Это один из компонентов объекта «Оазис Аль-Ахса», включённых в Список..." — "включённых" agrees with "компонентов" so reads as if components are listed, ok; "и лучшее завершение маршрута" is a slightly odd closing but understandable. (Outside the intro.)
- P8. "Остановки из списка ЮНЕСКО 2" in the header is terse; fine.
