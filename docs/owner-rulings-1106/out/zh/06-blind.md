# Blind read: /zh/trails/historic-hofuf/ intro

## 1. Paraphrase
The intro says the route runs through the centre of old Hofuf, between the Qut district and the Qaysariyah market. It passes four stops: a fort that once headquartered the Ottoman garrison, a residence where Ahsa people pledged allegiance to King Abdulaziz, the first government school in Ahsa, and a roofed market that is still lively. The stops are close together, so it suggests walking in the morning or in the evening. Last sentence: "周五凯撒利亚集市仅傍晚营业" means the Qaysariyah market, on Friday only, opens in the evening only. A Friday visitor should therefore not plan the market for the morning. They should reach it in the evening, or move the walk to the evening. (The text does not say what exact time "evening" is, or whether the other stops are open on Friday.)

## 2. Stumbles
- P1. "宜在上午或傍晚步行游览；周五凯撒利亚集市仅傍晚营业". I first read this as a general note that the market is evening-only on Fridays. Only on a second look did I see that it conflicts with "上午" for that stop. It never says outright what to do ("so on Friday walk in the evening"). The link between the two clauses is implicit, and the semicolon makes it look like a separate fact.
- P2. "仅傍晚营业" is slightly ambiguous. Does it mean "only opens in the evening", or "evening only, closed the rest of the day"? I take it as the first. "傍晚" is vague (around 5pm? after 6?). No hours are given.
- P3. "库特区". I did not recognise this as a place name, and I misread 库特 first as a generic "district". It is a transliteration with no explanation.
- P4. "哈萨第一所官办学校", "哈萨民众", "哈萨绿洲". 哈萨 is slightly unfamiliar. Mainland-style usage would be 哈萨 or 艾哈萨, and I needed context to see it is the oasis region. It reads fine once understood.
- P5. "一处见证哈萨民众向阿卜杜勒阿齐兹国王宣誓效忠的宅邸". The noun phrase is long. It reads a little like translationese, although it is parseable.
- P6. "站点 4 / 世界遗产站点 2". A bare number on its own line. I first read it as "4 stops, 2 World Heritage stops", which was correct, but the labels are dry.
- P7. Last stop text: "也是这条路线再好不过的收尾". "再好不过" is natural colloquially but a bit promotional for a guide.
- P8. "伊历 1238 年（公元 1822 年）". Fine. "2013 年底全面开放" is fine. "1.5 万多平方米" is natural.
- P9. "胡富夫" is a rarer rendering than 胡夫夫 or 哈萨's usual 胡富夫. It was understandable but I wondered whether it matches other maps.

## 3. Score
Readability of the intro: 78/100. The facts are clear and the Chinese is mostly fluent. The deduction is mainly for the Friday sentence (P1, P2), which gives a constraint but not an explicit instruction, plus the unexplained place names.
