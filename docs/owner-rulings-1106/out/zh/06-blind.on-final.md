## 1) Paraphrase of the intro's last sentence
"However, on Friday the Qaysariyah (Kaisariya) Market is open only in the evening, so on that day save the market for the evening and visit it then."
- Place: the Qaysariyah Market, the 4th and last stop.
- Day: Friday.
- Time: evening only. No clock time is given.
- A Friday visitor should do the other stops first and leave the market until the evening. They should not go in the morning.

## 2) Stumbles in the last two sentences
Sentence 1: 各站相距不远，宜在上午或傍晚步行游览。
Sentence 2: 不过，周五凯撒利亚集市仅傍晚营业，这一天请把集市留到傍晚再逛。

- P1. "宜在上午或傍晚步行游览". I first read this as general advice that either morning or evening suits the whole route. It is clear, and only "宜" is slightly formal. It sets up the Friday exception well. It is a very mild stumble, almost none.
- P2. "不过，周五…仅傍晚营业，这一天请把集市留到傍晚再逛". I first read "不过" as a contrast with "上午或傍晚都行": mornings are fine except Friday for the market. That reading is right. The only friction is that "仅傍晚营业" and "留到傍晚再逛" say nearly the same thing twice. There is also no opening time, so I would not know exactly when in the evening. This is a small gap in information, not a comprehension problem.

No real misreadings. Both stumbles are minor.

## 3) Readability of the intro
92/100. It is natural, fluent Chinese. The Friday warning is easy to find and act on. The only deductions are the slight redundancy and the missing evening hour.
