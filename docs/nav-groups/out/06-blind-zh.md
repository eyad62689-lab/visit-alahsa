# Blind reader notes (mainland China reader), nav labels

## Section headings (phone)
- **"探索绿洲"**: Reads naturally and is inviting. It tells me roughly "sights and things to do", but it is poetic rather than informative. It also sounds like a call to action, a bit like the gold button. Acceptable; "探索绿洲" works because the items under it (景点/路线/活动) make it clear. No change needed.
- **"美食与购物"**: Natural, standard website wording. Clear that it covers eating and buying. Fine.
- **"出行准备"**: PROBLEM. It means "preparing for the trip" (visa, packing, flights, how to get there). Under it I find only "住宿" and "我的收藏". Accommodation is plausible, but "我的收藏" (my saved items) is not trip preparation at all, so the heading does not tell me what is under it. It is also semantically very close to the gold button "行程规划" (trip planning), so I would expect the heading to be about planning and be confused about the difference. Suggest: rename to "住宿与收藏" (says exactly what is inside), or move "我的收藏" out and keep a heading like "行前准备" (the more idiomatic collocation, "行前准备", not "出行准备") only if real preparation content (visa, transport) lives there.

## Laptop menu buttons
- **"探索绿洲 ▾"**: Same as above; fine as a dropdown label.
- **"美食与购物 ▾"**: Fine.
- **"住宿"**: Natural, universally understood. Fine.
- **"地图"**: Fine. Inconsistency: on laptop it is a top-level link, on phone it sits under "探索绿洲". Not a wording issue, but a reader switching devices looks for it in different places.
- **"我的收藏"** (icon link): Natural ("收藏" is the normal Chinese word for saved/favorites). Fine. Weak as a standalone text if the icon is only a heart; fine with the label.
- **"行程规划"** (gold button): Natural and clearly a primary action. Slight confusion risk with "路线" (routes) and the heading "出行准备"; see those entries.

## Items under the menus
- **"景点"**: Standard, fine.
- **"路线"**: Ambiguous. A Chinese reader may read it as transport/driving directions rather than ready-made tour itineraries, and it sits next to the gold "行程规划". Suggest "推荐路线" (or "游览路线") to signal curated itineraries and separate it from planning.
- **"活动"**: Fine, though a bit generic (events/festivals vs activities). "节庆活动" would be sharper only if it is events; acceptable as is.
- **"传统集市、公园与农场"**: Awkward as a menu item. It is a long enumeration (12 characters, uses the "、" and "与" together), which wraps on a phone and reads like a sentence fragment, not a menu label. Real menus use a short category noun. Suggest "集市·公园·农场" (compact), or a category label such as "休闲去处" / "休闲与自然" if the three are one family.
- **"哈萨美食"**: PROBLEM. "哈萨" alone is easily read as a clipped "哈萨克" (Kazakh) and "哈萨美食" can look like Kazakh cuisine. The usual mainland rendering of Al-Ahsa is "艾哈萨" (or "胡富夫" for the city of Hofuf). Inside this menu the context helps, but a first-time reader may hesitate. Suggest "艾哈萨美食", or simply "本地美食" under the menu (the site name already supplies the place). The same naming issue applies to the page title "哈萨旅游指南": "艾哈萨旅游指南" is safer.
- **"餐厅与咖啡馆"**: Natural, clear. Fine.
- **"绿洲水果"**: Slightly unclear. Under "美食与购物" I cannot tell whether it is an information page about local fruit or a place to buy it. "绿洲" as a modifier is poetic but vague. Suggest "绿洲时令水果" if it is a seasonal guide, or "特产水果" if it is about local produce.
- **"网店"**: Ambiguous. It means "online shop", but whose, selling what? A reader may expect the site's own store, or an external marketplace, and it does not match the style of the other items. Suggest "特产网店" or "网上购买" (clear that it is where to buy local products online).

## Bottom row (phone)
- **"语言: 中文（简体） 切换 ⌄"**: The word "语言" is natural and the globe icon helps. Issues: (1) "切换" is a bare verb with no object right after the language name; it reads like "switch" and is redundant with the ⌄ arrow. Drop it, or use "切换语言" if a verb is wanted. (2) Punctuation is mixed: ASCII colon "语言:" next to full-width parentheses "（简体）"; use "语言：" in Chinese typography. (3) "中文（简体）" is understandable, but "简体中文" is the more common label on Chinese sites. Suggest: "语言：简体中文 ⌄".
- Not confusable with the gold button; the bottom row is clearly separate and quiet.

## Gold button vs headings
- **"行程规划"** vs **"出行准备"**: confusable in meaning (planning vs preparing); both sound like "plan your trip". This is the main confusion risk; fixing the heading (see above) resolves it.
- **"行程规划"** vs **"路线"**: both suggest itineraries; "推荐路线" reduces the overlap.
- "探索绿洲" as a heading is imperative-sounding but is visually and semantically distinct from an action button; low risk.

## Minor
- Main heading "全球最大的绿洲，在您眼前一章章展开": understandable, slightly translated in feel ("一章章展开" is literary), but acceptable for a brand headline. The mix of "您" in the heading is polite and fine.
