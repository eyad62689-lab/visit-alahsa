Seasons of the oasis
What season is Al-Ahsa in now?
Each season in the oasis brings its own bounty and events — here is what welcomes you in each.
You are here now
Winter
December – February
The gentlest weather of the year — prime time for the landmarks and forts
Kanar and citron fresh from the oasis groves
Festival high season: the Hasawi Bisht, the Processed Dates Festival, Palm Village, Al-Uqair Winter
You are here now
Spring
March – May
Oasis mulberries — a short season around April
Creative Al-Ahsa Festival and the close of Qaisariyah Nights
Late May: the first early rutab dates
You are here now
Summer
June – August
Rutab and early-harvest season
Hasawi figs, bambar and papaya at their peak
The Hasawi Lomi Exhibition closes the summer
You are here now
Autumn
September – November
The “siram” harvest: full dates until early October
The weather softens — the ideal visiting months begin
The horse racing season and Al-Wafrah Winter get under way
Seasons are approximate and event dates change from year to year — confirm via official channels before visiting.
