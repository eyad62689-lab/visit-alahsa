# Reader observations (Urdu, native reader)

## What the pottery place is
دوغہ الغراش is an old family pottery workshop in القارہ village at the foot of جبل القارہ, east of Al-Ahsa. The الغراش family has run it for generations. Potters still shape pots by hand on the wheel and fire them in kilns on the spot. Newspapers say pottery has been made there for more than 600 years, and its potters have shown their work abroad (France, USA, Canada). Visitors can watch every step, from kneading the clay to the wheel and the kiln, and take pots home. Entry is free. It is open daily 8 am to 12 noon and 3 pm to 7:30 pm, and the page says to confirm with the management before going.

## Beach stop: who looked after the beach
It says the Al-Ahsa municipality (الاحساء کی بلدیہ) developed/beautified the seaside area for outings and family activities, and also built the modern open-air theatre on the beach for events. Locals call it "the Roman theatre", but the text says plainly that it is a new building and not a Roman ruin.

## Mosque stop and its religious references
The mosque is near الکلابیہ village, north-east of Hofuf. The Banu Abd al-Qais tribe built it in year 7 AH after accepting Islam. Citing Sahih Bukhari via Ibn Abbas, it says this was the first mosque after the Prophet's mosque in Madinah where Jumu'ah was prayed, so it is called the second mosque in Islam where Jumu'ah was held. It is part of the UNESCO-listed Al-Ahsa Oasis. The religious references read respectfully and correctly to me. «حضرت ابن عباسؓ» is exactly how we write it, «مدینہ منورہ» is the respectful usual form, and «صحیح بخاری میں ... کی روایت ہے» is the normal way to cite a hadith in Urdu. Nothing in it would offend a practising reader.

## Issues in the Urdu
1. minor — Category label «روایتی بازار» (in the breadcrumb and the listing card: «روایتی بازاردوغہ الغراش») is used for a pottery workshop. A reader expects a market and finds a کارگاہ. Something like «دستکاری / روایتی کارگاہ» would fit better.
2. minor — The breadcrumb says «بازار اور پارک», but the menu and footer say «بازار، پارک اور زرعی فارم». The section name is inconsistent.
3. minor — «یہاں کے بنے برتن ساتھ لے جاتے ہیں» reads as if visitors just take the pots. «خرید کر ساتھ لے جاتے ہیں» would be clearer, unless the pots really are given away.
4. minor — «مسجد نبوی صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم کے بعد»: the full blessing placed between «مسجد نبوی» and «کے بعد» makes the phrase heavy and slightly breaks the flow. It is not wrong (see directed question b).
5. minor — «اسی لیے یہ اسلام کی وہ دوسری مسجد کہلاتی ہے جہاں جمعہ کی نماز ادا ہوئی» is a little awkward. «اسی لیے اسے اسلام کی دوسری مسجد کہا جاتا ہے جہاں (سب سے پہلے) جمعہ ادا ہوا» reads more smoothly. The meaning is clear.
6. minor — «یونیسکو کی عالمی ورثے کی فہرست» has two «کی» back to back. «یونیسکو کی عالمی ورثہ فہرست» or «یونیسکو کے عالمی ثقافتی ورثے کی فہرست» is smoother.
7. minor — «کارگاہ» is correct but somewhat bookish/Persianate for everyday Pakistani readers, who might say «ورکشاپ» or «کارخانہ». The gloss «(یعنی الغراش کی کارگاہ)» helps, so this is acceptable.
8. minor — «اسے الاحساء کی بلدیہ نے سنوارا ہے»: «سنوارا» is understandable but slightly informal for this kind of statement. «ترقی دی ہے / تیار کیا ہے» is more usual. «بلدیہ» is fine.

No serious issues: no gender errors, no wrong honorifics, nothing misleading in the facts as I read them.

## Directed questions
(a) Yes. «کارگاہ» is feminine and every word agreeing with it is feminine: «ایک روایتی کارگاہ، جو ... چلی آ رہی ہے», «الغراش کی کارگاہ», «یہ ... ایک روایتی کارگاہ ہے», «یہ کارگاہ ایک سیاحتی مقام ہے» (the predicate مقام governs ہے, which is fine). I found no agreement error.

(b) «حضرت ابن عباسؓ» is correct, respectful and fully natural. Strictly, «رضی اللہ عنہما» could be used since his father was also a Companion, but «ؓ» is standard in ordinary prose. «مسجد نبوی صلی اللہ علیہ وسلم» is correct and respectful, and Urdu writers do sometimes attach the blessing to «مسجد نبوی». Written out in full in the middle of «مسجد نبوی ... کے بعد», though, it is a bit heavy. Most natural prose would write either «مسجدِ نبوی کے بعد» (the word نبوی already honours him and many writers add no blessing there) or the compact «مسجد نبوی ﷺ». It is acceptable, not an error: minor stylistic only.
