# Reader observations (Urdu, native reader, Pakistan)

## What the pottery place is
Duqat al-Gharash is an old family pottery workshop in al-Qarah village, at the foot of Jabal al-Qarah, in the eastern part of al-Ahsa. The al-Gharash family has passed the craft down from one generation to the next. According to newspapers, pottery has been made at this spot for more than 600 years. Its potters have shown their work at exhibitions in France, the USA and Canada. As a visitor I would see every stage of the work: kneading the clay, the potter's wheel, and firing in the workshop's own kilns. I could also buy pieces made there and take them home. It is open every day, 8 am to 12 noon and 3 pm to 7:30 pm. Entry is free, and the page tells me to confirm with the place before I go.

## Beach stop: who looked after the beach
It says the beach at al-Uqair is meant for outings and family activities, and that the Al-Ahsa municipality (بلدیہ) did the work on it. The municipality also built a modern open-air amphitheatre there for events. Local people call it "the Roman theatre", but the text says plainly that it is a new building, not a Roman ruin.

## Mosque stop
The mosque is northeast of Hofuf, near al-Kalabiyyah village. The Banu Abd al-Qays tribe built it in the 7th year of the Hijra, after they accepted Islam. Sahih Bukhari has a narration from Ibn Abbas that the first Jumu'ah prayed anywhere after the Prophet's mosque in Madinah was at the Abd al-Qays mosque in Jawatha. That is why it is called the second mosque in Islam where Jumu'ah was prayed. The site is part of the UNESCO-listed Al-Ahsa Oasis. The religious references read respectfully and the way a Pakistani Muslim expects: مدینہ منورہ, حضرت ابن عباسؓ, مسجد نبوی ﷺ, صحیح بخاری. The claim matches the well-known Bukhari hadith about Jawatha. Nothing in it reads as off or irreverent.

## Issues
1. minor — The category label «روایتی بازار» (on the page breadcrumb and on the listing card) is used for a pottery workshop. It is not a bazaar, so the label is slightly misleading next to «کارگاہ».
2. minor — The section name changes from place to place: «بازار، پارک اور زرعی فارم» in the menu, «بازار اور پارک» in the breadcrumb, «تمام بازار اور پارک ←» at the bottom. Also «زرعی فارم» is redundant, since فارم already means a farm.
3. minor — «اسے الاحساء کی بلدیہ نے سنوارا ہے»: «سنوارنا» is understood, but it sounds soft and personal (grooming, adorning). For civic work, «ترقی دی ہے» / «تیار کیا ہے» / «کی تزئین و آرائش کی ہے» would be more natural.
4. minor — «سمندر کے کنارے یہ جگہ» right after the heading «العقیر کا ساحل سمندر» repeats the sea idea. «یہ ساحل سیر اور خاندانی سرگرمیوں کے لیے ہے» would read better.
5. minor — «کھلی تماشا گاہ» is correct (feminine, and «بنائی ہے» agrees with it), but «تماشا» can suggest a show or circus. «کھلا تھیٹر» / «ایمفی تھیٹر» is the more common word in Pakistan.
6. minor — «یونیسکو کی عالمی ورثے کی فہرست» has two «کی» stacked and reads a little clumsily. «یونیسکو کی عالمی ثقافتی ورثہ فہرست» or «یونیسکو کے عالمی ورثے کی فہرست» is smoother.
7. minor — «اس کے اردگرد کا آثار قدیمہ کا مقام» has two «کا» in a row and feels heavy. «اس کے اردگرد کے آثار قدیمہ» is simpler.
8. minor — The text writes «ہفوف» without «ال», while other names keep it («العقیر», «القارہ», «الکلابیہ»). The spelling is inconsistent («الہفوف»).
9. minor — «یہاں کے بنے برتن» is fine in speech, but «یہاں کے بنے ہوئے برتن» is the standard written form.
10. minor — In the extract, the mosque stop has no visible stop name or number. It starts directly with «یہ مسجد», so a reader of only this stop doesn't learn the mosque's name until the second sentence («جواثا میں عبد القیس کی مسجد»). This may be an artifact of how the text was cut.

Serious: 0. Minor: 10.

## Directed questions
(a) Yes. «کارگاہ» reads as a craftsman's workshop (Persian/literary register, like کارگاہِ ہنر), not a factory; «کارخانہ» would have meant factory. As with other ‑گاہ words it is feminine, and every agreement is correct: «ایک روایتی کارگاہ، جو ... چلی آ رہی ہے», «الغراش کی کارگاہ», «یہ کارگاہ ایک سیاحتی مقام ہے» (the predicate مقام is masculine, which is fine).
(b) It is understandable and grammatical, but «سنوارا» is a bit soft and poetic for a municipality improving a beach. «اسے الاحساء کی بلدیہ نے ترقی دی ہے / تیار کیا ہے» is more natural. Minor issue only.
(c) Yes, both are correct and natural. «حضرت ابن عباسؓ» is the standard form (a purist might write «رضی اللہ عنہما» in full, but ؓ is fully acceptable). «مسجد نبوی ﷺ» is common and respectful in Pakistani writing.
