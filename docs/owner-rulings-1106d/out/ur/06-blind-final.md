## Answers from the text alone

(a) Hand embroidery, "حساوی سوزن کاری" (Hasawi needlework). The robe is hand-embroidered with zari thread plated with gold or silver. The fineness of the handwork gives it its distinction. The craft is passed down through families.

(b) To escape the summer heat. The caves stay pleasantly cool in peak summer, with mild warmth in deep winter, so locals sheltered there from antiquity.

(c) No. The robe is never described as woven. Every passage says embroidered ("کڑھا ہوا", "کڑھائی", "سوزن کاری", "ہاتھ سے کڑھا"). The only other verb is "چوغہ سازی" in [8], which means making.

(d) No. [3], [4], [5], [6], [7] and [9] name the same craft consistently, as hand embroidery with zari thread. The only variation is wording: "سوزن کاری", "کڑھائی" and "کڑھا ہوا" in the other passages, and generic "چوغہ سازی" in [8]. There is no real conflict.
