## Answers

(a) The distinction comes from hand embroidery ("рукоделие Аль-Ахсы") with "zari" threads covered in gold or silver; done by hand, precisely, a family craft passed down generations. What "зари" means is not explained.

(b) Locals went into the caves since ancient times to seek shelter from the summer heat (they are cool in summer, warm in winter).

(c) No. The bisht is never described as woven; only as embroidered by hand, and a cloak (накидка). The fabric is mentioned only as "ткань" in [8].
