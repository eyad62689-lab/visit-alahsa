## Answers

(a) Embroidery ("вышивка Аль-Ахсы"), done by hand, with "zari" thread covered in gold or silver. It is a family craft passed down through generations.

(b) To find shelter from the summer heat, because the caves stay cool in summer (and warm in winter).

(c) No. The bisht is never described as woven; it is "embroidered/sewn" (расшитая, вышивка, шитьё). Only passage [7] says "изготовлению бишта" (making), neutral.

(d) Not really different names for the craft, but two words alternate: "вышивка" (embroidery) and "шитьё" (sewing), e.g. [2] and [3]/[4] "«вышивка Аль-Ахсы» — ручное шитьё / точное ручное шитьё". The wording differs mildly ("шитьё" vs "вышивка", plus "расшивают"/"расшитая"), but they all describe the same thing: hand embroidery with zari thread. Passage [7] "изготовление" is generic.
