## Answers from the text alone

(a) The distinction comes from embroidery (哈萨刺绣): hand-stitched, fine hand stitching, using zari thread (扎里线) plated with gold and silver; the skill is passed down through Al-Ahsa families.
(b) To escape the summer heat: the caves are cool in midsummer (and warm in deep winter), so locals have come there to shelter from summer heat since ancient times.
(c) No. The bisht is never described as woven. It is described only as hand-embroidered. The only weaving mentioned is palm-leaf weaving (棕榈叶编织) in passage 3, which is a separate craft.
