## Answers
(a) The bisht is distinguished by the Hasawi needlework/embroidery ("Hasawi-Nadelarbeit"): fine hand embroidery with zari threads coated in gold or silver, handed down in Al-Ahsa families.
(b) The text says the caves give refuge from summer heat for locals ("Zuflucht vor der Sommerhitze"): cool in summer, warm in winter. Only summer heat is given as the reason.
(c) No. The bisht is described only as embroidered ("bestickt", "handgestickt"), never as woven. (The word "Stoff" appears but no weaving.)

## Overall flags
- "Hasawi-Nadelarbeit" (quoted term, unclear).
- "die die Arbeiten der Familien führen, die ihn fertigen" (convoluted).
- "Direkt aus den Werkstätten der Handwerker" (clashes with buying in souk shops).
- "Umhang des Ansehens" / "ein Zeichen von Ansehen" (stiff).
- "der Bischt als solcher steht seit 2025 auf der UNESCO-Liste" (ambiguous).
- "Bischt-Workshops" (bare heading, unexplained term).
- Passages 3, 4, 7, 10 repeat the same embroidery sentence nearly verbatim.
