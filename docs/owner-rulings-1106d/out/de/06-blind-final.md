## Answers

(a) Hand embroidery with zari threads (threads coated in gold or silver). Families in Al-Ahsa pass the skill down through generations.

(b) The caves are cool in summer, so locals used them as a refuge from the summer heat (and they are warm in winter).

(c) No. It is never described as woven. It is described as embroidered ("bestickt", "Handstickerei", "handgestickt"); "Stoff" appears only once, as the base fabric.

(d) Not truly contradictory, but the wording varies: passages 4 and 5 call it "Hasawi-Nadelarbeit" (needlework, in quotation marks), while 3, 4, 5, 8 and 11 say "Handstickerei", "handgestickt" and "bestickt", and passage 10 says only "Bischt-Herstellung" (making). A careful reader may wonder whether "Nadelarbeit" means something other than embroidery, e.g. sewing or tailoring, although the sentence context ("Feinheit ihrer Handstickerei") makes clear it is the embroidery.
