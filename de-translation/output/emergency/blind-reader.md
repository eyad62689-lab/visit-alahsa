# Blind reader report: /de/emergency/ (stage 6)

Reader: German-speaking visitor planning a trip. Line numbers refer to blind-input.txt. Severity: H = could cause wrong action in an emergency, M = unclear or misparsed, L = style or register.

## High

1. (H) Line 153: "Die Nationale Plattform führt die 112 als Notrufnummer für Anrufe ohne SIM-Karte; das heißt aber nicht, dass Sie ohne verfügbares Netz anrufen können."
   112 is the number I know from home, and it appears only here, in the last FAQ. It is not in the main number table (lines 13-33) or the 34 note. I cannot tell whether I should try 112 with my German SIM, only without a SIM, or never. "führt ... als Notrufnummer" first read as "leads". The sentence also contradicts itself: 112 is for calls without a SIM, yet it "does not mean you can call without a network". If 112 matters, it belongs in the table with one clear usage line. Also, "Nationale Plattform" is never explained anywhere on the page (lines 35, 73, 113, 127, 153).

2. (H) Lines 8, 55, 72, 149: 911 versus 997 order is inconsistent.
   Line 8: "Rufen Sie 911 an. Einen Rettungswagen fordern Sie direkt unter 997 an." Line 55: "Rufen Sie unter 997 einen Rettungswagen". Line 72: "Rufen Sie 911 an und fordern Sie bei Bedarf unter 997 einen Rettungswagen an." When someone is injured after a car crash, do I call both, one after the other, or only 997? "bei Bedarf" makes it sound as if the second call is optional. Please state plainly: in a medical emergency, call 997 directly; if unsure, call 911. The FAQ sentence on line 149 ("und die 997, um direkt einen Rettungswagen zu rufen") is elliptical and reads oddly.

3. (H) Line 97: "Wenden Sie sich unter 999 oder über die 911 an die Polizei, um zum Meldeverfahren weitergeleitet zu werden".
   "unter 999 oder über die 911" is ambiguous. Line 8 says 911 is for immediate danger, and a lost passport is not that, so I would hesitate to call it. Which one for a lost passport? The page does not say. "Meldeverfahren" and "Nummer der Anzeige" are bureaucratic. I read "Anzeige" first as an advertisement, then as a police report. Suggest "Anzeigenummer/Aktenzeichen der Polizei" or similar.

4. (H) Line 73, the Najm paragraph.
   - "Najm" is never explained. Is it an insurer, a police service, or an app? Only "Najm-App" in the sources line hints at it.
   - "...um zu klären, wie Sie den Unfall im Rahmen der Zuständigkeit und der Servicebedingungen von Najm melden" is stiff and long, and I lose the thread.
   - "Die Nationale Plattform führt für Unfälle mit versicherten Fahrzeugen die Nummer 920000560." Do I call this number? It is unclear what the action is. It also looks like a second number for Najm, since 199033 is the first, and the page does not explain the difference.
   - "folgen Sie dessen Hinweis": "dessen" is ambiguous (Najm? the Unfall?). It is also unclear what happens if Najm declines.
   - "Ausweis" is ambiguous for a tourist. Passport?

5. (H) Line 56: "Fahren Sie nicht selbst, wenn Ihr Zustand Ihre Fahrtüchtigkeit beeinträchtigen könnte."
   Whose "Zustand"? Mine, or the patient's? It sits in a paragraph about helping an injured person, so I first read it as being about the patient. It also does not say what to do instead (wait for the ambulance?). Rewrite to name the subject.

6. (H) Line 90: "Schlangenbiss oder Skorpionstich: Rufen Sie sofort einen Rettungswagen."
   This is the only emergency here without a number, though 997 is given in every other case. Under stress I may not look it up. Repeat 997.

7. (H) Lines 129-144, the phrase block.
   Arabic, English and German run together with no explanation: "I need an ambulance.Ich brauche einen Rettungswagen." I do not know why English is there, nor that I am supposed to show or read out the Arabic line. There is no pronunciation help, so I cannot say the Arabic aloud. The page does not say whether the dispatcher answers in English or Arabic (line 145 only speaks of "mehreren Sprachen"). Missing: a sentence telling me how to use the block, e.g. show the phone screen to a helper. I am not suggesting content, only flagging the gap. Also, the English line "I do not speak Arabic. I need help in English." is shown with the German translation, but German speakers will need to say it in English, which is confusing.

## Medium

8. (M) Lines 64-67, the hospital list: "König-Fahad-Krankenhaus, Hofuf Gesundheitsministerium (öffnet in einem neuen Fenster)".
   The operator runs straight into the place name: "Hofuf Gesundheitsministerium" parses as one name. Add a separator or dash. The entries have no addresses or phone numbers, which is left hanging after line 60 tells me to go to the right hospital. Line 68 does say "nur zur Orientierung", which helps.

9. (M) Line 55: "einem herabhängenden Gesicht oder verwaschener Sprache".
   "herabhängendes Gesicht" is a word-for-word rendering. A German writer says "hängender Mundwinkel" or "einseitig herabhängendem Mundwinkel". Also "Rufen Sie unter 997 einen Rettungswagen bei Bewusstlosigkeit" is a long single sentence with the trigger list trailing at the end.

10. (M) Line 11: "der Berater Bulbul".
   Nobody introduced "Bulbul". It reads as a person's name. First read: a human adviser called Bulbul. It is probably a chatbot. Say what it is, or I wonder what channel I am being told not to use. "Die E-Mail-Adresse der Website, ihr WhatsApp-Kontakt" is also fine, but the "Website" and "Reiseführer" mix: "Visit Al-Ahsa ist ein unabhängiger Reiseführer" first read as a printed guidebook.

11. (M) Lines 14-24, the table descriptions.
   - 911 "Einheitliche Sicherheitsleitstelle der Ostprovinz": heavy compound, correct but bureaucratic.
   - 999 "Sicherheitsmeldung, Übergriff oder Diebstahl": "Sicherheitsmeldung" is not a German thing; I would not know what to call about. "Übergriff" (assault) is fine.
   - 996 "Vorfall auf Fernstraßen außerhalb der Städte": which highway does an Al-Ahsa tourist use? Fine.
   - 998 "Brand, eingeschlossene Personen oder Rettungseinsatz": "Rettungseinsatz" overlaps with the ambulance; I cannot tell the difference from 997.
   Also 911/998/997 seem to overlap for fire: line 93 says "911 oder 998" without telling me which to prefer.

12. (M) Line 34: "Die 911 ist in der Ostprovinz ... in Betrieb."
   "in Betrieb" is odd for a hotline ("gilt", "ist erreichbar"). The same sentence says the short numbers "gelten innerhalb Saudi-Arabiens; wählen Sie sie ohne die Ländervorwahl +966": fine. But it does not say whether they work with a foreign SIM or roaming. That is left hanging.

13. (M) Line 52: "Eine Rückrufnummer: Bleiben Sie dann in der Leitung".
   "dann" is unclear (after giving the number?). The list items are a mix of noun phrases and imperatives. The first item "Zuerst der Standort: Al-Ahsa, dann Stadt oder Dorf, Viertel, ..." is dense; "Orientierungspunkt" parses as "Orientierungs-Punkt" and is awkward ("Wahrzeichen" or "markanter Punkt").

14. (M) Line 53: "in einem Felsdurchgang".
   Unfamiliar word; I had to guess (a gorge? cave passage?). "teilen Sie Ihren Standort über den Kanal, den Ihnen die Leitstelle nennt": "Kanal" reads as a waterway, since later lines mention "Bewässerungskanälen". Better "Weg/Kommunikationsweg" or similar. "Warten Sie mit dem Anruf nicht, bis die Karte geladen ist": good.

15. (M) Line 82: "Senken und Flutrinnen".
   "Flutrinnen" is not an everyday word. A reader thinks of a drainage channel. The Arabic wadi concept is not conveyed (a dry riverbed that floods). "ungeschützte Orte" is vague (exposed locations?). Line 86: "Bleiben Sie auf offenen Wegen": open as in unlocked, or as in open terrain? Ambiguous. "folgen Sie den Anweisungen der Betreiber": operators of what?

16. (M) Lines 77-79, heading "Hitzeerschöpfung und Hitzschlag".
   The text covers prevention and heat stroke but says nothing specific about Hitzeerschöpfung, so the heading promises more than delivered. Line 79: "beginnen Sie, sie mit Wasser und kalten Kompressen zu kühlen": "sie ... sie" ambiguous (Person / Körpertemperatur). Then "Geben Sie ... nichts zu trinken" sits oddly against line 78's "trinken Sie regelmäßig Wasser" without the contrast being marked ("aber").

17. (M) Line 98: "den Dienst zur Gerätesperre Ihrer Telefonplattform".
   Translationese. "Telefonplattform" is not German usage; I guessed Apple/Google. Say "Geräteortung/Sperrfunktion von Apple oder Google" in whatever wording is allowed without adding facts. Also "Bestätigungscodes" fine.

18. (M) Line 93: "rufen Sie dann von einem sicheren Ort aus 911 oder 998 an".
   Which one? Line 8 says 911 for immediate danger and 998 is listed for fire. A visitor under stress wants one choice. Same with line 86: "wählen Sie 994 oder 911".

   "Laut den Notrufnummern der Nationalen Plattform" is a fragment, not a description; I do not know what this number is for. Compare "Najm (Kundenservice)" directly above.
