I read these as a Pakistani Urdu reader planning a trip. I wrote the report in English, with the Urdu quotes verbatim. Severity labels are جسیم / متوسط / معمولی. The text has no final section of post-action labels, so there was nothing to read there.

### A. Problems that run across all pages

1. "کھانے کی جگہ" is used on every page for "restaurant", for example "کھانے کی جگہ بھی ہے اور قہوہ خانہ بھی". It reads like a word-for-word calque. No Urdu writer says it this way, and it is even repeated inside a quoted name ("سنیار سمندری غذا والی کھانے کی جگہ"). Severity: متوسط. Suggestion: "ریستوران" (or "ہوٹل", the everyday Pakistani word) and "کیفے".
2. "قہوہ خانہ" for a café sounds old-fashioned and Persianized. The body text itself says "کافی", and "کیفے" or "کافی شاپ" is what readers actually say. Severity: معمولی.
3. "تجارتی نام" is used for "brand" (Lava: "اسی تجارتی نام کا ایک الگ کاروبار"; Ratio: "ریشو کافی کا ایک تجارتی نام ہے"). It sounds foreign. Severity: معمولی. Suggestion: "برانڈ".
4. Arabic "ال" names are glued to Urdu words, as in "الخلیج سڑک" and "شاہ عبد العزیز سڑک". It reads as half Arabic and half Urdu. Severity: معمولی. Suggestion: "خلیج روڈ" or one consistent style.
5. The link text runs straight into the address or category with no space: "محلہ المزروعگوگل میپس پر دیکھیں ↗", "قصر خزامتاریخی مقامات", "مسجد ام زرینیقمساجد اور مذہبی مقامات". "زرینیقمساجد" parses as one word. This may only be an artefact of how the text was cut. Severity: معمولی.
6. The footer on every page says "درجہ بندی اور اوقات بھی وہیں سے لیے جاتے ہیں", but no rating or opening hours appear anywhere on any page. So the sentence promises something that is not there. "درجہ بندی" could mean "ranking" or "rating", and I could not tell which. "ہر تازہ کاری پر" is also stiff officialese. Severity: متوسط. Suggestion: say plainly what is shown, or drop it.
7. The button "اشتراک کریں" is stiff. Pakistanis say "شیئر کریں". Severity: معمولی.
8. The footer says "نہ کسی خاص کھانے کی سفارش", yet the Sharq page lists many dishes. This is not a real contradiction, but it reads oddly. Severity: معمولی.
9. "ہفوف" and "الاحساء" are used from the first line without saying that Hofuf is the city in the Al-Ahsa region. I could not tell where I would be going. Severity: معمولی. This is missing information, not something I can fix with wording.
