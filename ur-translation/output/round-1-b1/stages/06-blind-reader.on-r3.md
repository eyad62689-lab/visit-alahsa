1. Page: قصر ابراہیم — grade: moderate — misreads on first pass (number)
   Text: «یونیسکو کے عالمی ورثے کا حصہ · 1563-004»
   What happened: I first took «1563» for a year (the year the fort was built), because this page gives no other date for the building. Only when I met «1563-009» on مسجد جواثا and «1563-007» on قصر صاہود did I realise it is some reference number. Nowhere is it said what the number is or why I need it.
   Wording: either drop the number, or put a word in front of it that says what it is.

3. Page: قصر ابراہیم — grade: minor — register
   Text: «یہ عیش و عشرت کا محل نہیں بلکہ ایک قلعہ ہے»
   What happened: the point is useful (the name says "palace", the place is a fort) and I understood it at once. But «عیش و عشرت» carries a disapproving shade in Urdu (indulgence, luxury one frowns at); it sounds a little like a sermon about palaces in general.

8. Page: قصر ابراہیم — grade: moderate — unclear, sounds foreign

9. Page: قصر ابراہیم — grade: moderate — sounds foreign, left hanging
   Text: «تو تاریخ کی وہ تہیں پڑھ لیں گے جو نسل در نسل جمتی چلی گئیں۔»
   What happened: "you will read the layers of history that kept settling" is not how an Urdu writer would say it; «تہیں پڑھ لینا» is a borrowed picture. And it promises history that the page never tells: no period, no ruler, no event. The promise hangs.
   Wording (something like): «تو آپ کو اندازہ ہوگا کہ یہ عمارت کتنے زمانے دیکھ چکی ہے۔»

10. Page: قصر ابراہیم — grade: moderate — card and hours pull against each other
   Text: «| بہترین وقت | دن میں، تاکہ آپ طرز تعمیر کی باریکیاں اور اندرونی صحن کی روشنی دیکھ سکیں |» against «جمعہ شام 4 بجے سے رات 8 بجے تک»
   What happened: I am planning a weekend. The card says the best time is in the daytime, but on Friday the place opens only at 4 in the afternoon, and on Saturday it is shut from 1 to 4. So on a weekend "daytime" really means Saturday morning, and I had to work that out myself. «دن میں» is also very loose (morning? noon?). Separately, «اندرونی صحن کی روشنی دیکھ سکیں» ("see the light of the inner courtyard") sounds translated.
   Wording for the last part: «اندرونی صحن میں پڑتی روشنی».
