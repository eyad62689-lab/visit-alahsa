# Reader observations — Urdu pages (home, places list, one place, not-found, extra labels)

Read as a phone visitor planning a family trip. The top bar, menu and page bottom are commented on once (under page 1). Grades: `serious` / `moderate` / `minor`.

1. **Text:** «فہرست» and «مرکزی فہرست»
   **Where:** page 1, top bar — the button that opens the menu, and the name announced for the menu itself.
   **What happened:** «فہرست» means "list / table of contents" to me. On a button I would not be sure it opens the site menu; «مرکزی فہرست» ("central list") told me even less. Every Urdu app I use calls this «مینو».
   **Grade:** moderate
   **Expected:** «مینو» / «مرکزی مینو»

2. **Text:** «سیر کے راستے» · «تجویز کردہ راستہ» · «اپنا راستہ خود ترتیب دیں» · «ان راستوں میں شامل» · «جن سے آپ اپنا راستہ مکمل کر سکتے ہیں» · «صفحے کا راستہ» · and in the body «بل کھاتے راستے», «اچانک راستہ … جا نکلتا ہے»
   **Where:** menu item and footer link (page 1); kicker and link in the two-day block (page 1); heading and sentence near the bottom of page 3; breadcrumb name (page 3); body paragraph (page 3).
   **What happened:** In the menu, «سیر کے راستے» read to me as walking paths or trails. Only when I reached «نخلستان میں دو دن» did I understand that «راستہ» here means a day-by-day programme. The same word is then also the real path inside the caves, and the name of the breadcrumb. One word, three things; I had to stop each time. «راستہ مکمل کرنا» ("complete your road") in particular is not something Urdu says about a trip plan.
   **Grade:** moderate
   **Expected:** for the programme sense something like «سیر کا پروگرام» / «سفری پروگرام»; I cannot say more than that.

3. **Text:** «بازار، تفریح گاہیں اور کھیت» against «تفریح گاہیں اور باغات» and «حساوی لیموں کا باغ»
   **Where:** menu item and footer link (page 1); category label on a nearby-place card (page 3); a stop in the two-day block (page 1).
   **What happened:** «کھیت» is a crop field — nobody goes sightseeing to a «کھیت», so I could not tell what the third part of this menu item offers. Then the category on page 3 is «تفریح گاہیں اور باغات», and the lemon place is a «باغ». I could not work out whether «کھیت», «باغات» and «باغ» are the same kind of place or three different ones.
   **Grade:** moderate

4. **Text:** «نخلستان کے پھل»
   **Where:** menu item and footer link (page 1).
   **What happened:** I would not know what I get if I tap it: a page about dates and fruit? things to buy? or "fruits" in the figurative sense (the produce/achievements of the oasis)? It sits between «تقریبات» and «الاحساء کے پکوان», which did not help.
   **Grade:** moderate

5. **Text:** «آن لائن دکانیں»

   **What happened:** Whose shops? Does this site sell something, or is it a list of other people's shops? Nothing else on the four pages mentions shopping online, so I had no way to guess.
   **Grade:** moderate

6. **Text:** «تقریبات», then under it «میلے، جو سال بھر ورثے میں نئی جان ڈالتے ہیں۔»

   **Grade:** moderate

I could not tell which one does what, or whether they are the same page. Also «پسندیدہ مقامات» and «محفوظ کردہ مقامات» look like two names for the places I saved.
