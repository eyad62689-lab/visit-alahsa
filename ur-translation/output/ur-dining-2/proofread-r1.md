### M2 · MANDATORY: N710 misstates the scope of N662, the ruling it narrows
- N710's text: «N662 binds «روایتی مہمان خانہ» to the source term 'heritage inn' / «نُزل تراثي»».
- N662's binds line (STAY s4) is wider: "the dining batches wherever they call this place an inn; **every later /ur/ 'heritage inn' / 'heritage hotel'**". N662 (2) adds that 'heritage hotel' "is said only as the class of the licence, never as a predicate".

That is defensible, but nothing in N710 says that it narrows N662's binds or that N662 (2) is confined to the licence sentence. Once both rulings are in memory, the record contradicts itself without saying so. The batch text is not affected.
- Fix (record only): extend N710's `ruling` with one sentence, for example: "N662's binds line reaches every later 'heritage hotel'; its point (2) governs the licence sentence (class of the licence, never a predicate). This ruling takes the place sense, a building named as the location of a restaurant (neither licence class nor predicate), out of N662's binds for this card and the alkoot-hotel detail page." A `base_amendment` on N662 is not possible in this file: N662 enters the termbase only when STAY merges, and the dry run would fail on «حكم غائب للتعديل».

### R1 · RECOMMENDED: `dining.dot-bakery.blurb` turns a touch of blue into "prominent"
- Text: «… اور کاؤنٹر پر نیلا رنگ نمایاں ہے۔». The Arabic (the arbiter) says «بلمسة زرقاء», a touch of blue, and the English says 'blue-accented'. «نمایاں» ('stands out, is prominent') raises the degree.

### R2 · RECOMMENDED: `dining.greasy-fingers.blurb` word order
