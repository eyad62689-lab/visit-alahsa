1. Search-result title and description (◆ / ◇). The title "الاحساء کے موسمی پھل" is clear. The description promises "الاحساء کے نخلستان کے پھلوں کی موسم بہ موسم سیر" ("a season-by-season tour of the fruits"). A "سیر" of fruits sounds like a translated brochure phrase; I expected a guide, not a tour. It ends with "سرکاری ذرائع سے تصدیق شدہ معلومات کے ساتھ", but the page itself later cites "بعض ذرائع", a news agency and local newspapers, and the footer says the site is independent and not a government body. The promise of "officially verified" is stronger than what the page delivers. Grade: moderate. Possible wording: "…موسم بہ موسم تعارف: …، قابلِ اعتماد ذرائع کی بنیاد پر۔"

2. Eyebrow above the heading: "نخلستان کی نعمتوں سے" ("from the blessings of the oasis"). It is a fragment that waits for a verb or noun and never gets one. I read it twice to see whether it belonged to the heading. Grade: minor. Possible wording: "نخلستان کی نعمتیں".

3. Intro paragraph under "# الاحساء کے موسمی پھل": "گرمیوں کے سنہری رطب، یعنی تازہ پکی کھجوروں، سے لے کر سردیوں کے کنار، یعنی بیر، اور نخلستان کے مشہور لیموں تک۔" The glosses set off by commas split the noun from its postposition ("کھجوروں، سے"). I lost the thread and had to re-read to find where "سے لے کر … تک" closes. Glossing "رطب" here is helpful in itself. Grade: moderate. Possible wording: "گرمیوں کے سنہری رطب (تازہ پکی کھجوروں) سے لے کر سردیوں کے کنار (بیر) اور نخلستان کے مشہور لیموں تک۔"

4. Figures block: "4 لاکھ / کھجور کے درختوں کے ساتھ ساتھ پھل دار درخت". I could not tell whether the 4 lakh are other fruit trees growing alongside the palms, or fruit trees in addition to (and so including) palms. The source line "گنیز ورلڈ ریکارڈز کے اندراج (اکتوبر 2020) اور ماحولیات، پانی اور زراعت کی وزارت کے اعداد و شمار کے مطابق" does not say which figure comes from which source. Grade: moderate. Possible wording: "کھجور کے علاوہ دوسرے پھل دار درخت".

5. Eyebrow "پھلوں کی سیر" above "## ہر موسم کا ایک پھل". As in item 1, a "tour of fruits" sounds odd in Urdu. The heading and the line "اپنی سیر کا موسم چنیں اور اس کے پھل دیکھیں:" read well. Grade: minor.

6. Season filter labels ("تمام موسم / سردیاں / بہار / گرمیاں / خزاں") read well. The date cards (خلاص، شیشی، رزیز، برحی، بواکیر) give only months and stages, with no season word, unlike every other card ("گرمیاں: …", "سردیاں: …"). Several dates run into September and October, which is autumn to me. I cannot tell which filter button they fall under. If "خزاں" answers "↻ اس موسم کے لیے ابھی کوئی پھل درج نہیں کیا گیا۔", that contradicts the date cards. Grade: moderate. Missing: the season name on the date cards.

7. "## کھجوریں، نخلستان کا تاج", production figures: "تقریباً 1 لاکھ سے 1.2 لاکھ ٹن تک ہے، جبکہ سعودی خبر رساں ادارے نے اکتوبر 2024 میں 2 لاکھ ٹن سے زیادہ کی پیداوار بتائی۔" "1.2 لاکھ" reads awkwardly; in Urdu we say "ایک لاکھ بیس ہزار". Two figures that differ by nearly double are put side by side with no word on which is current, so I came away not knowing the real size. The same digit style "1 لاکھ سے زیادہ" appears later in the lime card. Grade: moderate. Possible wording: "ایک لاکھ سے ایک لاکھ بیس ہزار ٹن".

8. Same paragraph: "الاحساء میں کھجور کے درختوں اور کھجوروں کے مرکز نے". I did not recognise this as the name of an institution and read it first as a generic description. Grade: minor.

9. Same paragraph: "تین چوتھائی حصہ خلاص، شیشی اور رزیز اقسام کا ہوتا ہے". The variety names read as ordinary Urdu words. "شیشی" is a small glass bottle or vial, and "خلاص" reads as colloquial "finished/over". In this list sentence I stumbled before reaching "اقسام". The photo caption "شیشی کے پیلے رطب" also first reads as "yellow rutab of the vial". The card headings make the names clear later, but this first mention misleads. Grade: moderate.

10. Contradiction between the date intro and the رزیز card. The intro says roughly three-quarters of total production is خلاص، شیشی and رزیز. The رزیز card says "آج یہ نسبتاً نایاب ہو گئی ہے، کیونکہ زیادہ تر کاشتکار خلاص اگانے لگے ہیں۔" One of the main varieties cannot also be rare. This made me distrust the figures. Grade: serious.

11. The stage label "پوری پکی کھجور" on the خلاص، شیشی، رزیز and بواکیر cards and in the table. The intro defines رطب as "تازہ پکی کھجور", and the cards then contrast it with "پوری پکی کھجور". "Fresh ripe" against "fully ripe" is a distinction I had to puzzle over. I was not sure "پوری پکی" means the dry, finished date sold in boxes. Grade: moderate. Missing: one plain explanation of the stage the first time it is used.

12. شیشی card season: "پوری پکی کھجور: وسط اگست سے مہینے کے اواخر تک". "مہینے کے اواخر" made me stop to work out which month. It is August by inference, but it reads as hanging. Grade: minor. Possible wording: "وسط اگست سے اگست کے اواخر تک".

13. خلاص description: "گودا نرم اور مٹھاس متوازن اور خوشبودار". Sweetness that is "fragrant" sounds off; the fragrance belongs to the fruit. Otherwise the card reads well and tells me what I would taste. The closing mineral line "جست، مینگنیز اور سیلینیم جیسی معدنیات بھرپور مقدار میں" sounds like a health brochure. Grade: minor.

14. Photo captions: the خلاص caption has "ایک ہی طشت پر" and the شیشی caption has "ایک ہی طشتری میں". Two words for what looks like the same dish, and "طشت پر دو پیالوں میں" is slightly clumsy. Grade: minor.

15. رزیز card reads well overall, including the explanation of “سفسیف”, though "یہ … تیار ہوتی ہے" takes a moment to attach to the sweet rather than the date. Grade: minor.

16. برحی card: the season "رطب: گرمیوں میں" is too vague to plan a weekend around, compared with the other date cards. Grade: moderate. Missing: months.

17. برحی description: "پیلے بلح (بسر) کے مرحلے میں". Two unfamiliar Arabic terms appear, one in brackets, and I could not tell whether the bracket is a synonym or a different stage. "بلح" is never explained, yet the card then says the date is eaten at three stages, "بلح، رطب اور پوری پکی کھجور". "القصیم" also appears with no hint that it is a region. Grade: moderate.

18. بواکیر card heading: "بواکیر، جلد پکنے والی اور مقامی اقسام". "بواکیر" is an unknown word with no gloss. With the commas, the heading reads like a list of three separate things rather than one group. Grade: moderate. Possible wording: "بواکیر (جلد پکنے والی اقسام) اور دوسری مقامی اقسام".

19. بواکیر card versus its own season line and the other cards. The season line says "رطب اور پوری پکی کھجور: مئی کے اواخر سے اکتوبر تک". The description says the late varieties "ام رحیم، زاملی اور ہلالی جیسے دیر سے پکنے والے رطب وسط اگست تک ملتے ہیں". Yet the خلاص card has its rutab running to late August, later than these "late" varieties. I could not work out whether late rutab ends in mid-August or in October. For deciding when to go, this matters. Grade: serious.

20. بواکیر description: the long string of names "طیار، مجناز اور غر … خنیزی … ام رحیم، زاملی اور ہلالی … شبیبی، شہل، وصیلی، حاتمی، کاسبی". Several read as ordinary words or adjectives ("ہلالی", "کاسبی", "حاتمی"). Context says they are varieties, but the list is tiring and gives me nothing to look for. Grade: minor.

21. Section heading "## ترشاوہ پھل". The word is correct but bookish and agricultural-office Urdu. In everyday speech I would say "کھٹے پھل" or "ترش پھل". Grade: minor.

22. Lime card title: "الاحساء کا لیموں (بن زہیری)". "بن زہیری" in brackets reads like a man's name ("son of Zuhairi"). I could not tell that it is the local or variety name of the lime. Grade: moderate.

23. Lime description: "“حساوی لیموں کی نمائش”". "حساوی" appears here for the first time and is used nowhere else; everywhere else the page says "الاحساء کا لیموں". At first it is not obvious that this is the same lime. The festival is said to happen "ہر سال" but no month is given, which is exactly what a trip planner needs. "الاحساء ایوان تجارت" lacks a linking izafat or "کا" and reads stiffly. Grade: moderate. Missing: when the exhibition takes place.

24. Lime description: "(“جمید”: دھوپ میں سکھایا اور پرانا کیا ہوا لیموں کا رس)". The bracket with a quoted term and a colon after a finished sentence is a heavy construction, but the meaning comes through. The rest of the card (taste, colour, family juicing tradition) reads well and is appealing. Grade: minor.

25. اترنج card: "یہ ترشاوہ پھلوں کی ایک قدیم قسم ہے۔ اس کا پھل بڑا اور خوشبودار ہوتا ہے اور چھلکا موٹا اور جھری دار۔" That is all. The card does not say whether it is eaten, how it tastes or what it is used for. Grade: moderate. Missing: taste or use.

26. Yellow melon card season: "گرمیاں: تقریباً مئی کے اواخر سے". It gives a start but no end, so I do not know whether a July or August visit still finds it. Grade: moderate. Missing: end of season.

27. Melon description opens with the abstract "یہ ایک زرعی اور ثقافتی ورثہ ہے" before telling me what the fruit is. It ends with a nutrition list ("وٹامن سی، پوٹاشیم اور کیروٹینائڈز … حرارے کم"). "کیروٹینائڈز" is technical, and the passage feels like a health leaflet. "یہ مقامی نسل" (نسل for a plant variety) is understandable. Grade: minor.

28. Fig card season: just "گرمیاں". It is too vague to plan by, the same problem as item 16. The description itself reads well: colour, size, sweetness and the Qur'an mention all land naturally. Grade: moderate. Missing: months.

29. بمبر card: "اس کا سائنسی نام" ends the line, and the next line begins "ہے، اور اسے سبستان…". The scientific name is simply absent, so the sentence reads "its scientific name … is" with a hole. It looks broken. Grade: moderate.

30. بمبر card: "بمبر" is unknown to me. The alternative names "سبستان، مخیط اور ہمبو" partly help ("سبستان" rings a bell). "اس کی چپچپاہٹ ضرب المثل بن گئی ہے" mentions a proverb but does not give it, which leaves me hanging. The photo caption "بمبر کے پھل" describes nothing. Grade: minor.

31. Grapes card season: "گرمیاں: گرمیوں کے شروع سے" ("summer: from the start of summer"). It is tautological and has no end. Grade: moderate. Missing: months or end.

32. Grapes description: "جسے چھوٹا ہونے کے باوجود لوگ اس کے ذائقے کی وجہ سے پسند کرتے ہیں" is a slightly tangled clause. The card ends by saying the variety is close to disappearing, which leaves me unsure whether I can actually find or buy it on a visit. Grade: minor.

33. Pomegranate card: the season slot reads "تقریباً ناپید". That is not a season, and it sits under "گرمیوں کے پھل" and in the table's season column as if it were one. I did not know whether to expect it in summer or not at all. Grade: moderate.

34. Pomegranate card naming: the title "الاحساء کا انار (السواری)" and the caption "السواری انار" present "السواری" as the variety. The description says "اس کے سب سے بڑے پھلوں کو “السواری” کہا جاتا ہے", i.e. only the largest fruits carry that name. These are inconsistent. The Arabic article "ال" also makes the word look un-Urdu. Grade: moderate.

35. Pomegranate description: "یہ اتنا میٹھا ہوتا ہے کہ مٹھاس کی شدت سے پھٹ جاتا ہے". "It bursts from the intensity of its sweetness" sounds like a literal translation of an Arabic idiom. I read it twice. Grade: minor.

36. Papaya card is brief but clear ("جون سے اگست تک"); it reads fine.

37. Mulberry card reads well and gives a usable season ("تقریباً اپریل میں، موسم بمشکل ایک مہینے کا"). Small points: "سرخ، سیاہ اور نیلے رنگ" ("blue" mulberries) surprised me. The closing line "اس کی کاشت نخلستان کی امید افزا فصلوں میں شمار ہوتی ہے" is دفتری in tone. Grade: minor.

38. Category labels are inconsistent. The section heading is "## بہار اور سردیوں کے پھل", but the cards are labelled "بہار کے پھل" and "سردیوں کے پھل", and the table again says "بہار اور سردیوں کے پھل". Likewise the date cards say "کھجوریں" while the table says "کھجوریں، نخلستان کا تاج". This is not confusing, just untidy. Grade: minor.

39. کنار card: it reads well and is warm (children like it, folk songs at the market), with a clear season. The variety names in quotation marks work. "صینی" sounds like "سینی" (a tray) when read quickly. "بازاروں میں اس کی خریداری کے ساتھ اس کے اپنے لوک گیت بھی جڑے ہیں" is slightly loose, since "اس کے اپنے" could point to the fruit or the buying. Grade: minor.

40. Table caption: "نخلستان کے پھلوں کے موسم، پھلوں کے تعارف کے مطابق". The second half is awkward; I took it to mean "as given in the fruit descriptions above". The table matches the cards exactly, which is reassuring. Grade: minor.

41. "## اس صفحے کی معلومات کی درستی": "ہم ہر بات شائع کرنے سے پہلے اس کی تصدیق ضرور کرتے ہیں". This firm claim sits beside two production figures that disagree (item 7), a variety that is both major and rare (item 10), and grapes sourced from local newspapers. The confident tone made the contradictions stand out more. "ایک فصل سے دوسری فصل تک تھوڑا آگے پیچھے" is fine. Grade: moderate.

42. Whole page, missing: for a family deciding what to look for, the page never says where these fruits can be seen or bought (market, farm or season stall), apart from a passing "بازاروں میں" for کنار. Grade: moderate. Missing: where to find them.

43. Top bar and footer: nothing stopped me. "ایک نخلستان، جس کی اپنی ایک داستان ہے" reads nicely.
