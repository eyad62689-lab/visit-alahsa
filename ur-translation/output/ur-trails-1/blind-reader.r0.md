## Overall impression

The Urdu is mostly correct, and the script and gender agreement are clean. Many passages read naturally («سورج ڈوبنے سے پہلے واپسی کی راہ لیں», «چھت دار بازار جس کی رونق آج بھی قائم ہے»). The practical cues (duration, mode of transport, 4x4 warning) are clear.

The problems fall into three groups:
1. **Words that misread on first pass.** Source names like سبق, عاجل, الیوم and المدینہ look like ordinary words or cities, and so do «خلاص», «قصر» for a fort, and «مدرسہ» for a government school.
2. **Calques and bookish phrases.** Examples are «ارتقا پذیر ثقافتی منظر», «آبی اجسام», «کسی ایک موضوع کے گرد جوڑتے», and the vague «ریاست» used alongside «مملکت».
3. **Gaps in what I can actually do.** This is worst on the oasis trail, whose two stops are categories with no specific place and no map. There is also no orientation on what or where الاحساء is.

The religious passage would benefit from customary honorifics for a Pakistani audience.

**Rating: 78 / 100** for naturalness and clarity.
