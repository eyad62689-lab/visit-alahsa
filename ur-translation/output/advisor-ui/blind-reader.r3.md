1. Item 15, "آپ آج کے سوالات کی حد تک پہنچ چکے ہیں۔" — Misread on first pass. "سوالات کی حد تک" is a stock phrase meaning "as far as questions go", so I first read "you have reached ... as far as today's questions". Only on the second pass did I get "you hit the limit". It sounds like a calque of English "reached the limit". MAJOR. Suggestion: "آج کے سوالوں کی حد پوری ہو گئی ہے۔"

2. Item 15, "کل پھر ملیں گے" — Register shift: it is breezy and chatty after a stiff first sentence. It also promises "tomorrow" without saying when the limit resets, and it does not say how many questions are allowed. The count is missing. MEDIUM.

3. Item 14, "سیاحتی مقامات دیکھ لیں، یا “سفر کا منصوبہ بنائیں” کا صفحہ کھول لیں۔" — Left hanging. I cannot tell whether "سیاحتی مقامات" is a link, a menu item or just a suggestion, and nothing in the chat is clickable that I can see. It is also worded differently from item 15 ("سیاحتی مقامات کے صفحات"). The quoted title in curly quotes only helps if the site has exactly that label. MEDIUM.

4. Item 14, "بلبل ابھی آرام کر رہی ہے۔" — Cute, but an outage is dressed as a bird resting. I was not sure whether it is broken, or whether I should wait and retry. It does not say "try later", unlike item 16, which does. "ابھی" hints at it but does not say it. MEDIUM. Item 14 and item 16 look like two different situations, and I could not tell what the difference means for me.

5. Item 8, "آپ کے سوالات کی پراسیسنگ Anthropic کی سروس کے ذریعے کی جاتی ہے اور انہیں محفوظ نہیں کیا جاتا۔" — Officialese (دفتری اردو): "کی جاتی ہے ... کے ذریعے ... کیا جاتا" is heavy passive. "Anthropic" is an unexplained Latin name that I do not know. "انہیں محفوظ نہیں کیا جاتا" does not say by whom, the site or Anthropic, so it is ambiguous. MEDIUM. Suggestion: "آپ کے سوالات Anthropic کی سروس پر پراسیس ہوتے ہیں اور محفوظ نہیں کیے جاتے۔"

6. Item 8, first sentence "بلبل صرف اس ویب سائٹ کے مواد سے جواب دیتی ہے۔" — Fine, but it conflicts with item 3. There the bird says it knows "every date palm" and offers places "only locals know". Those are bigger promises than "only this website's content". MEDIUM.

7. Item 3, "میں بلبل ہوں، اور الاحساء میں کھجور کے ایک ایک درخت سے واقف ہوں!" — Brochure-shouty hyperbole with an exclamation mark. It also makes a claim I do not believe. MEDIUM. The "ایک ایک درخت" boast is the part that grates.

8. Item 3, "کیا آپ سیر کے لیے آئے ہیں" — "آئے ہیں" means "have you come", as if I am already there. I am only planning a trip, so I paused. "سیر" here is a bit odd as well, since in Urdu it usually means a stroll or outing, not a trip. The either/or question is also unclear: do I answer "sightseeing" or "local places"? MEDIUM. Suggestion: "کیا آپ گھومنے آ رہے ہیں، یا ...". This is only a wording idea.

9. Item 3, "مجھے بتائیں کہ آپ کو کیا پسند ہے، میں آپ کے مزاج کے مطابق سیر کا منصوبہ بنا دوں گی۔" — Run-on with a comma splice. "مزاج" is slightly bookish; "پسند" is more natural. MINOR. Nothing says how long a plan is, what days, or whether it is a one-day or a multi-day plan.

10. Item 3, "آئیے، خوش آمدید!" — Two greetings stacked, a little stiff and Persianized. MINOR. "خوش آمدید" alone would do.

11. Item 3 — Missing: nowhere does it say where Al-Ahsa is (country or city), or that "بلبل" is an automated assistant. Item 8's mention of Anthropic is the first hint that it is an AI. A first-time Pakistani reader may think a human is typing. MEDIUM.

12. Item 2, "بلبل، Visit Al-Ahsa کی مشیر" — "مشیر" feels official, as in a government or ministry adviser, and not like a travel helper. It is also a mixed script: Latin "Visit Al-Ahsa" here, but "الاحساء" in Urdu elsewhere. That inconsistency made me wonder whether the two are the same place. The comma plus the Latin run is a bit hard to scan right to left. MEDIUM for the register. Suggestion: "رہنما" would sound more ordinary, but the choice is yours.

13. Item 1, "بلبل سے پوچھیں" — Fine and short. "بلبل" is an ordinary word (nightingale) and it was not clear at first whether it is a name, but the bird picture settles it. It also does not say what I can ask about, though that is explained later. MINOR.

14. Item 5 button, "جبل القارہ کے اوقات اور فیس کیا ہیں؟" — The name "القارہ" looks slightly un-Urdu, with the ہ ending reading as "Qara" and "ال" stuck on. As a reader I could not tell whether it is a hill, a cave or a fort. "فیس" for an entry fee sounds like school fees to me; "ٹکٹ" or "داخلہ فیس" is more natural. "اوقات" alone is understood as timings, though it has a faint other sense. A natural question? Mostly yes, but I would type "ٹکٹ" instead of "فیس". MINOR to MEDIUM.

15. Item 4 button, "ایک دن میں کیا دیکھوں؟" — Natural. I would type this. MINOR concern: it does not say "in Al-Ahsa", but the chat context gives that.

16. Item 6 button, "الاحساء کے پکوان کہاں چکھوں؟" — Natural enough. "پکوان" is good. "چکھوں" is a little cute, and I would type "کھاؤں" or "کیا کھاؤں". Is it "where" or "what"? I would probably ask "what" first. MINOR.

17. Item 11 (hidden), "نئی گفتگو" — "گفتگو" is bookish for a chat window. It does not tell a screen-reader user that it deletes the current chat. "نئی چیٹ" is more usual. MINOR to MEDIUM.

18. Item 13, "متعلقہ صفحات" — Officialese ("متعلقہ" is office language), but it is common on web pages and I understood it at once. MINOR.

19. Item 16, "بلبل سے رابطہ نہیں ہو سکا۔ تھوڑی دیر بعد دوبارہ کوشش کریں۔" — Clear, natural and short. Best text in the set. MINOR: it does not say whether the problem is my internet or the site.

20. Items 7, 9, 10, 12, 17, "اپنا سوال لکھیں…", "بھیجیں", "بند کریں", "بلبل لکھ رہی ہے…", "آپ" — All natural, no problems. Gender is clear throughout: feminine bird ("دوں گی", "دیتی ہے", "رہی ہے", "مشیر"), so no gender stumble.
