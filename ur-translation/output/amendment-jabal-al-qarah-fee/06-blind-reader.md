# Reader notes — جبل القارہ (Urdu reader, Pakistan)

Overall: the descriptive paragraph reads like real Urdu, warm and fluent. I mostly stumbled in the facts box and the lower half of the page.

1. **medium** — «بڑوں کے لیے 50 سعودی ریال۔ ٹکٹ “ارض الحضارات” (تہذیبوں کی سرزمین) نامی ثقافتی گاؤں سے ملتے ہیں» — the first sentence closes with «۔» but the second one just stops, with no «۔». Because the first half is punctuated, the missing stop at the end looks like the line was cut off; I re-read to see whether something was missing after «ملتے ہیں».

2. **medium** — «بڑوں کے لیے 50 سعودی ریال» — it says "for adults" and then nothing about children. I am travelling with family (the page itself says «خاندان»), so I immediately ask: are children free, half price, or unknown? Both the box and the FAQ leave it open.

3. **medium** — «ٹکٹ “ارض الحضارات” نامی ثقافتی گاؤں سے ملتے ہیں» — I do not know where this village is. Is it at the foot of the mountain, the entrance complex itself, or somewhere else in the city I must go to first? «نامی ثقافتی گاؤں» ("a cultural village called …") sounds like a separate place, which made me unsure.

4. **low** — the gloss «(تہذیبوں کی سرزمین)» appears in the facts box but not in the FAQ answer «ٹکٹ “ارض الحضارات” نامی ثقافتی گاؤں سے ملتے ہیں۔». Small inconsistency; the gloss is helpful and I missed it the second time.

5. **low** — «“ارض الحضارات”» — English-style curly quotes inside Urdu text. Understandable, but in right-to-left text they can look reversed; many Urdu pages use ” “ or ’ ‘ ordering or simple « ». Not a comprehension problem.

## Follow-up

**(a)** The fee line in the facts box — «بڑوں کے لیے 50 سعودی ریال۔ ٹکٹ “ارض الحضارات” (تہذیبوں کی سرزمین) نامی ثقافتی گاؤں سے ملتے ہیں» — is two sentences. The first is a verbless fragment ("50 Saudi riyals for adults"), closed with «۔»; that is normal for a facts box. The second is a full sentence. The punctuation inside is natural Urdu (the «۔» between them, the bracketed gloss). What is odd is the ending: the second sentence has no «۔» at all. With one full stop in the middle and none at the end, the line looks unfinished or truncated — I checked whether text was missing after «ملتے ہیں». Compare the hours line just above, which has no internal «۔» and so its unpunctuated ending looks fine; here the mix is what stands out. Either add the final «۔» or avoid the internal full stop. A lesser oddity: the English-style curly quotes around «ارض الحضارات».
