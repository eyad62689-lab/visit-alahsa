1. Title, heading and every mention: “کفو” کی راتیں
   On first pass I read کفو as the Urdu word I know (kufw, "an equal match", as in ہم کفو, marriage compatibility), so the name came out as "nights of the equal match". The curly quotes do not help. They read like scare quotes, as if the word were being used ironically, not as the name of the festival. Only the paragraph's explanation two lines later tells me it is a name, and even then it does not say how to pronounce it. Grade: serious. Possible fix: mark it as a name the first time it appears, e.g. «کفو» نامی رمضان کی راتیں, or explain it straight away in the description line.

2. Paragraph under the heading: “کفو” عربی کے ایک فعل سے نکلا ہے جس کا مطلب ہے “صلہ پانا”، یعنی انعام اور تعریف حاصل کرنا۔
   The sentence is grammatical, but after reading it I still could not see why a Ramadan festival would be called "getting a reward". The meaning is given, but how it connects to the event is not, so the explanation just hangs. It also clashes with the Urdu sense of کفو (item 1), and nothing tells me the Urdu meaning is not the one meant here. Grade: moderate. I can't offer a wording, because the missing link (why the event has this name) is not on the page.

3. Paragraph under the heading: اسے الاحساء میں شاہ فیصل یونیورسٹی منعقد کرتی ہے، اور یہ میلہ پہلی بار 2024 میں منعقد ہوا۔
   منعقد is used twice in one sentence, which sounds heavy and office-like. Grade: minor. Possible fix: اسے الاحساء میں شاہ فیصل یونیورسٹی منعقد کرتی ہے، اور یہ پہلی بار 2024 میں ہوا تھا۔

4. Paragraph under the heading: ان راتوں کی نمایاں سرگرمیوں میں “غبقہ” بھی شامل ہے۔ یہ خلیجی عرب ممالک میں رمضان المبارک کی راتوں کا ایک مقبول کھانا اور سماجی رسم ہے۔
   غبقہ is a word I don't know. It is explained in the next sentence, which helps, but "a meal and a social custom" still left me unsure whether this is a meal served to visitors, something to buy, or something to watch. The paragraph moves from what the name means straight to غبقہ, with nothing in between. Grade: minor. It is clear enough, but what the visitor actually does is not explained.

5. Search-result description: رمضان المبارک کا مہینہ، رمضان کے دوران۔
   "The month of Ramadan, during Ramadan" says the same thing twice in a row. It reads like two fields pasted together. Grade: moderate. Possible fix: رمضان المبارک کے دوران۔

6. Facts block: موسم / رمضان المبارک کا مہینہ — کب / رمضان کے دوران — اندازاً دورانیہ / رمضان کی کئی راتیں (and متوقع وقت / رمضان کے دوران just above them)
   Four labels give me the same answer, "Ramadan", four times. موسم as a label also reads as "weather". اندازاً دورانیہ is bookish (دفتری). Grade: moderate. Possible fix: keep one line, e.g. کب: رمضان کی کئی راتوں میں.

7. Under the heading: رمضان کے دوران / متوقع وقت
   The value comes before its label, so at first I read متوقع وقت as a separate heading. "Expected" also made me wonder whether the event is even confirmed for this year. Grade: minor.

8. Before you go: یہ تقریب ہر سال تقریباً اسی موسم میں لوٹ آتی ہے
   Ramadan moves through the solar year, so "about the same season every year" made me stop. Is the event tied to Ramadan, or to a fixed time of year? Grade: moderate. Possible fix: یہ تقریب ہر سال رمضان میں لوٹ آتی ہے.

9. Before you go: جانے سے پہلے منتظمین کے اعلانات سے تصدیق کر لیں۔
   I am told to check the organisers' announcements, but the page never says where they are published: no account, no link, nothing. The instruction leaves me with nowhere to go. Grade: moderate. Missing: where to find the announcements.

10. Missing practical information for a family decision
   The page gives no dates for this year, no opening hours (before or after تراویح?), and does not say whether entry is free or ticketed. It does not say whether it suits families with women, beyond the children's section. The page openly says dates can change, which is honest, but I still cannot decide from this page alone. Grade: moderate (missing, not wrong).

11. Highlights: “کفو” کا باورچی خانہ
   "Kafu's kitchen". باورچی خانہ makes me think of a home kitchen, not a food area at a festival, so I could not tell if this is a place to eat, a cooking show, or stalls. Grade: moderate. I can't offer a wording, because what it is isn't stated.

12. Highlights: “غبقہ” کا کھانا

13. Place name: شاہ عبد اللہ ماحولیاتی پارک
   ماحولیاتی پارک sounds like a translated term ("environmental park") and is a bit stiff, though understandable. Grade: minor.

14. Organisers label: منتظمین / شاہ فیصل یونیورسٹی
   The label is plural but only one organiser is named. Grade: minor. Possible fix: منتظم.

15. Eyebrow line above the heading: نخلستان کا ایک موسم
   "A season of the oasis" is vague and brochure-like. I didn't know what it was labelling. Grade: minor.

16. Other events section heading: دوسرے موسم
   The heading "Other seasons" introduces a list of festivals, not seasons. With موسم also meaning weather, it reads oddly. Grade: minor. Possible fix: دوسری موسمی تقریبات.

17. Other events: حساوی چوغے کا میلہ
   حساوی is an adjective I haven't seen before. The site writes the place everywhere else as الاحساء, so at first I didn't connect the two. چوغہ for the robe is understandable. Grade: minor.

18. Other events: القیصریہ کی راتیں
   The Arabic article "ال" is kept, so to an Urdu reader it is plainly a foreign name. It is understandable, but nothing says what القیصریہ is. Grade: minor.

19. Header: the stray symbol №, and the language button ▢ زبان UR / UR
   № stands alone with no meaning I can see. "UR" in Latin letters, in an Urdu interface, is a small foreign touch. Grade: minor.

20. Search panel: ↻ ▢ ویب سائٹ میں تلاش (appears twice), and the hint split as Esc / سے بند کریں · تیر کے نشانوں سے اوپر نیچے جائیں · Enter سے کھولیں
