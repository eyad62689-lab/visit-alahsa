1. “کفو” کی راتیں (tab title, search description, breadcrumb, page heading, and throughout)
   What happened: To an Urdu reader کفو is already a familiar word, kufu: "equal standing, a suitable match", the word used in marriage talk (رشتہ کفو میں ہو). So my first reading of the heading was something like "Nights of the equal match", which made no sense for a Ramadan fair. The quotation marks tell me it is a name, but they don't say how to say it (kufu? kafu? kaffu?) or that it is not the Urdu word. The paragraph's explanation comes later, and it gives a different meaning without ever saying this is not the Urdu کفو. The clash stays.
   Grade: serious
   Suggestion: The first time the name appears, say it is a Gulf Arabic word and how it sounds, e.g. “کفو” (خلیجی عربی لفظ، تلفظ: کَفُو). I can't supply the exact pronunciation myself.
