1. Paragraph under the heading: “کفو” عربی کے ایک فعل سے نکلا ہے جس کا مطلب ہے “صلہ پانا”، یعنی انعام اور تعریف حاصل کرنا۔
   To me کفو is an everyday Urdu word: kufw, "an equal / a suitable match", the word we use in rishta and nikah talk (ہم کفو). On first reading the title means something like "Nights of the (marriage) match". The paragraph then says it means "صلہ پانا", which does not fit the word I know, and nothing tells me how to say the name. So I can't tell if this is a different word that looks the same, or whether I'm misreading it. The name is the first thing on the page, and it stays unclear even after the explanation. Grade: serious. Fix: give the pronunciation (or a transliteration) and say plainly that this is a different Arabic word from the Urdu کفو. I can't supply the pronunciation myself.

2. Title, heading, description and activity list: “کفو” کی راتیں / “کفو” کا باورچی خانہ (always in curly quotation marks)
   The quotation marks go around the name every single time. I can't tell what they are for: is it a nickname, a so-called, or a foreign word? Combined with item 1, it looks like the site isn't sure of the name either. Grade: minor. Fix: write the name without quotes once it has been introduced.

3. Description (search result): رمضان المبارک کا مہینہ، رمضان کے دوران۔
   Two pieces of text that say the same thing are run together ("the month of Ramadan, during Ramadan."). It reads like filler or a glitch, and gives no actual timing. Grade: moderate. Fix: "رمضان المبارک کی راتوں میں۔"

4. Lines under the heading and in the facts box: رمضان کے دوران / متوقع وقت / موسم: رمضان المبارک کا مہینہ / کب: رمضان کے دوران / اندازاً دورانیہ: رمضان کی کئی راتیں
   "During Ramadan" is said four or five ways in a row, and none of them is concrete. "کئی راتیں" (several nights): which ones? First ten, last ten, weekends? The page also never says what time it starts (after iftar? after taraweeh?), whether there is an entry fee, or whether it suits families and women. For a family outing in Ramadan, I'd need these things to decide, and they are missing. Grade: moderate (missing information, plus repetition).

5. Label: متوقع وقت (shown right after the value رمضان کے دوران)
   The value comes before its label, and "expected time" for an annual festival leaves me unsure whether the date is confirmed or a guess. Grade: minor. Fix: put the label first, e.g. "متوقع وقت: رمضان کے دوران".

6. Paragraph: یہ میلہ پہلی بار 2024 میں ہوا۔
   Understandable, but میلہ ... ہوا sounds flat for a festival; we'd normally say منعقد ہوا or شروع ہوا. Also, it calls itself "annual" but started only in 2024, so it's a young event; that's fine, just noting it. Grade: minor. Fix: "یہ میلہ پہلی بار 2024 میں منعقد ہوا۔"

7. Paragraph: ان راتوں کی نمایاں سرگرمیوں میں “غبقہ” بھی شامل ہے۔ یہ خلیجی عرب ممالک میں رمضان المبارک کی راتوں کا ایک مقبول کھانا اور سماجی رسم ہے۔
   غبقہ is unknown to a Pakistani reader. The explanation helps, but it doesn't say when in the night it is eaten or what is in it, so I still can't picture it. That information is missing, not wrong. Also, a meal is called one of the "activities". Grade: minor.

8. Activity list: “کفو” کا باورچی خانہ
   باورچی خانہ means a home kitchen, so as an activity name it's odd: is it food stalls, a cooking show, a place to eat? I can't tell what happens there. Grade: moderate. Fix: impossible without knowing what it is; a word like "کھانے کے اسٹال" would only work if that is what it actually is.

9. Activity list: “غبقہ” کا کھانا
   The paragraph says غبقہ is itself a meal, so "the food of the ghabqa" is circular. Grade: minor. Fix: "غبقہ (رمضان کی رات کا کھانا)" or just "غبقہ".

10. Facts box label: موسم — رمضان المبارک کا مہینہ
    موسم makes me think of weather or season (summer, winter), but the value is a month. The same label is used for "سردیاں" lower down, so for Ramadan it feels mismatched. Grade: minor.

11. Before you go: یہ تقریب ہر سال تقریباً اسی موسم میں لوٹ آتی ہے
    Ramadan moves about 11 days earlier every year, so "around the same season" is misleading for a Ramadan event. It returns in the same Islamic month, not the same season. Also, لوٹ آتی ہے is a bit literary. Grade: moderate. Fix: "یہ تقریب ہر سال رمضان میں ہوتی ہے، البتہ اس کی تاریخیں اور تفصیلات سال بہ سال بدل سکتی ہیں۔"

12. Label: منتظمین — شاہ فیصل یونیورسٹی
    The label is plural but there is only one organiser. Grade: minor. Fix: "منتظم".
