# Blind reader report, round 2: Urdu homepage (ur-home-1)

Reader stance: native Urdu reader from Pakistan, planning a possible trip, never heard of this place before. I read only `blind-input.txt`. Glued strings like `داستان شروع کریںسیدھے...` look like two buttons placed side by side, so I do not count them as text errors.

## Overall impression

The Urdu is mostly fluent, warm and readable. Phrases like «جو دل کو بھائے، وہ چنیں», «کھنچے چلے آتے ہیں», «نخلستان آپ کا منتظر ہے» and the four-wheel-drive note sound like a native wrote them. Gender agreement is correct throughout (جھیل، مسجد، بندرگاہ، پارک, the verb endings), and I saw no Arabic letterforms or misspellings.

The main weakness is orientation rather than language:
- The destination's name is missing from the opening.
- «ہفوف», «المبرز», «عیونی» and «قومی پارک» appear with no introduction.
- One route heading promises something its stops do not show.

The few translated-sounding spots are «آبی اجسام», «تفریحی سفر کیے جا سکتے ہیں» and «وقت کے تراشے ہوئے». The repeated descriptions in chapter 05 make the page feel padded on a second read.

**Rating: 84 / 100** for naturalness and clarity.
