1. Name "布勒布勒" (items 1, 2, 3, 12, 14, 16): I take it as a transliterated name, "Bule-bule", pronounced bù lè bù lè. On first pass it reads as a nonsense reduplicated name, not a bird; nothing in the Chinese says it is a bulbul (the 小鸟 picture and the 🐦 emoji are the only clue). Chinese readers know bulbul as 鹎 or 白耳鹎, and neither appears. "布勒" could also look like a transliterated person's name. Missing: a short line saying who or what 布勒布勒 is, for example "布勒布勒（一只小鸟）". I am not suggesting a new name.

2. "问布勒布勒" (item 1, button text): I read it as "Ask Bule-bule", which works as a verb plus name. But it sits on the page next to a place name I may not know, and without the bird picture I would not know it is a chat. It is fine as a button, only the name problem above remains.

3. "布勒布勒，Visit Al-Ahsa 顾问" (item 2): a Chinese name, a comma, then an English brand, then 顾问. The mix of scripts and the comma make it read like a list, not a title. Also "Visit Al-Ahsa" is not the Chinese name 哈萨 used in the rest of the text, so I briefly wondered whether 哈萨 and Al-Ahsa are the same place. Suggestion: 布勒布勒，哈萨旅行顾问. Not sure the site wants the English name kept.

4. "哈萨" (items 3, 6): I read it as Hasa, and a mainland reader will likely think of 哈萨克 (Kazakhstan) first. The first mention in the panel has no clarification that it is Al-Ahsa in Saudi Arabia. Missing: Saudi Arabia / 沙特 is never named anywhere in these texts, so a reader who has never heard of the place cannot place it. Also item 2 uses "Al-Ahsa" while the rest use 哈萨; inconsistent.

5. "热烈欢迎！🐦🌴" (item 3): "热烈欢迎" is the register of a banner at a conference or an official reception, loud for a chat bubble. Two emoji plus an exclamation mark is shouty next to the polite 您 used later. A natural alternative: 欢迎来到哈萨！

6. "哈萨的每一棵椰枣树我都认得！" (item 3): grammatical but exaggerated, and a bit odd. "认得" for trees is cute, but 椰枣树 is a plant a Chinese reader may not know well; 椰枣 is the standard term, though, so fine. The boast contrasts with the later disclaimer "回答仅来自本站内容", which I find slightly contradictory (a bird that knows every tree, yet answers only from site content).

7. "您是随意逛逛，还是想找只有当地人才知道的去处？" (item 3): natural Chinese, good. One stumble: "您是……还是……" followed by "说说您的喜好" feels like two questions; I was not sure which one to answer. Fine overall.

8. "我来为您安排一次合心意的出游" (item 3): slightly stiff and promising. "安排" suggests it will book or plan a trip for me; I am unsure whether it only recommends. "合心意" is a little old-fashioned; "称心" or "合您心意的行程" would be more usual. Missing: what it actually can do (recommend places only? make an itinerary?).

9. Switch of 您 and no 您 elsewhere: items 3 and 15 use 您, item 16 and 14 omit it; fine, polite register is consistent enough. Item 3 first line "热烈欢迎" is the only loud one.

10. Button 1 "一天时间，我该去哪些地方？" (item 4): natural enough, I might type "只有一天，哈萨有哪些地方值得去？". "一天时间" is a slightly translated shape (one day's time); "只有一天" or "一天的话" is more native. "我该去" is fine. Acceptable as a typed question, but a little stilted.

11. Button 2 "卡拉山的开放时间和费用是多少？" (item 5): reads naturally as a question. But "卡拉山" misread on first pass: I read it as a plain mountain name, a 山 called Kala, and could not tell it is a tourist site or cave attraction. Nothing in the texts says what it is. If it is the Qarah mountain, I do not know it. Also "卡拉" alone invokes 卡拉OK. Missing: a short descriptor such as (景点/洞穴).

12. Button 3 "在哪里可以尝到哈萨美食？" (item 6): natural, I would type it. "尝到" is good. Maybe "哈萨有什么特色美食？" is how I would actually ask, but this is fine.

13. Hint "请输入您的问题……" (item 7): bureaucratic, like a form field. Chatty apps usually say 说点什么吧 or 有什么想问的？. Also the ellipsis is the Chinese 6-dot "……", correct. Acceptable but a little stiff.

14. Note "回答仅来自本站内容。提问通过 Anthropic 的服务处理，不会被保存。" (item 8): two stumbles. (a) "Anthropic" is not explained; a mainland reader will not know it, and may wonder whether the data goes abroad. "不会被保存" is a strong promise; I read it twice to see if it means my question or the answer. (b) "回答仅来自本站内容" is a little abrupt and legal; it also means the helper cannot answer anything else, which conflicts with the opening promise of "只有当地人才知道的去处" (local-only secrets). Suggestion: 回答仅依据本站内容。你的提问由 Anthropic 的服务处理，不会保存。 (not sure about the wording of the privacy claim).

15. "发送", "关闭" (items 9, 10): fine, standard.

16. "新对话" (item 11): the button clears the chat, but "新对话" says new conversation, not delete; I would guess it starts over. Fine. Screen reader only, so minor.

17. "布勒布勒正在输入……" (item 12): natural, copies the chat-app idiom. Fine.

18. "相关页面" (item 13): fine, though on a travel site 相关景点 or 相关页面 is about equal. No problem.

19. "布勒布勒正在休息。请浏览景点页面，或前往“行程规划”页面。" (item 14): cute and clear, tone is warm but slightly twee for an error. Two stumbles: the quote marks around 行程规划 imply a page title, and I cannot tell where that page is (menu? button?). "景点页面" assumes I know where those are. Missing: no link or hint. Also "休息" for a service outage may read as the bot is simply gone for the day, not that it will return; unclear if I should retry later (contrast item 16 which says 稍后再试).

20. "您已达到今日提问上限。明天见，景点页面随时可浏览。" (item 15): first sentence is bureaucratic ("已达到……上限"), second is casual ("明天见"); the register jumps. "随时可浏览" is stiff next to "明天见". Also the number of questions is not stated. Suggestion: 今天的提问次数已用完，明天再来找我吧。景点页面随时可以看。

21. "暂时无法连接布勒布勒，请稍后再试。" (item 16): clear and natural. But it treats the bird like a server and sounds technical next to item 14; 14 and 16 differ in tone, and I cannot tell the difference between "resting" and "cannot connect" as a reader.

22. "您" (item 17, reserved label for my own messages): fine, but a bare 您 as a speaker label would be odd; 我 or 你 is usual for your own side. Not shown today, so low priority.

23. Overall: the voice is inconsistent. Item 3 is excited and boastful, item 8 and 13 are formal, item 15 mixes both. 您 is used throughout, which is polite but a bit distant for a friendly bird; fine, though.

24. Missing: nothing tells me this helper works in Chinese (it does by default, but I do not know if I can ask in English) and nothing says what 哈萨 is or where it is.
