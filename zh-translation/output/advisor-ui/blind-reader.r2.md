1. Text 1 "问布勒布勒" (floating button): I read 布勒布勒 as a nonsense or sound-word, like 布鲁布鲁. I could not tell it is a name, or a bird, or that it is a helper. Only the small bird picture hints at it. Because the button sits on every page, the first impression is unclear. Rating: medium. Suggestion: add a role word, e.g. "问布勒布勒 · 旅行顾问" (only if that is what it is).

2. Text 2 "布勒布勒，Visit Al-Ahsa 顾问": the comma between name and brand reads like a list or an address ("Bulebule, Visit Al-Ahsa advisor"), so I read it twice. The Latin brand name in the middle of Chinese is awkward. Everywhere else the place is 哈萨, so "Al-Ahsa" here is a second name for the same place and I had to connect them. Rating: medium. Suggestion: "布勒布勒 · 哈萨旅行顾问" (if the brand must stay Latin, use a separator rather than a comma).

3. Text 2 / 3 / 6 / 8: "哈萨" as the place name. For a mainland reader 哈萨 on first sight reads as an abbreviation of 哈萨克 (Kazakhstan). Nothing in the helper's texts says where it is (Saudi Arabia). That is missing, not for me to add, but it makes "哈萨美食" and "哈萨的每一棵椰枣树" ambiguous on first pass. Rating: medium.

4. Text 3 line 1 "欢迎欢迎！🐦🌴": the doubled 欢迎欢迎 is casual and chatty, which is fine for a mascot, but then line 3 switches to polished 您 and 合心意的出游. The tone drifts between chatty and service-desk. The 🌴 is a coconut-palm emoji while line 2 says 椰枣树 (date palms), a small mismatch. Rating: minor.

5. Text 3 line 2 "我是布勒布勒，哈萨的每一棵椰枣树我都认得！": the line never says what 布勒布勒 is, only the 🐦 emoji does. The boast is cute but a bit shouty with "!" after the doubled welcome. Reads fine otherwise. Rating: minor.

6. Text 3 line 3 "说说您的喜好，我来为您安排一次合心意的出游。": slightly translated-sounding ("arrange a satisfying outing for you"); a Chinese writer would more likely say 帮您规划一趟合心意的行程. Also it promises to arrange (安排) a trip, which a chat helper that answers only from site content may not do. It is unclear what I would actually get. Rating: minor to medium.

7. Text 3 line 3 "只有当地人才知道的去处": natural, fine. "随意逛逛" is also natural. No issue.

8. Text 4 "一天的话，我该去哪些地方？": reads like something I would type, spoken and natural. The "的话" opener is colloquial and good. Rating: none (natural). Tiny note: it does not say a day where, but the context supplies it.

9. Text 5 "卡拉山的开放时间和费用是多少？": the question form is natural, but 卡拉山 is an unknown mountain to me. It reads like a generic place (Kara mountain) or even makes me think of 卡拉OK; I would not type it myself because I would not know it. A first-time visitor offered this button would stumble on "what is 卡拉山?" and the answer risk is that I would not recognise it as an attraction. Rating: medium (button is a natural sentence, but not one I would choose).

10. Text 6 "在哪里可以尝到哈萨美食？": natural; I would type something like this. Minor issue only from 哈萨 (see item 3).

11. Text 7 "请输入您的问题……": normal Chinese UI wording. The 请 and 您 are a little formal next to the chatty bird, but they are standard for an input box. Rating: minor / fine.

12. Text 8 "回答仅来自本站内容。提问通过 Anthropic 的服务处理，不会被保存。": two issues. (a) "Anthropic" means nothing to a casual reader and is unexplained; I read it as a company name and wondered whether it is a third-party site. (b) "不会被保存" has no subject: saved by whom, the site or Anthropic? That is a trust-relevant sentence and it is left hanging. Also "通过……的服务处理" is stiff, translated-sounding. Also "回答仅来自本站内容" sits oddly with a mascot who says it knows every tree (mild tension). Rating: medium. Suggestion: "回答只依据本站内容。您的提问由 Anthropic 提供的服务处理，不会保存。" (the wording of who does not save must be set by the owner; I cannot add it).

13. Text 9 "发送": fine.

14. Text 10 "关闭": fine.

15. Text 11 "新对话": as the name of a button that clears the chat, I read it as "start a new conversation", which is what it does, but it does not tell me the current one disappears. Standard UI wording, acceptable. Rating: minor.

16. Text 12 "布勒布勒正在输入……": natural chat wording. Fine. (Here the name is clearly a person, which helps item 1.)

17. Text 13 "相关页面": fine, standard.

18. Text 14 "布勒布勒正在休息。请浏览景点页面，或前往“行程规划”页面。": the cute "resting" is nice but it hides that the service is down, so I may wait for the bird to come back. It then hands me two pages without saying where they are (a menu? a link?), so I do not know how to get there; the text is not a link as far as the file shows. "前往……页面" is stiff next to the cute first sentence. Also “景点页面” (attractions pages) is generic, plural, unclear which. Rating: medium. If those words are links, say so; if not, this is left hanging.

19. Text 15 "今天的提问次数已用完。明天见，景点页面随时可以浏览。": clear. "明天见" is warm and fine; "景点页面随时可以浏览" is slightly flat and repeats the idea of item 18. Does not say how many questions or when it resets (missing, not mine to add). Rating: minor.

20. Text 16 "暂时无法连接布勒布勒，请稍后再试。": a standard error line, but the register is bureaucratic next to the mascot-voice of item 18, and "连接布勒布勒" (connect to Bulebule) is odd, since the name is a character, not a server. Two different voices for two error states. Rating: minor to medium. Suggestion: "暂时连不上，请稍后再试。"

21. Text 17 "您": reserved label, not shown. As a label for my own message, 您 is unusual; a chat UI would show "我" or "你". Not visible today, so rating: minor.
