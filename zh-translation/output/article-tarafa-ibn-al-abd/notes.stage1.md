# Stage 1 notes: tarafa-ibn-al-abd (zh)

## Decisions taken
- Register: sober cultural-history prose; every report keeps its narrator (按……的记述 / ……记载 / 在……笔下). No CE years, no added identifications.
- al-Bahrayn: `巴林（此处指历史上的东部沿海地区）` in `description` and at the first body mention (intro, paragraph 2); bare `巴林` afterwards. FAQ answers 1, 2 and 3 explain the region themselves, so no gloss there. Never `巴林王国`.
- governor = `总督` everywhere; chief town (قاعدة) = `首府`; principal town (قصبة) = `中心城镇`; "the qasaba of Hajar" kept as `“哈杰尔的 qasaba”，也就是哈杰尔的中心城镇`.
- Quoted fragments that recur (Ibn Sallam's and Ibn Qutaybah's verdicts, "the 20-year-old", "who was at Hajar") use one wording in body, summary list and source list.
- Yaqut: `雅库特·哈马维` at first body mention and in FAQ / source-list lines, `雅库特` elsewhere.
- Hijri death dates: `卒于伊斯兰历 232 年` at the first mention on the page, `卒于伊历 …… 年` afterwards, source list included.
- Book titles: Latin transliterated titles in the source list; `《地名辞典》（Mu'jam al-Buldan）` in the body; `《文学宝库》（Khizanat al-Adab）` glossed in FAQ 3 because the body never names that book.
- Link text for the 25 source lines: `沙米拉文库`, glossed once in the lead-in line.
- Latin glosses: given at first body mention for the main persons, authors, tribes and rare terms. Not given inside the lineage chain, inside Abu Ubayda's quoted list of ten places, or inside any quoted sentence (the Ibn Yamin gloss sits in the verse translation instead).
- Verse translations are plain-prose renderings in italics, checked against the Arabic.
- `Syria` (الشام) rendered `黎凡特` as the brief instructs.
- Crafts cross-reference worded on the published Chinese title: `哈萨活着的手艺`.
- `open country` (بادية) = `旷野`, avoiding 游牧 / 贝都因 per the termbase note on badiyah.

## Doubts for stage 4 / the owner
1. **Nothing was web-verified at stage 1.** All 61 additions are tagged `[house-derived]`, including the forms I believe are established in Chinese scholarship (塔拉法, 悬诗, 蒙昧时期, 乌姆鲁勒·盖斯, 莱比德, 伊本·古太白, 阿慕尔·本·杏德, 希拉, 《地名辞典》). Stage 4 must confirm them.
2. **蒙昧时期**: the Mushaqar-fortress termbase note says the site uses no 蒙昧时期 / 贾希利叶. Here the source names the term and then explains it, so I used `蒙昧时期（Jahili）` + `也就是伊斯兰教兴起之前的诗人`, and the tag `蒙昧时期诗歌`. Owner to confirm, or fall back to `伊斯兰教兴起前的诗歌`.
3. **希拉 collision**: `希拉（al-Hira）` vs the existing `希拉（Al-Hillah）` (old Hofuf quarter). Different pages, both glossed.
4. **阿拉** for al-Ara reads like the common Chinese rendering of Allah; it appears once, inside the list of ten place names.
5. **阿布· vs 艾布·**: I followed the house pattern (阿布·努乌什) for Abu Ja'far and Abu Ubayda; scholarship normally writes 艾布·.
6. **穆塔莱米斯 / 塔米姆 / 栽德**: table transliterations; scholarship may prefer 穆太莱米斯, 泰米姆, and the termbase has 宰德 in a shop name.
7. **黎凡特 for "Syria"**: follows the brief; raise with the owner whether الشام in a pre-Islamic narrative should rather be 沙姆 or 叙利亚.
8. **印度海** (Sea of India) is literal on purpose; a reader may take it for a slip for 印度洋.
9. **"before its conquest"** rendered `被征服之前` with no agent named, as in the source.
10. **"held himself above killing him"** (تكرّم عن قتله) rendered `总督自持身份，不肯杀他`; stage 2/3 may refine.
11. Tarafa's alternative name Amr and King Amr ibn Hind both read 阿慕尔, exactly as in the source.
