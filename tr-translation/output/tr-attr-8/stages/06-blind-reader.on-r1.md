## Page 1 - Kanzan Macera Parkı (Eş-Şube)

1. Title and heading: "Kanzan Macera Parkı (Eş-Şube)". First pass: "şube" is "branch (office)" in Turkish and "eş" is "spouse/partner", so "(Eş-Şube)" in brackets read like a branch designation of a company, not a place name. I only understood it as a mountain name two lines later ("Cebel eş-Şube"). No fix without adding information, but the reader needs to know early that it is a place name.
2. "El-Ahsa Belediyesinin Eş-Şube/Kanzan dağ kütlesinde inşa etmekte olduğu dağ parkı; dağ kaydırakları, asma köprüler ve şelaleler." The long relative clause ("... inşa etmekte olduğu") sits in front of "dağ parkı", so I lost the thread until the noun. "Belediyesinin" has no apostrophe ("Belediyesi'nin"). The slash in "Eş-Şube/Kanzan": I could not tell whether these are two names for one thing or two places. Possible: "El-Ahsa Belediyesi'nin, Eş-Şube (Kanzan) dağında inşa ettiği dağ parkı: ..."
3. "dağ kaydırakları" (lead) and "dağ kaydırağı alanları" (body). I took it as slides down a mountainside. I could not tell whether it means slides, a mountain coaster or something else. The phrase "dağ kaydırağı alanları" (areas of mountain slides) is odd Turkish.
4. "Vahanın kuzeydoğusunda Cebel eş-Şube kütlesi uzanır; tarihî adı Kanzan'dır." "Vaha" is not named, so I had to lean on the lead for El-Ahsa. "Cebel" is not a word I know; I guessed "mountain" from "kütle". The lead says "Eş-Şube dağ kütlesi" and here it is "Cebel eş-Şube", so I wondered if these are different. "Tarihî adı" has no clear subject: the massif's name? The park's?
5. "Projenin resmî adı Cebel eş-Şube Parkı'dır." This contradicts the page title: if the official name is "Cebel eş-Şube Parkı", then who calls it "Kanzan Macera Parkı", and why is that the heading? It is left hanging.
6. "Parkın üst bölümü 2022'nin sonlarında kısmen hizmete girdi." "Üst bölüm" appears here for the first time: upper part of what? "Kısmen" is unclear (which parts?). The date is four years old, so I cannot tell whether this section is open now.
7. "yaklaşık 1.100 metre uzunluğunda yapay şelaleler". A plural noun with one length. Is it 1.100 m in total or each one? "1.100" parsed as 1100 in the end, but I paused on it. "Yapay şelaleler" is also missing from the lead, which just says "şelaleler".
8. "zipline hattı". An unadapted English word in the middle of a Turkish list; it sounds foreign. Possibly "zip-line" or a Turkish description such as "kayar halat hattı".
9. "Proje kapsamında ayrıca üç istasyonlu, 1.500 metrelik bir teleferik ile iki göl duyuruldu." "Duyuruldu" is a passive with no agent and no date. "İki göl duyuruldu" (lakes were announced) is an odd collocation. I could not tell whether they exist, are being built or are only plans.
10. "Projenin kalan inşaat aşamaları sürüyor; parkın tamamen hizmete girmesi henüz teyit edilmedi." The second half is vague: not confirmed that it has fully opened, or no confirmed date? "Kalan ... sürüyor" is clumsy. The reader is still not told which of the listed things (teleferik, lakes) can be used now.
11. Visit card, "Ziyaret saatleri: Her gün 16.00–24.00". I cannot tell if this applies to the whole park or only the partly opened upper section, and I cannot tell whether it is open right now. It is also not stated whether "24.00" means midnight closing.
12. "Ücretler: Giriş ücretsiz". Only entry is mentioned. Whether the zipline, the cable car or other facilities cost money is not said. Missing, not filled in here.
13. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir — ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin." "İşletmeci" has not appeared before; the text names the municipality as builder. Who is the operator, and how do I reach them? No contact is given. The word is also bureaucratic in register.
14. "[button] Benden ne kadar uzak?" The register is colloquial and chatty, next to the polite imperatives "kaydedin" and "Paylaşın". It is also unclear from where and by what means the distance is measured.
15. Category "Doğa" (Nature). The text describes artificial waterfalls, zipline, cable car and playgrounds, so the label felt a little off for what reads as an amusement/adventure park.

## Page 2 - El-Musa Parkı

1. Breadcrumb "Çarşılar ve parklar" and the back link "Tüm çarşılar ve parklar →" next to the category label "Parklar ve bahçeler". A children's park filed under "Çarşılar" (markets/bazaars) felt odd, and the two labels do not match.
2. Lead, "Müberrez'in El-Faysal mahallesinde ...". "Müberrez" was not recognisable as a place name at first (it resembles an ordinary adjective), and nothing says it is a town in El-Ahsa. Only the word "mahallesinde" shows that El-Faysal is a neighbourhood.
3. "kapsayıcı oyun ekipmanları" (lead). "Kapsayıcı" is a calque of "inclusive"; on first read it can mean "containing/covering". I understood it only from the rest of the sentence ("engelli çocukları akranlarıyla buluşturuyor").
4. "engelli çocukları akranlarıyla buluşturuyor" and "engelli çocukların ve akranlarının". "Akran" means same-age peers; I could not tell if they are other disabled children or children without disabilities. The reader has to guess.
5. "Park, 2019'un kasım ayı sonlarında “Birlikte Oynuyoruz” (Nalaab Ma'an) girişiminin ikinci parkı olarak açıldı." The date phrase is heavy ("2019'un kasım ayı sonlarında"). "(Nalaab Ma'an)" is Arabic in transliteration with no Turkish reading; it is a bare foreign string in the sentence. "İkinci parkı" raises "which was the first?", which is only answered at the very end.
6. "Girişim, El-Muheydib Toplum Hizmeti ile El-Musa Uzmanlık Hastanesi arasında toplumsal bir ortaklıktır." "El-Muheydib Toplum Hizmeti" does not say what kind of body it is (it reads like a service or a place, not an organisation), and "El-Muheydib" could be a person's name. "Toplum ... toplumsal" repeats. Hospital and organisation: the connection to the park is not stated beyond "ortaklık".
7. "Park, o dönemde Suudi Arabistan'da nadir görülen basit bir fikirden doğdu: engelli çocukların ve akranlarının yan yana oynayabilmesi için özel olarak tasarlanmış oyun ekipmanları." After the colon I expected an idea (a clause), but got a noun phrase (equipment), so the sentence lacks a verb after the colon. "O dönemde" is vague (2019?). "Nadir görülen basit bir fikir" is awkward. Possible: "... fikirden doğdu: engelli çocukların ve akranlarının yan yana oynayabileceği, bu amaçla tasarlanmış oyun ekipmanları."
8. "Böylece kapsayıcılık, çocuklara öğretilen bir şey olmaktan çıkıp onların yaşadığı bir şeye dönüşür." The register jumps to a slogan or editorial tone, in the present tense, after past-tense facts. "Böylece" does not clearly connect to the colon sentence before it. "Kapsayıcılık" is abstract.
9. "on ikiden fazla özel kapsayıcı oyun ünitesi barındırır". "Özel" is read first as "private", and "özel kapsayıcı" is two stacked adjectives. "Yayılır" (spreads over) is odd for a park's area. The page never says what any of these units are; missing, not filled in.
10. "Girişimin ilk parkı ise bundan önce El-Huber'de açılmıştı." "İse" contrasts with nothing, and the sentence comes last, after the second park. "Bundan önce" is redundant with "ilk". "El-Huber" read to me like a personal or foreign name, and nothing says it is a city or whether it is in El-Ahsa. No date is given.

The visit card on this page (24 saat açık, Giriş ücretsiz) reads cleanly, and I understood the park is open at any time.
