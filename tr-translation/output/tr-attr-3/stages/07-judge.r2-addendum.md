## The deciding question

Did the reader stumble on any of the three round-2 edits themselves? No.

- **jalaniyah, «Cami, kıble duvarındaki iki mihrabıyla … dikkat çeker».** The reader made no remark on the
  sentence. The round-2 stumble (the roof read as the subject) is gone.
- **dibs, «Bunlar bir zamanlar caminin yanındaki sokağı doldururdu».** One remark (p2#5). The reader did not
  lose the thread (the round-1/round-2 complaint about the long participial chain is not repeated) and did not
  misread: he asks whether «Bunlar» is the stores or the trade, finds «ticaret sokağı doldururdu» odd and
  «depolar … doldururdu» more natural, then concludes the name himself («adı ben çıkardım»). The oddity is the
  English's own figure: «the date stores and dibs (date molasses) trade that once filled the street beside it»
  attaches one relative clause to both nouns (the Arabic has the dibs «flowing in its street», no «trade»; this
  is already source_defect no. 1 on `dibs.body`). «Bunlar» takes up both nouns exactly as the English clause
  does. A fix would pick one noun or restate the nouns, which is a choice the source does not make. No edit.
- **qubba-mosque, «Camiden ayrı duran, silindir biçimli bir minare ise …».** One remark on the sentence (p3#6),
  and it is not about «Camiden ayrı duran»: the reader quotes that phrase without complaint (the referent is
  settled) and objects to «taş sarmal bir merdivenin çevresinde yükselir» (he pictured the stair outside and the
  minaret standing around it). «çevresinde» is the English «around»; the Arabic says the minaret is
  «with»/«adjoining» a stone spiral stair (source_defect already logged, `qubba-mosque.body`). Not caused by
  the round-2 edit. No edit.
