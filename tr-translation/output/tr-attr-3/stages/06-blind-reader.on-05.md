## Page 1 - El-Calaniye Camii

1. "Uyuniler dönemine ait bir cami" (description and subtitle). On first pass "Uyuniler" read like the Bolivian salt flat "Uyuni" with a plural. I could not place it as a dynasty until the body said "Uyuniler Devleti". Nothing in the first lines tells me it is a state or dynasty. Misread on first pass.
2. "El-Battaliye köyünde ... El-Battaliye Camii adıyla da bilinir." "Battaliye" reads as "battal" (oversized, Battal Gazi) plus a suffix. Also the page gives two names for the mosque and never says why; the same "also known as" sentence appears three times (description, subtitle, body), which feels padded.
3. "El-Battaliye köyü, Hüfuf'un yaklaşık on iki kilometre kuzeydoğusundadır." Hüfuf is never situated (what city, where), so the twelve kilometres give me nothing to hold. The "ü" spelling also made me hesitate (I know it as Hofuf/Hufuf). Left hanging.
4. "Köyün batı mahallesinde El-Calaniye Camii yükselir" — "yükselir" is brochure register for a village mosque; "bulunur" would be calmer. (The same verb recurs on pages 2-4.)
5. "cami, ... El-Ahsa'nın en ünlü tarihî camilerinden biridir" — "en ünlü" is a claim with nothing behind it on the page, and it sits in the same sentence as the name repetition, so the sentence is heavy.
6. "İnşası, Uyuniler Devleti dönemine, Hicri beşinci yüzyıla (yaklaşık on birinci yüzyıl) uzanır." Stumbles: "inşası ... dönemine ... yüzyıla uzanır" stacks two datives, then a parenthesis with no calendar named. I had to work out that the parenthesis is the Gregorian century. "Miladi on birinci yüzyıl" would have been clear.
7. "Adı, devletin kurucusunun kızı Prenses Hibe bint Abdullah bin Ali el-Uyuni ile ilişkilendirilir; cami de ona atfedilir." Three problems. (a) "Adı" — which name, Calaniye or Battaliye? (b) "Hibe" reads as the ordinary word "hibe" (grant), and the "bint ... bin ... el-" chain buries it. (c) "ilişkilendirilir; ... atfedilir" are two near-synonyms: did she build it, fund it, or just lend her name? Left hanging. Also the founder is not named, so I cannot tell whether the "Abdullah bin Ali" in her name is the founder.
8. "Kıble duvarındaki iki mihrabı ve sivri kemerleri taşıyan sütun sıralarıyla dikkat çeker" — "iki mihrabı" parsed as an accusative ("mihrabı") waiting for a verb; only at "sıralarıyla" did I see that it should be "iki mihrabıyla". Misread on first pass. Suggest "iki mihrabı ve sivri kemerleri taşıyan sütun sıraları dikkat çeker" or "iki mihrabıyla ... dikkat çeker".
9. "bin kişiden çok daha fazla cemaat alır" — "cemaat alır" sounds translated (a mosque takes in worshippers); "bin kişiden çok daha fazla" is vague as a figure. Is it 1,000+ or several thousand? The number is left hanging.
10. "Camiyi Cübeyl ve Yenbu Kraliyet Komisyonu restore etti" — one commission or two bodies? I read it as two on first pass. The proper name is also long enough that the verb arrives late.
11. "2017'de ise cami, El-Ahsa'nın resmen tescil edilmiş tarihî camileri arasına alındı." "ise" contrasts with nothing. "resmen tescil edilmiş ... arasına alındı" is bureaucratic. Page 5 calls the same thing "resmî kaydında yer alıyor", so the page set uses two different formulas for one fact.
12. Ziyaret bilgileri shows only "Bölge", which repeats the first sentence. There is no entry/hours line, so I cannot tell whether a visitor can go in. Missing; not filled in.
13. "Mekândan fotoğraflar" and "Gününüzü tamamlayın" — grammatical but calques ("photos from the venue", "complete your day"); no Turkish site would use them. "Mekân" is also a little bookish.
14. "Doğa Al-Shaban Mountain / Müzeler Al-Khalifa Heritage Museum" — the category label runs into an English name with no separator, so "Doğa Al-Shaban Mountain" reads as one phrase. English names mixed with "El-" transliterations elsewhere on the page ("El-Ahsa") look inconsistent. "Sahud Sarayı" is the only one in Turkish.

## Page 2 - Ed-Dibs Camii

1. "Ed-Dibs Camii adıyla da bilinen tarihî El-Fatih Camii, El-Kut mahallesinin en eski camilerinden biri; Osmanlı döneminde, Hicri 962 (1555) yılında inşa edildi." The page is headed Ed-Dibs but this sentence treats El-Fatih as the real name, so the stacked "adıyla da bilinen" opening made me reread. "El-Fatih" reads as the Istanbul Fatih. The same sentence is both description and subtitle, then repeated in the body.
2. "Ed-Dibs Camii, eski Hüfuf'un El-Kut mahallesindedir." "eski Hüfuf" — the old town or an earlier Hüfuf? Unclear. "Kut" reads as the plain word "kut".
3. "Doğusunda Kasr İbrahim yer alır; El-Cebri Camii'ne ve Beyt el-Biat'a kısa bir yürüyüş mesafesindedir." The subject changes between the two clauses (Kasr İbrahim is east of the mosque, then the mosque is a short walk to...). "El-Cebri Camii" is never introduced and is not in the nearby list. "Biat" reads as the Turkish word "biat" (allegiance), so "Beyt el-Biat" has no meaning for me.
4. "erken Osmanlı camilerinin tarzında yapılmıştır" — 1555 is the classical (Kanuni) period in Turkish usage; "erken Osmanlı" means the 14th-15th century. It contradicts the date given a few lines earlier.
5. "ortada büyük bir kubbe yükselir, çevresini ışık alan küçük kubbeler sarar" — "ışık alan küçük kubbeler" is ambiguous (domes that receive light, or let light in?).
6. "Cami uzun süre eğitim de veren bir camiydi; burada Kur'an, fıkıh ve hadis Şafii mezhebine göre okutulurdu." The Qur'an is not taught "according to a mezhep", so lumping it with fıkıh and hadis reads wrong. Why Şafii when the builder is Ottoman is not explained. "Uzun süre" has no end point; is it still a school? Left hanging.
7. "Doğudaki minaresi, El-Kut Caddesi açılırken Hicri 1391 (1971) yılında kaldırıldı." "Doğudaki minaresi" suggests there are several minarets; and "doğu" collides with the Kasr İbrahim to the east two sentences earlier. "Kaldırıldı" (removed? demolished?) and whether the mosque has any minaret today is left hanging.
8. "Hicri 1406 (1986) yılında bir bölümünün çökmesinin ardından, eski mimari tarzı korunarak onarıldı" — "bir bölümünün çökmesinin ardından" is a chain of genitives and a nominalisation before the verb; I lost the thread. A split into two clauses would read better.
9. "bugün de ibadete açık, yaşayan bir camidir" — "yaşayan cami" is a calque of "living mosque", and "bugün de ibadete açık" makes it redundant with the Giriş line.
10. "Giriş: İbadet için kullanılan bir cami; ücretsiz" — "İbadet için kullanılan bir cami" is a definition, not an entry condition. It does not tell me whether a visitor may enter, and when. No hours given; missing. (Pages 1, 3, 4, 5 have no Giriş line at all.)
11. "Parklar ve bahçeler Al-Koot Park" — English "Al-Koot" against "El-Kut" in the same page; and the category runs into the name.

## Page 3 - Ebu Bekir el-Mulla Ailesi Ribatı

1. Title and description: "Ribat" — I know ribat as a fortified frontier post, so "ribat" for a scholars' hostel made me stop; the description gives "ilmî bir vakıf" as a gloss, which is bookish ("ilmî", "ilim öğrencileri", "işlevini sürdürüyor"). Register is Ottoman-bureaucratic for a travel page.
2. Description: "Hicri 1280 yılında" has no Gregorian year, while the body says "(yaklaşık 1863)". The two are inconsistent in form, and a visitor only sees the Hijri year in search results.
3. "Er-Ruveyde semti, Hüfuf'taki El-Kut mahallesinin bir parçasıdır." In Turkish a "mahalle" is the smaller official unit and "semt" the looser larger one, so "semt a part of a mahalle" is reversed. "Ruveyde" is fine but the sentence made me rethink the hierarchy.
4. "Burada, asıl adıyla Ebu Bekir el-Mulla Ailesi Ribatı, yerel adıyla El-Bubekir Ribatı yükselir." The two names in commas pushed the subject and verb apart; I read "Burada ... Ribatı" as a list. "Bubekir" misread as "bu Bekir". The reason for the two names is not given.
5. "Ribat, Şeyh Abdullah bin Ebu Bekir el-Mulla'nın Hicri 1280 (yaklaşık 1863) yılında kurduğu ilmî bir vakıftır." The relative clause stacks a long name, a date and an apposition before the predicate; had to read twice. Compare the neighbour name "Muhammad bin Abi Bakr Al-Mulla" below: Ebu/Abi and Bekir/Bakr spellings do not match, so I cannot tell whether it is the same family.
6. "Kuşaklar boyunca El-Ahsa'dan ve çok daha uzak yerlerden gelen İslami ilimler öğrencilerine ve yolculara ücretsiz barınma, yiyecek ve içecek sağladı; kuşaklarca âlim ve kadı yetiştirdi." Long participial chain: "gelen İslami ilimler öğrencilerine" is a noun stack, and the verb arrives only at the end. "çok daha uzak" — farther than what? "Kuşaklar boyunca ... kuşaklarca" repeats. "Âlim ve kadı" is bookish.
7. "Alçı, çakıl, ahşap ve hurma ağacı gövdelerinden yapılmıştır" — after the long previous sentence the subject (the ribat) is too far away; it briefly read as being about the students or scholars.
8. "odalar, revaklarla çevrili açık bir avlunun etrafında iki kat halinde sıralanır" — fine in meaning, "iki kat halinde sıralanır" is a little stiff; "iki katlı odalar ... avluyu çevreler" would be more natural.
9. "Ribat, Hicri 1430 (yaklaşık 2009) yılında kapsamlı biçimde restore edildi ve eski tarzını korudu." "yaklaşık 2009" while page 1 states the same Hijri year as exactly "(2009)"; the "yaklaşık" is inconsistent. "ve eski tarzını korudu" has the ribat as subject of an action that was done to it.
10. "Bugün de El-Mulla ailesinin gözetiminde işlevini sürdürüyor" — what function? Does it still house students and travellers? Left hanging. "El-Mulla" capitalised here but "el-Mulla" in the title sentence.
11. "vahadaki ilmin derinliğine tanıklık ediyor" — brochure-poetic, preachy, and vague ("ilmin derinliği"). It says nothing about what I would see there. Suggest cutting.
12. Ziyaret bilgileri: only "Bölge"; no Giriş line. I cannot tell if the ribat can be visited at all, given that it is a family foundation in use. Missing; not filled in.
13. "Camiler ve dinî mekânlar Sheikh Muhammad bin Abi Bakr Al-Mulla Mosque" — an untranslated English name beside Turkish ones, and a long one where the category label runs into it.

## Page 4 - El-Kubbe Camii

1. Description: "kalenin en dikkat çekici mimari özelliklerinden biri" — then the body says "kalenin en dikkat çekici mimari unsuru". The phrase is repeated and the claim differs: "one of the most" vs "the most".  Also the Kasr (palace) is called "kale" with no explanation; the page jumps between the two words.
2. "Surlarının içinde yükselen El-Kubbe Camii" — "surlarının" refers back to Kasr İbrahim but the subject of the sentence is already the mosque; briefly read as the mosque's own walls.
3. "tarihî El-Ahsa'yı tanımlayan görüntülerden biridir" — calque ("one of the images that define"); no Turkish writer would say "görüntülerden biri" for a landmark. "simgelerinden biri" would work.
4. "Cami, Hicri 979 (1571) yılında, yaklaşık beş yıl süren inşaatın ardından kuruldu" — "kuruldu" for a mosque is odd (a mosque is "inşa edilir" or "tamamlanır"). Is 1571 the start or the end of the five years? The sentence says "ardından" (after), so end, but "kuruldu" makes it sound like the founding. Date unclear.
5. "İslami ilimler okulu ile Kur'an öğretimine ayrılmış bir yeri de kapsayan bir külliyenin parçasıydı." Had to read twice: "okulu ile ... bir yeri de kapsayan bir külliyenin" is a long chain before the predicate. "İslami ilimler okulu" is a foreign-sounding gloss for what a Turkish writer would call medrese. "ayrılmış bir yer" is vague. Whether the külliye still exists is left hanging ("parçasıydı").
6. "Camiyi, yaklaşık 13 metre çapında yarım küre biçimli bir merkez kubbe taçlandırır" — object-first order plus "taçlandırır" makes it brochure-bookish; "Caminin ... merkez kubbesi vardır" or "yarım küre biçimli merkez kubbenin çapı yaklaşık 13 metredir" would be plainer. "köşelerdeki küçük kubbeler" — how many corners, not said.
7. "Bağımsız, silindir biçimli bir minare ise taş sarmal bir merdivenin çevresinde 19 metre yükselir." "Bağımsız" reads as "independent" (political/administrative); I meant "ayrı duran / camiden ayrı" only after re-reading. "taş sarmal bir merdivenin çevresinde" — around a stair, from outside? The staircase being inside the minaret is not said. "ise" has nothing to contrast with.
8. "Girişinin üzerindeki alçı levhada kuruluş tarihi sülüs hattıyla yazılıdır." Whose entrance? The last subject was the minaret, so "Girişinin" first read as the minaret's door. "Kuruluş tarihi" again uses "kuruluş" for a mosque.
9. "Cami, vaha tarihinin bütün bir dönemine tanıklık eder." Vague and ceremonious: which period? Nothing on the page backs it up; it leaves me asking what I would see. Suggest cutting.
10. Ziyaret bilgileri: no Giriş line; since the mosque is inside Kasr İbrahim, I cannot tell whether it can be entered or whether there is a fee for the fortress. Missing; not filled in.
11. "Kasr İbrahim'in içi, El-Kut mahallesi — Hüfuf" — "içi" is a bit casual next to the formal rest of the page.
12. "Tarihî yerler Kasr İbrahim — 1 km'den az" — contradicts the body: the mosque is inside Kasr İbrahim, so Kasr İbrahim cannot be "a nearby place within 1 km" that I could add to my route. Contradiction.
13. "Parklar ve bahçeler Al-Koot Park" — English name with "Al-Koot" next to "El-Kut" in the body.

## Page 5 - Et-Tahimiye Batı Camii

1. "Et-Tahimiye, El-Ahsa'nın doğu köylerinden biridir ve Cebel el-Kare'ye yakındır." "Kare" reads as the ordinary word "kare" (square), so "Cebel el-Kare" parsed as "square mountain". The name is also unexplained. "Yakındır" gives no distance here (the list says about 1 km).
2. "Bu cami, köyün geriye kalan eski çamur camilerinden biridir" — also repeats the description. "geriye kalan" is fine, but the sentence then runs on after a semicolon into a second and third fact.
3. "köy eskiden “kırk caminin köyü” lakabıyla tanınırdı" — "lakap" is a nickname for a person; for a village "kırk caminin köyü olarak anılırdı" would be natural. "eskiden ... tanınırdı, bugün ..." — the comma/semicolon chain reads as one very long sentence.
4. "bugün bu camilerden yalnızca yaklaşık on bir tanesi ayakta" — "yalnızca yaklaşık" is an awkward pair (only roughly); "on bir kadarı" or "yaklaşık on biri" is better. "Kırk" is probably a round number, so "yaklaşık on bir" against "kırk" invites a "which count is right?" question; the page does not say whether kırk is literal.
5. "Et-Tahimiye'nin camileri, çamur ve hurma ağacı gövdelerinden yapılmış geleneksel yapılardır." Which mosques: the forty, the eleven? Ambiguous. Also "hurma ağacı gövdeleri" as in the earlier pages; fine.
6. "Bunlardan ikisi, Doğu Camii ile Batı Camii, El-Ahsa'daki tarihî camilerin resmî kaydında yer alıyor." "Bunlardan" — from the eleven or the forty? "Resmî kayıt" vs "resmen tescil edilmiş" on page 1; and present tense "yer alıyor" while the rest of the page uses the aorist. Also, I cannot tell which of the two this page is about without matching "Batı" with the title; the sentence only lists both.
7. The page has nothing specific about this mosque: no date, no size, no features, nothing I could see or do there. The text is about the village and its mosques in general. Missing; not filled in. For a page whose title is a single mosque, that is the main gap.
8. Ziyaret bilgileri: only "Bölge"; no Giriş line (same gap as on the other pages). "Et-Tahimiye köyü — El-Ahsa'nın doğu köyleri" just repeats sentence one.
9. "Camiler ve dinî mekânlar Al-Tahimiyah East Mosque" — English spelling "Al-Tahimiyah" against "Et-Tahimiye" in the body; the page calls it "Doğu Camii" in the text, so I would not match them. "Dar Al-Turath (Heritage House)" is also untranslated, and it is labelled "Müzeler".
10. "1 km'den az" and "yaklaşık 1 km" are both used for the neighbours in the same list; I could not tell whether Cebel el-Kare and Dar Al-Turath are closer or farther than the East Mosque.
