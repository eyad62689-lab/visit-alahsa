# Blind reader notes (Turkish reader), on-05

## Page 1 - /tr/events/ (Festivaller ve etkinlikler)

1. "El-Kaysariye Çarşısı'ndan hurmalıklara kadar El-Ahsa, mirasını yıl boyu yeniden canlandıran mevsimlerle hareketlenir." Stumbled. The participial chain "mirasını yıl boyu yeniden canlandıran mevsimlerle" held the verb back, and "seasons that revive heritage" is an odd idea (seasons do not revive anything; events do). Also "hareketlenir" is a small step down in register after that. No wording to offer without changing the meaning.
2. "Kutlamalar ve festivaller her yıl kendi mevsiminde geri döner." followed by "Bu etkinlikler her yıl yaklaşık aynı mevsimlerde yinelenir." The same claim is made twice in three lines. I read the second as a repeat, not new information.
3. "Resmî tarihler ve ayrıntılar her dönemde yıldan yıla değişebilir." "her dönemde yıldan yıla" is a double time phrase; I read it twice. "Her dönem" is also unclear (each season? each edition?). Later pages use "yıldan yıla değişebilir" alone, which reads fine.
4. "Kartlar, cihazınızın tarihine en yakın etkinlikten başlayarak otomatik olarak sıralanır." "Cihazınızın tarihi" sounds technical and bureaucratic. "En yakın" is unclear: nearest in the past or the future? It also contradicts what I see: the cards run İşlenmiş Hurma (ocak–şubat), Kaysariye, Yaratıcı, Lomi (yaz), Hurma Köyü (kasım 2026) and so on in calendar order, not "nearest first", and the first card is not one I can tell is "şu an mevsiminde". The page copy I was given may not show the sorted state, but as written the claim and the list do not match.
5. "Şu an mevsiminde olan etkinlik varsa en başta gelir." "Mevsiminde olmak" is said of fruit and vegetables. For an event it sounds foreign. The badge text "Şu an mevsiminde" has the same problem.
6. "Kış Beklenen sezon" / "Ocak–şubat Birkaç hafta" (every card). The labels are glued to the values with no separator. I first read "Kış Beklenen sezon" as one phrase. "Beklenen sezon" ("expected season") is also strange as a label: the value after it is sometimes a season (Kış), sometimes a month range, sometimes "Mübarek ramazan ayı". "Birkaç hafta" has no label at all on the cards.
7. "El-Ahsa Belediyesi Kalesi, Hüfuf" (İşlenmiş Hurma Festivali, also Yaratıcı El-Ahsa). "Belediyesi Kalesi" stacks two genitive-type nouns. I could not tell whether this is a fort named after the municipality, a fort owned by it, or a venue name. Unclear what I would look for on a map.
8. "Miras Füreyci (mahalle), El-Ahsa Belediyesi Kalesi". Two problems. "Miras Füreyci" parses as "Heritage Füreyci": I cannot tell if "Miras" is part of the name. The comma list makes me wonder whether these are two venues or one. The parenthetical "(mahalle)" after the name is clumsy.
9. "El-Ahsa Lomi Sergisi" (first appearance, card and table). "Lomi" means nothing to a Turkish reader here and is not explained on this page. Only page 7 says "misket limonu (lomi)". A reader of the listing page alone has no idea what the exhibition is about except from "misket limonu" in the bullets.
10. "Kurutulmuş misket limonu ürün sergileri". "Ürün sergileri" is vague (exhibitions of what?). Then "Sergiye katılan kuruluşlardan misket limonu ürünleri, yiyecekler ve bakım ürünleri." is a sentence with no verb. It reads like a caption for the button that follows. "Bakım ürünleri" (care products) from limes is surprising and unexplained.
11. "[button] Sergi ürünlerinden alışveriş yapın — El-Ahsa Ticaret Odası mağazası (dış bağlantı) ↗". Long, with a dash and a bracketed "(dış bağlantı)" that reads like screen-reader text leaking into the visible label. "Sergi ürünlerinden" is unclear: products from this year's exhibition, or products of the exhibitors in general?
12. "Muheyris mahallesi, Müberrez". "Müberrez" looks like a misspelling of a Turkish-looking word and I did not know how to say it. I would not recognise it on a map. "Muheyris" is also hard to place.
13. "Kış Beklenen sezon / Kasım 2026 Süre henüz açıklanmadı" (Hurma Köyü). In Turkish, kasım is autumn. The page does not say the "winter" here is the local one, so "Kış · Kasım 2026" jarred. It is also the only card with a year, while the rest are "yaklaşık"; I could not tell if it is a one-off or yearly.
14. "El-Ahsa Belediyesinin denetimindeki özel işletmeciler". "Denetimindeki" suggests inspection/audit and sounds bureaucratic. Unclear what the municipality does here (supervises? licenses?).
15. "150'den fazla yemek aracı (food truck)". The Turkish term in front of the English one is not what people say, so I took it as a gloss for a word the translator was not sure of. "El-Ahsa halk yemekleri" is ambiguous: "halk yemekleri" can read as "public/people's meals". Probably "local dishes" is meant. "Açık havada kış buluşmaları" says nothing about what one does there.
16. "Yaklaşık kasım ayından itibaren / Uzun bir kış sezonu". No end is given and "uzun bir kış sezonu" is not a duration. Left hanging: how long?
17. "El-Ahsa Binicilik Arenası, Et-Taraf kasabası" (At Yarışı Sezonu). For horse racing a Turkish reader expects "hipodrom" or "yarış pisti". "Binicilik arenası" sounds like a riding ring for shows, so the match with "At yarışları" is shaky. The venue and the organiser have the same name, which looks like a copy error. Also the season is "Kasım–şubat" here, but page 10 says the season started 31 Ekim: October is outside the stated range.
18. "Düzenleyicinin X hesabı". "X hesabı" reads as "an account called X". People still say "Twitter" in Turkish. No better wording.
19. "El-Ahsa Bişti Festivali" next to "Bişt sergisi", "Etkileşimli bişt çarşısı". First, "Bişti" reads as the Turkish verb form "it cooked" (pişti / bişti), not a name. Second, the name has "-i" but the bullets use bare "bişt": I could not tell whether it is one word or two. Third, "bişt" (a cloak) is never explained anywhere, so I do not know what I would see or buy. "Etkileşimli bişt çarşısı" ("interactive bisht market") is unclear.
20. "Miras Kurumu (Suudi Arabistan'ın Kültür Bakanlığı)". The parenthesis reads as if Miras Kurumu and the ministry are the same thing. Unclear what the relationship is.
21. "Ukayr Plajı Parkı" / "Ailelere yönelik deniz ve spor etkinlikleri" / "Eğlenceli oyunlar". "Plajı Parkı" is two nouns in a chain; is the park on the beach? "Deniz etkinlikleri" does not say what activities. "Eğlenceli oyunlar" is a filler phrase (all games are meant to be fun), and I could not tell what kind of games.
22. "Mübarek ramazan ayı Beklenen sezon / Ramazan boyunca Uzun ramazan geceleri" (Kafu Geceleri). "Mübarek ramazan ayı" in a season slot is not a season, and "mübarek" is a religious, slightly bookish register next to the neutral rest of the page. "Uzun ramazan geceleri" as a duration reads as "long Ramadan nights", not how long it lasts. Casing is mixed: "Ramazan boyunca" capital, "ramazan ayı" lowercase.
23. "Etkileşimli Kafu mutfağı" / "“Gabka” yemeği". "Etkileşimli ... mutfağı" is a calque; I could not picture it. "Gabka" is unexplained here and I would read it "gabka" with a hard g; page 4 gives some explanation but not what it is made of.
24. "Etkinlikler hem Hicri hem Miladi takvime göre belirlenir ve her yıl yaklaşık aynı mevsimlerde yinelenir." Contradiction. A Turkish reader knows Ramadan moves about eleven days earlier every year and passes through all seasons. The page itself gives Kafu no season ("Mübarek ramazan ayı"). So "her yıl yaklaşık aynı mevsimlerde" cannot be true for it. Also "belirlenir" is vague: which events follow which calendar?
25. "Festivaller, El-Ahsa'nın kimliğini ve mirasını halk giysileri, el sanatları ve özgün yerel ürünlerle öne çıkarır." Brochure tone ("özgün yerel ürünler"). It also does not fit every event listed (horse racing, food trucks, beach activities).
26. "Programlar bütün aileye, her yaştan kişiye uygundur. Eğlence, yaratıcılık, alışveriş ve tadım öne çıkar." Blanket claim with no basis on the page; "bütün aileye, her yaştan kişiye" says the same thing twice. Reads like a brochure.
27. "Mevsiminizi eksiksiz bir geziye dönüştürün". "Mevsiminizi" ("your season") sounds foreign; I did not know what it meant. "Eksiksiz" is advertising speak.
28. "Festivali vahada çevresindeki gezilecek yerlerle birleştirin ve rotanızı “Kayıtlı yerlerim” sayfasında hazırlayın." "Vahada çevresindeki" stumbled: I read "vahada" and "çevresindeki" as two locations. Which festival is "Festivali"? There are many. "Kayıtlı yerlerim" is unexplained: does it mean saved places?
29. "Yaklaşık aralık ayında", "Yaklaşık kasım ayından itibaren" (card values, table, and everywhere else). "Yaklaşık" in front of a month name is foreign. A Turkish writer would say "aralık ayı civarında" or "aralık ayında". The "-ında" after "yaklaşık" does not sound right.
30. "Halk sanatı gösterileri" (Kaysariye), "Folklor gösterileri" (Bişt), "Halk gösterileri" (Yaratıcı). Three different phrasings that look like the same thing. I could not tell whether they differ. "Toplum buluşmaları" is a calque ("community gatherings"); I do not know what happens there. "Geleneksel oyunlar" can mean games or plays/dances.
31. "El-Kaysariye". My first read was "Kayseri". Fine once "Çarşısı" arrives, but the first reading did go there.
32. "İşlenmiş Hurma Festivali". "İşlenmiş" ("processed" / "embroidered") sounds industrial or like needlework. A festival of "processed dates" does not tempt me. Not sure what the name means in practice.
33. Table at the bottom: "Mübarek ramazan ayı · Ramazan boyunca" says the same thing twice. Also the whole card list is repeated as a table, so the page feels long and doubled.
34. Search-engine description: "...ve daha fazlası; mevsimler, düzenleyenler." The ending is a loose fragment that adds nothing. Tab title "El-Ahsa festival ve etkinlikleri: yıl boyu vaha mevsimleri" reads a bit clumsy ("festival ve etkinlikleri" with a singular first noun).

The same date appears twice (top and "Zaman"), and "Beklenen sezon" vs "Mevsim" are two labels for one thing. "Sezon" and "mevsim" are used as if different. ## Page 4 - /tr/events/kafu-nights/ (Kafu Geceleri)

1. "Kafu Geceleri, her yıl ramazanda düzenlenen toplumsal bir festivaldir." "Toplumsal bir festival" is foreign ("societal festival"); it sounds sociological. Probably "community festival" is meant.
2. "Festivali Kral Faysal Üniversitesi El-Ahsa'da düzenler." Stumbled: "Üniversitesi El-Ahsa'da" can be read as a university "in El-Ahsa" or as "organises in El-Ahsa". Also "Festivali ... Üniversitesi ... düzenler" with no comma is a word-order stumble.
3. "Festivalin ilk dönemi 2024'te yapıldı." "İlk dönem" reads as "first term/semester", not first edition. Sounds foreign.
4. "“Kafu” adı, Arapçada “ödüllendirilmek” anlamına gelen bir fiilden gelir: ödül ve övgü kazanmak." I read this twice. The colon part repeats the idea ("ödüllendirilmek" then "ödül ve övgü kazanmak") and gives no new help. "Fiilden gelir" is stiff.
5. "Gecelerin öne çıkanlarından biri, “Gabka” denen yemektir." The page never says what Gabka is (a dish of what?). I asked myself what I would actually eat. Left hanging.
6. "Bu yemek, Arap Körfezi ülkelerinde mübarek ramazan ayının gecelerinde yenir; aynı zamanda halk arasında sevilen bir toplumsal gelenektir." The subject "Bu yemek" and the predicate "bir toplumsal gelenektir" do not agree in sense: a dish is not a tradition. "Mübarek ramazan ayının gecelerinde" is a long religious-bookish chain. "Arap Körfezi" is unusual to my ear; I am used to "Basra Körfezi" or "Körfez ülkeleri", and I paused. No change to suggest.
7. "Mübarek ramazan ayı / Ramazan boyunca / Uzun ramazan geceleri" in the facts: three versions of the same thing, and "Uzun ramazan geceleri" as a duration is odd. Casing of "ramazan" varies.
8. "Etkileşimli Kafu mutfağı": calque, unclear what one does.
9. "Diğer mevsimler" shows Kış events; for a Ramadan event that moves through the year, the "season" framing does not fit.

1. "El-Ahsa Lomi Sergisi, El-Ahsa Limon Sergisi ya da El-Ahsa Limon Festivali olarak da bilinir." The alternative names say "Limon" (lemon), but the rest says "misket limonu" (lime). A Turkish reader knows these are different fruits, so I paused. It is also the first time the page admits "lomi" is a thing, and no word for it is given before this.

4. "Ziyaretçiler misket limonunun özelliklerini, faydalarını ve yetiştirilişini etkileşimli bir deneyimle tanır, çiftçilerle doğrudan görüşür ve üretici ailelerin tezgâhlarından alışveriş yapar."
