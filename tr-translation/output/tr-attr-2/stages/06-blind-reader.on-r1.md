# Blind reader observations (Turkish reader), round 1

## Muheyris Sarayı

1. Lead: "Müberrez'in kenarında, ... küçük bir kale." The body says "Müberrez'in kuzeyinde". Edge vs north: slightly different positions, reads as a mild contradiction. Also I do not know what Müberrez is (town? district?). It is never glossed, and the word looks like a common adjective (mübarız/müberrez-like), so my first pass took it as a descriptive word, not a place name.

2. Title "Muheyris Sarayı" vs "küçük bir kale". "Saray" means palace; a small watchtower fort is not a palace. I stumbled: is it a palace or a fort? The same happens on Huzam ("Huzam Sarayı", then "bu kaleyi"). Elsewhere the text uses "Kasr İbrahim", so "Saray" vs "Kasr" naming is also inconsistent across pages.

3. "Çamur, kerpiç ve taştan yapılan" - kerpiç is already sun-dried mud brick, so "çamur ve kerpiç" reads as a doubling. I could not tell what the two materials are.

4. "çatısı ağaç gövdeleri ve hurma yapraklarıyla örtülüdür" - "ağaç gövdeleri" sounds foreign; a Turkish writer would say "hurma ağacı kütükleri" (palm trunks) or name the tree. Which trees is not stated.

5. "Planı kare, her kenarı yaklaşık yirmi metredir." Copula missing on the first clause; reads like a note, not travel prose. Comprehensible, but abrupt.

6. "Güneydoğu köşesinde ... bir kule yükselir; kalenin duvarları yükseldikçe içe doğru eğilir." "Yükselir" and "yükseldikçe" twice in one sentence; I read it twice. The meaning (walls slope inward, a batter) is clear only on the second reading.

7. "son döneminde erzak ve cephane de burada saklanıyordu." Whose "son dönem"? The fort's last period? The Saudi state's? Unclear which period. Also the sentence before is in simple past and this switches to imperfect; fine, but "son dönemi" has no anchor.

8. "El-Ahsa'nın festival dönemlerinde" - which festivals? It makes me ask what the park/area is like on a normal day. Missing: whether one can enter the fort itself or only the park around it. "Kale alanında" suggests only the grounds.

## Huzam Sarayı

9. "Adı, “zimam” sözcüğünden gelir" - Huzam and zimam look unrelated to a Turkish eye (the language of the word, Arabic, is not named, and the letter change h/z is invisible). First-pass: "where does Huzam come from zimam?" I would expect "Arapça".

10. "zimam, develeri yönlendirmeye yarayan yulardır. Bu ad, kalenin bölgeye hâkim olduğunu gösterir." The logic leap: why does a camel halter name show that the fort commands the region? Thread lost here; the link is not explained.

11. "Yaklaşık 70 × 80 metrelik dikdörtgen sur, altı kuleyle korunur" - OK in meaning, but "korunur" (is protected) for a wall with towers is a slightly odd verb choice; "altı kulesi vardır" is the usual way.

12. "bir meclis salonu" - in Turkish "meclis" first suggests parliament/assembly. Misread on first pass. A reader wants "toplantı/divan salonu" or a gloss for majlis.

13. "Kale sonradan bedevi işlerinin yürütüldüğü bir merkez olarak kullanıldı" - "bedevi işleri" sounds foreign (calque). What business? Then "su kaynakları yüzünden çıkan anlaşmazlıklar da burada çözülüyordu" - between whom? Bedouin tribes? Not said.

14. "2017'de restore edilerek ziyaretçilere açıldı." vs the visit card "Şu anda kapalı (restorasyon ve kültürel miras geliştirme çalışmaları nedeniyle)". Reads as a contradiction on first pass: it was opened in 2017 and now it is closed, with no bridge ("sonra yeniden kapandı"). I have to guess the order of events.

15. "kültürel miras geliştirme çalışmaları" - stiff calque of an official phrase; a Turkish writer would say "restorasyon ve çevre düzenleme çalışmaları" or just "restorasyon". Also "Şu anda" has no date; the reader cannot tell how current this is.

16. "Bugün, 2018'de UNESCO Dünya Mirası Listesi'ne alınan El-Ahsa Vahası alanının on iki bileşeninden biridir." Long, front-loaded modifier; I read twice before the verb. "Bugün" at the start adds nothing. The "on iki bileşen" is not explained (twelve sites in the property?).

## Beyt el-Biat

17. Lead: "El-Ahsa kadısı evi Hicri 1203 (1788) yılında yaptırdı" - first pass reads "El-Ahsa kadısı evi" as a noun phrase "the house of the qadi of El-Ahsa", then "yaptırdı" has no subject/object and the thread is lost. The sentence needs "El-Ahsa kadısı bu evi ... yaptırdı" or a different order.

18. Lead: "El-Mulla ailesine ait tarihî ev." Fragment fine, but "El-Mulla" is introduced without saying it is a family name until you read on; I wondered whether "Mulla" was a title.

19. "Hicri 28 Cemaziyelevvel 1331 (4 Mayıs 1913) günü" - "Hicri" glued to the day number reads oddly ("Hicri 28 ..."). A Turkish writer would put it as "Hicri 1331 Cemaziyelevvel'inin 28'i". Minor.

20. "Kral Abdülaziz" in 1913 - jars: he was not king then. I read it as an anachronism. Not an editor point, but it made me doubt the dates.

21. "Eski Eserler ve Müzeler Ajansı" - "ajans" in Turkish means news/advertising agency; sounds foreign for a heritage body. Misreads on first pass.

22. "evi 2005–2007 yıllarında restore etti; o tarihten bu yana ... burada yapılıyor" - "o tarihten" is ambiguous: 2005 or 2007?

23. "odalarında, evin hikâyesini anlatan eserler, aletler, el yazmaları ve fotoğraflar sergileniyor" - does "evin hikâyesini anlatan" cover only "eserler" or all four? Also "aletler" (tools) - what kind? I cannot picture it.

24. "Zemin kat, iç balkonların baktığı geniş bir karşılama avlusundan oluşur" - "balkonların baktığı" stumbles; I had to reconstruct that the upper galleries look onto the courtyard.

25. "çoğu yatma ve yaşam alanı olarak ayrılmış odalar" - calque; and "tarihî ve bilgilendirici fotoğraflarla kaplı" - "bilgilendirici fotoğraf" sounds foreign. Missing: hours, fee, whether it is open (the page calls it a museum but gives nothing practical in this excerpt).

26. "Evi ... yaptırdı; yapı sonradan ... geçti" - "ev / yapı" alternation and the very long chain of bin-names ("Şeyh Abdurrahman bin Ömer bin Muhammed el-Mulla") made me read twice; the thread to the verb is lost behind the name.

## Ukayr Kalesi

27. "Liman, Suudi Arabistan'ın doğu kıyısındaki ilk deniz limanıdır" - "deniz limanı" is redundant; "ilk" is also unclear (first built? first in importance?).

28. "emirlik binası" - "emirlik" reads as the emirate (the state), not an administrative building; I misread on first pass. Maybe "emir konağı / valilik binası".

29. "Sınırları belirleyen Ukayr Protokolü" - which borders, between whom? A Turkish reader without background loses the point; the protocol is named but its meaning is missing. Also a comma-less leap from the list of buildings to a treaty.

30. "binaları ise bütün bir çağın tanığı olarak ayakta kaldı" - cliché, sounds foreign/translated-brochure. "Binaları" - whose? (the port's) is clear only on second read.

31. "Yapılar 1990'ların sonunda restore edildi. Alanda restorasyon çalışmaları sürüyor; bazı bölümler kapalı olabilir." Two restoration statements back to back read like a contradiction (finished in the 1990s, ongoing now). "Olabilir" - I do not learn whether I can go. Missing: what to do there, what can be entered.

## El-Cebri Camii

32. Opening: "El-Kut mahallesi, tarihî Hüfuf'un en eski semtidir" - the page starts with the neighbourhood, not the mosque; I did not know what I was reading about until sentence two. Also "mahalle" and "semt" alternate for the same thing.

33. "Cebriler Devleti" - "Cebri/Cebriler" next to "Cebriyye" (the determinist sect) in a religious text: first pass I read it as a sect, not a dynasty. Needs "Cebrî hanedanı/emirliği" or a gloss.

34. "kesişen tonozları" - not the Turkish term; "çapraz tonoz" (cross vault) is what a writer would say. "kireç taşı" is normally "kireçtaşı". "çok sayıdaki revakları" fine but the whole sentence is a feature list I cannot visualise.

35. "yaklaşık 1.600 kişi namaz kılabilir" - fine, but the "1.600" with a dot is read by some as 1.6; minor.

36. "tarihî camilerin bakım programı kapsamında" - vague, program of whom? Sounds like a literal rendering of an official project name. I would expect a named project or "devlet programı".

37. "yaşayan bir camidir" - calque of "living mosque"; a Turkish writer would say "hâlâ ibadete açık".

38. Visit card, row "Giriş": "İbadet için kullanılan bir cami; ücretsiz". Under "Giriş" I expect conditions: who may enter, when, visitor dress. It only says it is a mosque and free. Can a non-Muslim visitor, or a visitor outside prayer times, go in? Missing. Also "Giriş" alone could be read as "Introduction".

## General

39. "Hicri ... yılında" repeated on every page and always before the Gregorian year in parentheses; fine once, heavy by the third page. "Hicrî" with circumflex would be expected in Turkish prose.

40. "El-Ahsa", "El-Kut", "El-Mulla": the hyphenated "El-" with capital second element is a transliteration style Turkish readers know from Arabic names, but then "Hüfuf" and "Müberrez" are in Turkish-spelled form; mixed conventions make it look unsystematic.
