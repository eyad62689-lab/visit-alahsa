# Blind reader notes (Turkish reader), on-05

Numbering restarts on each page. Tags: STUMBLE / UNCLEAR / FOREIGN / REGISTER / HANGING / MISREAD.

## Page 1 - Velid en-Nacim Müzesi

1. MISREAD. "El-Cefr kasabasında özel bir müze" (and "Vahanın doğusundaki El-Cefr kasabasında..."). "Cefr" has no vowel and I could not pronounce it. At a glance it looked like the language-level abbreviation CEFR. I read it as an acronym, not a place name. I am not suggesting a fix, but this is where I stopped.

2. STUMBLE. Subtitle: "koleksiyonu, El-Ahsa'nın eski yaşamını yeniden canlandıran yirmi altı temalı köşeye yayılıyor." The participle chain "El-Ahsa'nın eski yaşamını yeniden canlandıran yirmi altı temalı köşe" comes before the verb, so I read it twice. "Köşeye yayılıyor" is also odd: a collection spreads over twenty-six corners, and the present tense sounds like a work in progress. "Eski yaşam" sounds like a calque; I would expect "eski günlerin yaşamı" or "geçmişteki yaşam".

3. UNCLEAR. "Vahanın doğusundaki El-Cefr kasabasında..." "Vaha" is lower-case and has no name, so I do not know which oasis. I assume it is the El-Ahsa oasis, but the sentence does not say so. The genitive has no apostrophe and looks like a typo ("Vahanın").

4. REGISTER. "Müze, bölgenin en öne çıkan özel müzelerinden biridir." It is brochure-style praise. The subtitle already said "özel bir müze" and the next sentence says it again, so the opening repeats itself.

5. STUMBLE. "yaklaşık 400 metrekarelik bir alanda, yirmi altı temalı köşede düzenlenmiştir." Here "400" is in digits and "yirmi altı" is in words, which looks uneven. "Temalı köşe" is also an odd unit for a museum. I did not know whether it means a room, a display or a stand.

6. REGISTER / FOREIGN. "Müze, Suudi Arabistan'ın Kültür Bakanlığından ruhsat almıştır." "Ruhsat almıştır" is bureaucratic. "Suudi Arabistan'ın" is repeated several times in the paragraph. "Kültür Bakanlığından" has no apostrophe before the suffix, although "El-Kaysariye Çarşısı'nın" on page 2 does. The result is inconsistent. The sentence also leaves me asking what the licence means for a visitor, and the page does not say.

7. STUMBLE. "Müzenin köşeleri, El-Ahsa'nın eski yaşamından eksiksiz sahneleri yeniden canlandırır." "Eksiksiz sahneler" ("complete scenes") sounds foreign. I do not know what makes a scene complete. "Yeniden canlandırır" also repeats the "yeniden canlandıran" from the subtitle.

8. MISREAD. "erkeklerin ve kadınların meclis salonları". In Turkish, "meclis" means parliament or a council. I first imagined parliament-like halls. Only after "erkeklerin ve kadınların" did I guess a reception or sitting room. This is very much a first-pass misread.

9. UNCLEAR. "ilk dönem makineleri". "Early-period machines" does not tell me what kind of machines, or from which period. It sounds like a translation of something else. "Eski sikkeleri ve pulları" could be postage stamps or something else, and I could not tell.

10. STUMBLE / REGISTER. "Suudi Arabistan'ın turizm ve miras otoritesinin yetkilileri, müzeyi miras değeri bakımından kayda değer bir özel müze olarak övmüştür." This is a stack of genitives ("Arabistan'ın... otoritesinin yetkilileri") before I reach the verb. "Otorite" for an institution is foreign, and "miras değeri" alone reads like inheritance value. "Kayda değer" sits awkwardly with "övmüştür" (praised), and the authority is not named, so I cannot judge how much weight the praise has. The sentence also repeats the "en öne çıkan" claim from earlier.

11. HANGING. Visit info: "Ziyaret saatleri / Akşamları, önceden randevuyla". There are no hours for "evenings", and no way to book is given anywhere on the page. I am left asking who to call or write to and when. The information is missing, and I am not inventing it.

12. HANGING. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir — ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin." There is no contact detail for "the operator", and that is a problem on a page where booking is required. "Teyit" is slightly bookish, and "işletmeci ile" would normally be written together as "işletmeciyle".

13. REGISTER. The buttons "Bu yeri kaydedin", "Paylaşın" and "Benden ne kadar uzak?" mix a formal plural imperative with a casual first-person question. "Benden ne kadar uzak?" is casual and shifts the register of the page.

14. MISREAD (brand). The browser tab says "Visit Al-Ahsa" but the body says "El-Ahsa". For a moment I thought these might be two different places.

## Page 2 - El-Kaysariye Çarşısı

1. MISREAD. "El-Kaysariye". "Kaysariye" is close to "Kayseri", the Turkish city, and my eye slid onto that. I took it first as a Kayseri-related name and had to reset.

2. STUMBLE / FOREIGN. Subtitle: "El-Ahsa'nın en eski halk ve geleneksel çarşılarından biri, zanaat ile yerel ürünler için bir gezi noktası." "Halk ve geleneksel çarşı" joins two different kinds of adjective ("folk" and "traditional") in a way Turkish would not. "Zanaat ile yerel ürünler için bir gezi noktası" is a calque, and a Turkish writer would not say "gezi noktası". "İki yüzyıldan eski" is also unnatural; "iki yüzyıllık" would be normal.

3. UNCLEAR / MISREAD. "UNESCO Dünya Mirası alanının bileşenlerinden biri · 1563-005". "Bileşen" for "component" is a technical calque. The number "1563-005" means nothing to me and looks like a code or phone number. The site itself is not named, so "the site" is not identified. The dot and the button run together, so I misread them as one line.

4. CONTRADICTION. The subtitle says "El-Ahsa'nın en eski ... çarşılarından biri". The body says "Arap Körfezi bölgesinin en eski ve en köklü çarşılarından biridir". The claim is made at two scales (oasis versus Gulf), which feels inflated. The "iki yüzyıl" claim is also repeated three times (subtitle, body and FAQ).

5. FOREIGN. "Arap Körfezi". In Turkish I would expect "Basra Körfezi" or simply "Körfez". "Arap Körfezi" is the usual Arabic framing, and it made me stop for a moment.

6. FOREIGN. "Çarşının mimarisi kendine özgüdür." The sentence is correct but empty. It promises "unique architecture" with no detail, so it reads as filler.

7. STUMBLE / FOREIGN. "Sıkı sıkıya dizilmiş dükkânlar sizi baharat ve parfüm kokan, bakırın parıldadığı geçitlerden geçirir." Shops that "pass you through passages" is a causative personification that Turkish would not use. The two participial descriptions "baharat ve parfüm kokan, bakırın parıldadığı" come in front of the noun "geçitlerden" before the verb arrives, and I lost the thread.

8. REGISTER. "El-Ahsa'da geleneksel alışverişin kalbi burada atar." It is a brochure cliché ("the heart beats here"). It is fine as Turkish, but the voice is shouty next to the neutral card below.

9. UNCLEAR / MISREAD. "ünlü El-Ahsa biştini, yani itibarın ve büyük günlerin pelerinini". I did not know the word "bişt". The gloss "itibarın ve büyük günlerin pelerini" is poetic rather than informative, and I still cannot tell what the garment is. "Pelerin" means a cape, which gives the wrong picture. It is also spelled three different ways ("biştini", "Biştin yanı sıra", FAQ "bişti"). Without apostrophes I could not tell which one is the stem, and "Biştin" looked like another word.

10. CONTRADICTION. "“El-Ahsa dokuması”, ... el işlemesinin titizliğiyle ayırt edilir." "Dokuma" means woven fabric, but the sentence then describes embroidery ("el işlemesi"). I could not tell whether the thing being praised is weaving or embroidery. In addition "altın ya da gümüş kaplamalı sırma" is redundant, since "sırma" already means gold or silver thread. The sentence has no obvious link to the previous one, so I had to infer that it is about the bisht.

11. UNCLEAR. "Bu zanaatı El-Ahsa aileleri kuşaktan kuşağa aktarmıştır." "Bu zanaat" has two possible referents (the weaving or the embroidery), so I do not know which craft is meant.

12. STUMBLE. "Biştin yanı sıra hurma yaprağı ve çömlek işi yerel zanaatlar, her çeşit hurma, kahve ve baharat bulabilirsiniz." Four items are chained in a list, so "hurma yaprağı ve çömlek işi yerel zanaatlar" can be read as "palm leaf and pottery-type local crafts" or as two separate things. The verb comes at the very end. "Hurma" without context could also be persimmon, but the setting makes dates clear.

13. HANGING. Heading "El-Kaysariye Çarşısı ziyaret saatleri" is followed only by "Açılış saatleri ve ücretler aşağıdaki ziyaret bilgileri kartında yer alır". The heading promises hours, but the sentence just points to the card. "Yer alır" is bureaucratic.

14. CONTRADICTION. "çarşının en iyi saatleri akşamdır" versus the card's "En iyi zaman: Sabah ya da akşam". One text says evening only, the other says morning or evening. I do not know which to follow.

15. UNCLEAR. "08.00–12.00 ve 15.30–22.00 (Cuma yalnızca akşam)". For a Turkish reader, 15.30 is afternoon, not evening, so "akşam" does not match the opening time. The sentence also does not say which hours apply on Friday.

16. STUMBLE. FAQ answer "Ünlü El-Ahsa bişti, hurma yaprağı ve çömlek işi yerel zanaatlar, her çeşit hurma, kahve ve baharat." This is a bare noun list with no verb. It repeats the body's list, and the spelling "bişti" differs from "biştini" above.

17. REGISTER. Several FAQ answers repeat the card text word for word. "Hayır, giriş ücretsizdir." is fine, but the five questions give no new information.

## Page 3 - Medeniyetler Diyarı

1. MISREAD. "Cebel el-Kare" (used throughout). "Kare" is a Turkish word meaning "square", so "Cebel el-Kare'nin" read as "of the square mountain", and the suffix made that reading worse. I was not sure it was a name. "Kara" would mislead in the same way. The mountain, the village (page 5) and the cave experience all use the same name, and that is the same trap each time.

2. FOREIGN / REGISTER. Title and subtitle: "Medeniyetler Diyarı", "bölgenin medeniyet aşamalarını gözler önüne seren". "Diyar" is Ottoman-bookish. "Medeniyet aşamaları" ("stages of civilisation") is a calque, and "gözler önüne seren" is a worn cliché. Together they sound grander than a heritage village next to a mountain. Which "bölge" is meant is also not stated.

3. UNCLEAR. "bir miras köyü ve sergi". It is not clear whether this is one place or two, or what a "miras köyü" is in practice. The paragraph says only that it "bölgenin medeniyet aşamalarının izini sürer" ("traces the stages of civilisation"), and I cannot picture what I would see or do.

4. STUMBLE / MISREAD. "Köy, Cebel el-Kare'yi ve mağaralarını ziyaret deneyimiyle rotalar, tesisler ve manzara noktaları aracılığıyla bütünleşmiştir." This is the hardest sentence on the page. "Bütünleşmiştir" would normally take "-le" ("Cebel el-Kare'yle"), but here the object is accusative ("Cebel el-Kare'yi"). Two instrumentals ("ziyaret deneyimiyle" and "aracılığıyla") are stacked. "Rotalar", "tesisler" and "manzara noktaları" are calques (Turkish would say "güzergâh", "seyir terası" and so on). I could not work out what is being said, only that it is probably about routes and viewpoints.

5. STUMBLE. "biletler Cebel el-Kare mağaraları deneyimini de kapsar." "Cebel el-Kare mağaraları deneyimi" is four nouns in a row without proper case endings, and I read it twice. "Biletler" (plural) also conflicts with the card's "tek bilet" (single ticket).

6. CONTRADICTION. The body says the tickets cover "Cebel el-Kare mağaraları deneyimi" (the cave experience). The card says "tek bilet Medeniyetler Diyarı'nı ve Cebel el-Kare'yi kapsar" (the mountain itself). The scope is different: caves only, or the whole mountain?

7. MISREAD. "El-Umran şehri". "Umran" is a Turkish word (civilisation, prosperity) and also a given name, so on a page called "Medeniyetler Diyarı" it looked like a pun or a made-up place. "Şehri" ("city") also sits oddly next to the "köy" and "kasaba" of the other pages, and I cannot judge the size.

8. UNCLEAR. "Her gün 08.00–23.00 (tatillerde uzatılabilir)". "Tatillerde" does not say which holidays (public holidays or school holidays). "Uzatılabilir" is an impersonal bureaucratic passive, so I cannot tell how long the extension is.

9. HANGING. "Yetişkinler için 50 Suudi riyali (SAR)". "For adults" implies that there is a price for children, but none is given. I am not adding one. The page leaves a question open.

10. UNCLEAR. "50 SAR" in the FAQ. "SAR" is defined on the card but not here, and to me "SAR" looks like the Turkish verb "sar".

11. UNCLEAR / HANGING (category). The page sits under "Çarşılar ve parklar / Parklar ve bahçeler", but the text describes a heritage village and an exhibition, not a park or garden. A reader cannot tell why it is filed there. The page also does not say what I would actually do on a visit (walk? tour? see an exhibition?).

12. REGISTER. The FAQ "Medeniyetler Diyarı nedir?" repeats the subtitle word for word, and "nerede?" repeats the card. "Yer alır" is bureaucratic and appears twice.

## Page 4 - Zanaatkârlar Çarşısı

1. STUMBLE. Subtitle: "Tarihî Hüfuf'un kalbinde, zanaatkârları, dükkânlarını ve bir el sanatları akademisini bir araya getiren, El-Ahsa Belediyesinin gözetimindeki bir çarşı." This is one long participial chain, and the head noun "çarşı" arrives only at the very end. "Zanaatkârları, dükkânlarını" reads as possessives (his craftsmen, his shops) before I understood it as a list. "Tarihî" is capitalised as if it were a name. "Belediyesinin" has no apostrophe. "Gözetimindeki" is bureaucratic.

2. UNCLEAR. "Çarşı, el sanatlarını ve halk sanatlarını desteklemek amacıyla Mart 2020'de açıldı." Clear enough, but "el sanatları" and "halk sanatları" overlap, and I could not tell the difference.

3. MISREAD. "zanaatkârlar için 112 dükkân". "112" is the emergency number in Turkey, and I paused on it for a second. "12.000 metrekare" parsed correctly, because the dot is the normal Turkish thousands separator.

4. FOREIGN. "geleneksel bir kahvehane, bir tandır fırını". "Kahvehane" in Turkey is a tea-and-backgammon coffeehouse, and I pictured that, not Arabic coffee. "Tandır fırını" is a tautology: a tandır is already an oven. A Turkish reader also thinks of "kuzu tandır" (lamb dish) before the oven.

5. HANGING. "Akademinin bu görevi, El-Ahsa'nın UNESCO Yaratıcı Şehirler Ağı'na katılımıyla yakından bağlantılıdır." "Bu görev" is odd, because the previous sentence did not describe a task; it said that masters teach. "Yakından bağlantılıdır" is bureaucratic. The sentence also raises a question the page never answers: how are the academy and the network connected?

6. REGISTER / UNCLEAR. "Çarşı, Hüfuf'un tarihî şehir merkezini geliştirme projeleri kapsamında kuruldu; çarşıyı El-Ahsa Belediyesi yönetir." "Tarihî şehir merkezini geliştirme projeleri kapsamında" is a bureaucratic noun chain. The subtitle said "gözetimindeki" (under supervision) but here the municipality "yönetir" (manages). I could not tell if those are the same thing. The tense also moves from "açıldı" and "kuruldu" (past) to "yönetir" (present) in one paragraph.

7. STUMBLE. Hours: "Her gün 07.30–24.00 (Pazar ve Pazartesi 07.00'den · Cuma 14.30'dan)". "Her gün 07.30" and then "Pazar ve Pazartesi 07.00'den" look contradictory on first read, and the parenthesis with the dot separator is hard to parse. I had to read it twice to understand that the parenthesis lists exceptions. "24.00" is also unusual in Turkish; "00.00" would be more common.

8. HANGING. This page has no "Ücretler" row at all, yet the card's closing line says "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir". I do not know whether entry is free or paid, and the page does not say.

9. REGISTER / FOREIGN. FAQ: "Bu saatler, işletmenin Google Haritalar'da güncellediği saatlere göredir." "İşletme" (business) for a municipal market sounds foreign. "Saatler ... saatlere göredir" is circular. Pointing to Google Maps also weakens a page that otherwise says to confirm with the operator, and the wording "güncellediği" does not tell me who updated it.

10. REGISTER. FAQ: "Zanaatkârlar Çarşısı nerede ve kim yönetiyor?" "Kim yönetiyor" is spoken style and the rest of the page is written formally; the answer then uses "yönetir".

11. UNCLEAR (category). Filed under "Geleneksel çarşılar" although the text says it opened in March 2020. A "traditional bazaar" that is five years old (relative to the reader) is a small mismatch, and "tarihî" in the title refers to the surroundings, not to the market.

## Page 5 - Duğa el-Garaş Çömlek Atölyesi

1. MISREAD. "Duğa el-Garaş". In Turkish, "ğ" lengthens the preceding vowel and is silent, so I read "Duğa" as "Dua" (prayer). I could not pronounce it as a name, and it made me think of a religious word. "Garaş" is close to "garaj" (garage).

2. STUMBLE. Subtitle: "Cebel el-Kare'nin yanında, El-Garaş ailesinde kuşaktan kuşağa aktarılan geleneksel bir çömlek atölyesi; Suudi Arabistan'ın bu zanaattaki en bilinen merkezlerinden biri." A long participial chain ("El-Garaş ailesinde kuşaktan kuşağa aktarılan") precedes the noun. The later "bu zanaattaki" has no antecedent except "çömlek", so I had to look back.

3. CONTRADICTION. The subtitle says "bu zanaattaki en bilinen merkezlerinden biri" (pottery). The body says "halk zanaatının en ünlü merkezlerinden biri" (folk craft in general). The claim widens from pottery to all folk crafts between the two, so I cannot tell which one the site is asserting.

4. REGISTER / UNCLEAR. "Basın haberleri, çömlekçiliğin bu yerde altı yüz yıldan fazla süredir sürdüğünü kaydeder." "Basın haberleri" is vague (which press?). "Bu yerde" is stilted and "burada" would do. "Altı yüz" is in words while other numbers are in digits. For a figure this big I would want the source.

5. UNCLEAR. "Bu da atölyeyi, Suudi Arabistan'daki halk zanaatının en ünlü merkezlerinden biri hâline getirir." "Bu da" suggests that being 600 years old is what made it famous. That feels like a leap, and it repeats the subtitle.

6. UNCLEAR. "Atölyenin çömlekçileri çalışmalarını Fransa, Amerika Birleşik Devletleri ve Kanada'daki sergilerde sundu." There are no dates, and no names of exhibitions. The simple past gives the impression of a single event. The sentence stands alone and is not connected to the rest of the paragraph.

7. FOREIGN / STUMBLE. "Duğa el-Garaş bugün turistik bir duraktır; ziyaretçiler kilin yoğrulmasından çömlekçi çarkına ve fırına kadar zanaatın aşamalarını izler ve atölyenin ürünlerini eve götürür." "Turistik bir durak" is calque-like. "-den ... -e kadar" is used with a nominalised first term ("kilin yoğrulmasından") and plain nouns after it ("çarkına", "fırına"), which is uneven. "İzler" can also mean "traces" (plural noun), so I read it as a noun for a moment. The present tense reads like a promise that I can always watch every stage, and I do not know that.

8. HANGING. Hours "Her gün 08.00–12.00 ve 15.00–19.30" with "Giriş ücretsiz". Nothing says whether the workshop is really working during those hours (can I see the potters?) or just open. The page also has no FAQ block, unlike the others.

9. UNCLEAR (category). The workshop is filed under "Geleneksel çarşılar" and the link at the bottom says "Tüm çarşılar ve parklar", but the text describes a village pottery workshop, not a market. It is not clear why it sits there.

10. UNCLEAR. "El-Kare köyü — Cebel el-Kare'nin yanı, El-Ahsa'nın doğusu". The village and the mountain both carry the name "Kare" (and the page 3 location is "El-Umran şehri ... Cebel el-Kare'nin yanı"). I could not tell how these two places relate to each other, as the page gives no map or distance.
