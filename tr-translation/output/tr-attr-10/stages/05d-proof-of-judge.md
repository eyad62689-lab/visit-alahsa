## qaisariyah.body — DEFECT (one word, low severity)
Edited text: «Sıkı sıkıya dizilmiş dükkânlar sizi geçitlerden geçirir. Bu geçitler baharat ve parfüm kokar, bakır parıldar.»
EN: "its close-packed shops lead you through passages fragrant with spices and perfume and gleaming with copper".
- The split itself is right (the passages are named again, as lesson 1 asks; «geçirir» keeps "lead you through"), and «baharat ve parfüm kokar» is grammatical (kokmak takes a bare noun).
- Problem: «..., bakır parıldar.» Grammatical as a bare asyndetic clause, but its subject silently changes from «bu geçitler» to «bakır», and the relation is lost. In the English the passages are what gleam with copper; in the Turkish the copper just gleams, as a general statement, with no locus. This is exactly the "subject silently changed / nothing says where" stumble of lesson 3 on a sentence the judge wrote.
- Smallest fix: «Bu geçitler baharat ve parfüm kokar, bakırla parıldar.» (one word, «bakır» to «bakırla»; the subject stays «geçitler», and "gleaming with copper" is restored literally). Alternative if the judge prefers a clause: «; içlerinde bakır parıldar.» Neither adds a fact; «ve» is not needed (the comma pair of two predicates of one subject is fine once the subject is shared).
- The next sentence, «El-Ahsa'da geleneksel alışverişin kalbi burada atar.», still reads correctly («burada» = the souq); not a stumble.
- The second edit in this string, «Açılış saatleri» to «Ziyaret saatleri ve ücretler aşağıdaki ziyaret bilgileri kartında yer alır» under the heading, matches the tm pair and the English ("Opening hours and fees ... visit-information card below"); tail «çarşının en iyi saatleri akşamdır» unchanged and correct. ## ardh-alhadarat.body — SOUND
After: «Köy, Cebel el-Kare'yi ve mağaralarını ziyaret etme deneyimiyle bütünleşmiştir. Bu bütünleşmeyi rotalar, tesisler ve manzara noktaları sağlar. Ziyaret saatleri ve ücretler aşağıdaki ziyaret bilgileri kartında yer alır; biletler Cebel el-Kare'deki mağara deneyimini de kapsar.»
EN: "integrated with the experience of visiting the mountain and its caves through trails, facilities and viewpoints. Opening hours and fees ... tickets include the Al-Qarah Mountain caves experience."
- Does the split keep "through" as the means? Yes. The village is joined to «ziyaret etme deneyimi» (instrumental «-yle» with «bütünleşmiştir»), and the means (trails, facilities, viewpoints) become the explicit subject of the next sentence. The two instrumentals no longer compete, and «Cebel el-Kare'yi ve mağaralarını ziyaret etme» now has its accusative on a verbal noun with «etmek», which is correct («mağaralarını» = its caves, resolved by the shared accusative).

- «Cebel el-Kare'deki mağara deneyimini»: harmony right (Kare + de + ki), apostrophe on a place name only; locative removes the four-noun run. Subjects in the last sentence are clear (biletler).
- Observation, not a defect and not created by the judge: the English subject is "a heritage village and exhibition" but sentence 3 says only «Köy» (also in the pre-edit text). If the owner wants it exact, «Köy ve sergi» would repeat sentence 2's subject; left as is.

## craftsmen-souq.summary — SOUND
After: «Tarihî Hüfuf'un kalbinde bir çarşı; zanaatkârları, dükkânlarını ve bir el sanatları akademisini bir araya getirir ve El-Ahsa Belediyesinin gözetimindedir.»
EN: "A souq in the heart of historic Hofuf gathering artisans, their shops and a handicrafts academy, overseen by Al-Ahsa Municipality."
- Head noun first, as the card lead of the series does (cf. najim-museum.summary). Subject of both finite verbs is the çarşı, carried over the semicolon; «getirir» (verbal) and «gözetimindedir» (nominal predicate) share it with «ve» correctly.
- Is the possessive reading resolved? Yes. «zanaatkârları» is the accusative plural (zanaatkârlar + ı), forced by the transitive «bir araya getirir» and echoed by the second accusative in the list («dükkânlarını» = "their shops", the artisans'); a momentary possessive reading ("its artisans") before the verb is not possible to keep once «getirir» arrives, and the list order matches the English. Nothing lost: "overseen by" becomes «gözetimindedir»; institution without apostrophe, correct.
- Definiteness: the accusative makes "artisans" definite; Turkish cannot mark an indefinite plural object in a list like this without worse prose, so this is acceptable.
