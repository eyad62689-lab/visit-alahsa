# Blind reader notes (Turkish reader), on-r1

## Page 1 - /tr/stay/ (Konaklama)

1. Browser tab "Konaklama — Visit Al-Ahsa": the tab says "Al-Ahsa" while every line of the page says "El-Ahsa". I paused for a moment to check whether it was the same place. The Latin brand name beside a Turkish transliteration looks like two different spellings.
2. "Liste öneri içermez, yalnızca mekânları anlatır ve genişlemeye devam eder." (description and intro): "Liste ... genişlemeye devam eder" sounds foreign, because a Turkish writer would say "liste zamanla genişleyecek" or similar. The same disclaimer ("öneri içermez / yalnızca anlatır") comes back four times on this page (description, intro, FAQ, "Bu sayfa hakkında"). It reads defensive and repetitive.
3. "eski şehrin içindeki tarihî bir konukevinden": which "eski şehir" is meant is never said. "Hüfuf" first appears in the FAQ without any explanation, so I cannot tell how El-Ahsa, Hüfuf and the "eski şehir" relate to each other (region, town, old quarter?). That relation is missing.
4. Type label "Tarihî konukevi" above the name "Al-Koot Heritage Hotel", then "tarihî otel" in the FAQ ("ruhsat alan ilk tarihî otel"): konukevi and otel are used for the same building. I could not tell whether it is a guesthouse or a hotel.
5. "El-Kut mahallesi — Kasr İbrahim'in karşısında" vs the Latin name "Al-Koot": the Turkish "Kut" and the Latin "Koot" do not look like the same word. On first pass "El-Kut" reads as the Turkish word "kut" (luck, sacred). "Kasr İbrahim" is never introduced, so I do not know whether it is a palace, a fort or a park, and the card assumes I do.
6. "Hurma ağacı gövdeleri ve yapraklarından yapılmış tavanların altında odalar; saray, tarihî konukevi olarak restore edilmiş." (card text): "saray" appears suddenly, as if I already knew about a palace. It is a telegraphic fragment. The evidential "restore edilmiş" sounds like hearsay for a fact the page itself states elsewhere. "gövdeleri ve yapraklarından" (two nouns sharing one ablative) made me read it twice.
7. Photo description "sadu dokumasıyla": "sadu" is unexplained here, and I do not know what it is. (Page 2 explains it, but this page does not.)
8. "Mazgallı korkulukları ve dar, ince açıklıkları olan kum rengi bir bina; kıyıya yakın bir çimenliğe bakıyor." "dar, ince açıklıklar" sounds foreign, a calque of "narrow slits". I could not picture it. "çimenliğe bakıyor" is also odd for a building, since a building faces something; it does not look at a lawn. It is unclear what is on offer here (rooms? a hotel with a beach?).
9. "Ukayr kıyısı" (card) vs "Ukayr Plajı" (FAQ) vs "Al Uqayr" (name): kıyı and plaj are used for the same place, and the spelling "Ukayr" against "Uqayr" looks like two names. For a Turkish reader "kıyı" also suggests a rocky coast, not a beach.
10. "Evet — Al-Koot Heritage Hotel ... Otel, El-Ahsa'da ruhsat alan ilk tarihî otel oldu; ruhsatı Suudi Turizm ve Millî Miras Kurumu verdi." The question was whether I can stay in a historic guesthouse. The answer adds a licensing fact that feels bureaucratic and does not help a traveller. "Millî Miras Kurumu" is a stiff, official-sounding rendering.
11. "Karavanıyla gelenler için plajın kendisinde ayrılmış bir alan da var": the question was about a place to stay near the beach, and the answer suddenly turns to caravans, so the thread is lost. "plajın kendisinde" is foreign-sounding. It is unclear whether the area belongs to the hotel or is separate.
12. "rezervasyon çevrim içi, El-Ahsa Belediyesi üzerinden yapılır": ambiguous. I could not tell whether this is the hotel booking or the caravan area booking. A municipality taking bookings is unexpected, and nothing says where or how. This is left hanging.
13. "seçenekleri güvenilir rezervasyon platformlarında karşılaştırın": "güvenilir rezervasyon platformları" is vague. Which ones? It sounds preachy, and it points away from the page without naming anything.
14. "Mekânları birbirine göre sıralamıyoruz, fiyat da yazmıyoruz": the first-person plural appears for the first time here, where the rest of the page uses impersonal verbs. "Kartlar yalnızca bilgi verir" is a dry, bureaucratic register.
15. "kısa tanıtım yazıları her mekânı fotoğrafında göründüğü gibi tanıtır": "tanıtım ... tanıtır" repeats the same root, and the idea (descriptions describe the photo) sounds like a translation. I could not tell what the reader gains from it. "Adlar ve konumlar doğrudan Google Haritalar'dan alınır" is again a statement about sourcing, not information for the trip.

## Page 2 - /tr/stay/alkoot-heritage-hotel/ (Al-Koot Heritage Hotel)

1. Search-engine description "saray, tarihî konukevi olarak restore edilmiş": a fragment with no subject introduced and an evidential verb form. In the search result I would not know which palace is meant.
2. "Hüfuf'un El-Kut mahallesinde, Kasr İbrahim'in tam karşısında, yaklaşık iki yüz yıllık bir saray yer alır." Hüfuf is again not explained, and "Kasr İbrahim" is assumed known. The sentence stacks three locators before the subject ("saray") arrives, and I lost the thread.
3. "Saray restore edilerek tarihî konukevine dönüştürüldü; konuklar bu sarayın içinde kalır." Redundant: the previous sentence already said it. The two verb forms (past passive, then aorist) in one sentence also feel mixed.
4. "963 metrekarelik bina eski El-Ahsa mimari üslubuna uyar." A sentence starting with a bare number is abrupt. "uyar" can also be read as "warns" on first pass. "eski El-Ahsa mimari üslubu" sounds foreign, since it is a calque. It is not clear whether 963 m² is the footprint or the floor area.
5. "Binayı Abdülaziz bin Muhammed el-Abdülkadir kendi girişimiyle restore etti." The man is never introduced (owner? investor?), so the name hangs without a role. "kendi girişimiyle" is bureaucratic and calque-like ("on his own initiative"). The long chain of Arabic name parts is hard to take in.
6. "Açılışı Hicri 1439 (2018) yılında Prens Sultan bin Selman ile Prens Suud bin Nayif yaptı." "Hicri 1439 (2018) yılında" parses oddly: "yılında" after a Hijri year, and the bracketed Gregorian year looks like an exact equivalent when the two calendars overlap. "Prens Suud" read for a second as "Suudi". Two further princes' names after the previous long name make the paragraph feel like a ceremony report, not a travel text.
7. "Otel, El-Ahsa'da ruhsat alan ilk tarihî otel oldu; ruhsatı Suudi Turizm ve Millî Miras Kurumu verdi." Word for word the same as the FAQ on the stay page. Again, no date is given for the licence, and the sentence has no use for a guest.
8. "Otelde sekiz süit var." The page 1 text spoke of "odalar" and a single "oda"; now the property has eight suites. I cannot tell whether there are ordinary rooms besides the suites. Number and wording do not match.
9. "Süitlerin tavanı hurma ağacı gövdeleri ve yapraklarından yapılmıştır; duvarlarda kemerli nişler vardır ve sadu denen dokumalar asılıdır." The "-dır" endings (yapılmıştır, vardır, asılıdır) give an encyclopedic, bookish register, unlike the neighbouring "yer alır / sergilenir". "sadu denen dokumalar" finally explains the word, but only as a name, with no description of what the weaving looks like.
10. "Giriş holünde antika parçalar sergilenir." "antika parçalar" is vague (what kind of pieces?). It is also a different register from the sentence before.
11. "Üst kattaki kütüphanede El-Ahsa'nın ve El-Kut'un tarihine dair kitaplar birkaç dilde bulunur." "birkaç dilde" leaves open which languages, and whether Turkish is among them. "El-Kut'un" with the suffix reads as "kutun".
12. "Kasr İbrahim kapıdan birkaç adım uzaklıktadır; El-Kaysariye Çarşısı ile El-Fevaris Çarşısı yaklaşık yarım kilometre mesafededir." "kapıdan": which door? "El-Kaysariye" first reads as "Kayseri" with a suffix, which is a misread for a Turkish reader. "yarım kilometre mesafededir" is heavy and bookish ("yarım kilometre uzaktadır").
13. "Burada konaklamak, tarihî şehrin kenarında değil, içinde kalmak demektir." This is the only slogan-like line on a page that elsewhere says it does not recommend anything. The "tarihî şehir" is again undefined, and the promotional register is out of place.
14. Section "Konum" repeats "El-Kut mahallesi — Kasr İbrahim'in karşısında", the same line as the sub-heading (and the card on the list page). The same locator appears three times, but no distance or way to get there.
15. "Ad ve konum doğrudan Google Haritalar'dan alınır; puan ise site her güncellendiğinde oradan alınır ve değişebilir." No rating is shown anywhere on this page, so the sentence leaves me wondering where the "puan" is. "site her güncellendiğinde" is foreign-sounding (a technical statement). The next sentence says "yıldız sınıflandırması yer almaz", which also contradicts the idea of a rating being shown.
16. "bu sayfa öneri içermez, yalnızca oteli anlatır": the disclaimer again. It sounds legalistic and a little defensive.
17. The browser tab and the button "[button] Paylaşın": the formal plural imperative is fine, but the tab ends with the Latin brand name and no Turkish name for the site. Minor.

## Page 3 - /tr/restaurants-cafes/ (Restoranlar ve kafeler)

1. Description and intro: "El-Ahsa'da yeme içme rehberiniz: ..." is brochure-like ("rehberiniz"), and the very long single sentence with semicolons was heavy. The description and the intro are identical, and the description is too long to show in a search result.
2. "hurma ekmeği (“kırmızı ekmek”) fırınları": I do not understand what the bread is or why it is "red". "nitelikli kahve" is understandable but sounds like a calque of "specialty coffee".
3. "Her birinin fotoğrafını ve Google Haritalar'daki konumunu tek tıkla ya da dokunuşla görebilirsiniz." "tek tıkla ya da dokunuşla" is clumsy. It also does not match the cards: many show only "Ayrıntılara bakın", with no visible map link.
4. Filter numbers: "Tümü (29)" = 10 + 15 + 4 is consistent, but the region options add up to 24 (2+3+4+3+1+2+1+1+1+2+2+2), not 29. Five places must be missing from the regions, and indeed several cards (Bun Café, Bu Hussain, Bu Mubarak, SHYLM, OUDA) show no location line at all. As a reader I cannot find them by region.
5. Region names: "Kuzey er-Rifa mahallesi" is a hybrid that reads strangely; "Arap Körfezi Yolu" is a road, not a district, but sits in the same list as districts; "Hüfuf tarihî merkezi" and "El-Kut mahallesi" overlap with each other (the FAQ calls El-Kut part of Hüfuf). It is not clear whether the list is by district or by road.
6. "El-Kare" and "Cebel el-Kare": "kare" reads at first as the Turkish word for "square". It is a misread name. "Cebel" instead of "dağ" is understandable but bookish.
7. "Dar Basma", "Dar Huwaija": "Dar" reads as the Turkish "dar" (narrow). I read "Dar Basma" as "narrow Basma" on first pass. Also "Huwaija" has no clear Turkish pronunciation.
8. "Bu Hussain Traditional Bakery", "Bu Mubarak Traditional Bakery": "Bu" reads as the Turkish demonstrative "bu" ("this Hussain bakery"). The Latin forms ("Khunaina", "Al Hawy", "Sharq", "Qaisariyah") cannot be pronounced by a Turkish reader without help, and they differ from the Turkish transliterations elsewhere on the page ("Al-Khudud" against "El-Hudud", "Al-Koot" against "El-Kut").
9. Dar Basma: "Hurma ağaçlarının arasında açık hava oturma alanları ve Kasr İbrahim parkının kenarında cam bir cephe." The sentence is a list of visual features and never says what kind of food. Throughout the cards, descriptions say what the facade looks like (cephe, tabela, kapı), not what one eats or does there. It is unclear what I would actually do at most of these places.
10. Al Hawy: "Kral Faysal Üniversitesinin karşısında": no apostrophe after the proper noun, unlike "Kasr İbrahim'in" elsewhere. Inconsistent. "tabak tabak serilmiş" is lively but fine.
11. Khunaina: "Koyu yeşil kadife koltukların arasında hasır sepetlerle servis edilen geleneksel yemekler." Which dishes? "geleneksel yemekler" is empty. The card has a Maps link but no detail page, unlike the cards above it (the difference is unexplained).
12. Sinyar: "Adı Arap hat sanatıyla yazılmış, modern bir toprak yapı." "toprak yapı" sounds foreign (kerpiç? toprak sıvalı?). I could not tell whether the building is earth or earth-coloured. The card reads like a photo caption.
13. Greasy Fingers: "Metal tepsilerde hamburger ve patates kızartması — listedeki modern ve gündelik seçenek." This is an editorial judgement ("listedeki modern ve gündelik seçenek") on a page that claims to give no recommendations, and the tone is oddly casual.
14. Lava: "Mermer masalarda, demir tavalarla sıcak servis edilen yemekler." Which dishes? Same emptiness. "Lava" is read as the word for lava.
15. Ammo: "Üstünde parlayan tabelası olan modern bir cam cephe." says nothing about the restaurant itself. "Ammo" is also unclear to pronounce.
16. Sharq Restaurant: the card name is "Sharq Restaurant", but the photo description says "Sharq Al-Qaisariyah". I could not tell whether these are the same place. "Qaisariyah" connects to the "El-Kaysariye" of page 2 only if I guess it.
17. Code: "Hem restoran hem kafe — kendini böyle tanıtıyor, koyu cephesindeki parlayan Arapça yazı da bunu duyuruyor." "kendini böyle tanıtıyor" is oddly casual and slightly ironic. I can't read the Arabic writing, so "bunu duyuruyor" tells me nothing. The same "kendini böyle tanıtıyor" returns for Baking Up.
18. Baithana: "Hüfuf'un eski sokaklarından birinde, ahşap kapılı, çamur sıvalı bir ev." A house, not a cafe: what do I do there? "Baithana" has an unclear pronunciation ("bayt-hane"?).
19. 7st. Speciality Coffee: the name has a full stop in the middle and a British spelling; "7st" is unreadable aloud. The card says "Kuzey er-Rifa mahallesi", while the FAQ says it is "Kasr İbrahim'in yanındaki 7st". Two different locations are given for the same cafe.
20. Bait Al-Koot: "Hüfuf'un tarihî kalbinde, üzerine ışıklar asılmış, El-Ahsa üslubunda bir ev." "kalbinde" is a brochure cliché. "üzerine ışıklar asılmış" is hard to parse (hung on what? the house?). The participle chain made me read it twice. "Bait" reads as an odd Turkish word.
21. Dar Huwaija: "Beyaz alçı kemerlerin ve kahve hazırlama takımlarıyla dolu rafların arasında oturma alanları." Two genitive chains inside one noun phrase before the subject appears; I lost the thread. "alçı kemerler" is fine, but "kahve hazırlama takımlarıyla dolu raflar" is heavy.

"minderler ve halılarla döşeli" is fine. This is also the third "Koot" name (Al-Koot Heritage Hotel, Bait Al-Koot, Ratio Al-Koot), and I could not tell if they are connected.
