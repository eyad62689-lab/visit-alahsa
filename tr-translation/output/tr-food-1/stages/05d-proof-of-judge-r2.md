1. fd.heroSum - DEFECT (minor)
   Current: "Bu malzemelerden sıcak yemekler ortaya çıkar; bu yemekler kuşaktan kuşağa sofradan sofraya aktarılır."
   Problem: the participle-before-subject is fixed and no fact is lost, but "kuşaktan kuşağa sofradan sofraya" stacks two "X-dan X-a" distributives with no conjunction. "sofradan sofraya" is the idiom of spreading from table to table (spatial), so beside "kuşaktan kuşağa" it reads as a doublet and blurs the English "handed down the tables generation after generation" (the tables are the place, not a second chain). The sagu card already uses "elden ele" (D13), so the page has three of these idioms.
   Fix: "Bu malzemelerden sıcak yemekler ortaya çıkar; bu yemekler sofralarda kuşaktan kuşağa aktarılır."
