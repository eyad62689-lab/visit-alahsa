# Blind reader notes (Turkish reader), on-r3

## Page 1 - El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği

1. Breadcrumb: "Ana sayfa / Çarşılar ve parklar / El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği Çiftlikler El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği". A lime farm sitting under "Çarşılar ve parklar" (markets and parks) does not fit, and the closing link "Tüm çarşılar ve parklar →" repeats the mismatch. The run-together breadcrumb and category label ("...Çiftliği Çiftlikler El-Ahsa...") also read as a stumble.
2. "ünlü El-Ahsa misket limonunu (lomi)": in Turkish "misket limonu" is simply the lime. "(lomi)" is not a word I know, and I cannot tell whether it is a second name, a local variety or something different. It is left unexplained. It also looks like a typo for "limon".
3. "Bir narenciye çiftliği; ziyaretçiler ünlü El-Ahsa misket limonunu (lomi) mevsiminde burada toplar." The same sentence appears twice, as the lede and again as the tagline directly under the buttons. It reads as duplicated text.
4. "ziyaretçiler ... burada toplar": I understand it as pick-your-own, but nothing says whether this is allowed, guided or paid. "ünlü" ("famous") is brochure-style praise with nothing behind it.
5. "Haziran ortasından ağustos ayına kadar, misket limonu toplama mevsimi." The range is lopsided ("from mid-June until the month of August"). I cannot tell whether August is included. The sentence is also a bare noun phrase after the heading.
6. "Her gün 16.00–21.30 (yetişme mevsimlerinde)": "yetişme mevsimi" is the growing season, not the picking season ("hasat"), and the plural is odd given the single season above. It sounds foreign. It also leaves a gap: what are the hours outside those seasons, or is the farm closed?
7. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir — ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin." No fees appear anywhere on the page, so "ücretler" points at nothing. "İşletmeci" (the operator) is never named and no contact is given, so "confirm with the operator" is a dead end. "Teyit edin" is bureaucratic. I would say "sorun" or "doğrulayın".
8. Nothing says where the farm is. I can only press "Benden ne kadar uzak?" ("How far from me?"), and that button gives me no hint of what it will do.
9. "[button] + Bu yeri kaydedin [button] Paylaşın": the polite-plural imperatives feel a little brochure-like next to the plain page text. The browser tab mixes "El-Ahsa" with "Visit Al-Ahsa", so the place name is spelled two ways on one screen.

## Page 2 - Çilek Çiftliği

1. Same breadcrumb problem as Page 1: a strawberry farm under "Çarşılar ve parklar" with a closing link to "Tüm çarşılar ve parklar →".
2. "Ukayr yolu üzerinde bir çilek çiftliği": I cannot tell whether this is a road named Ukayr or the road to a place called Ukayr. "Ukayr" is not a word I know and I am unsure how to say it. Nothing explains what or where it is.
3. "mevsimlik çilek toplama imkânı sunar": "imkânı sunar" ("offers the possibility of") is brochure and officialese. "Mevsimlik" then promises a season, but the season is only given later, in the hours line.
4. "Kış ve ilkbahar mevsimlerinde 15.30–21.30": it does not say "her gün". I do not know whether this is daily, and nothing says which days. This is the only seasonal information, and it sits under visit hours rather than a "best time" line as on Page 1.
5. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir — ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin.": again no fees are shown and no operator is named.
6. The lede and the tagline repeat the same sentence, as on Page 1.
7. The title "Çilek Çiftliği" is just "Strawberry Farm", a generic descriptor and not a name. I cannot tell it from any other strawberry farm, and the page gives me no name to search for.

## Page 3 - Es-Sevsen Kuş Bahçeleri

1. "El-Ahsa vahasının havasında kuşların sergilendiği bir bahçe.": "vahasının havasında" reads first as "in the air of the oasis", with birds displayed in the air. Only on a second reading does it become "the atmosphere of the oasis". I could not work out what is actually promised.
2. "kuşların sergilendiği": the passive "exhibited" makes the birds sound like a museum or cage display. I cannot tell what I would do there: watch wild birds, see aviaries or something else.
3. "Es-Sevsen": my eye reads "sevsen" as the Turkish verb ("if you loved"). The name does not parse as a place name. I am unsure how to pronounce it.
4. Contradiction in number: the title says "Kuş Bahçeleri" (plural, gardens), but the lede says "bir bahçe" (one garden).
5. Nothing says where it is, how large it is or which birds. The only content is one sentence plus the hours.
6. "Her gün 15.30–22.30" is word for word the same as the Et-Taraf zoo hours on the next page. That is not necessarily wrong, but it looks suspicious, as if copied. "ücretler" appears in the standard notice with no fees shown, as on the other pages.

## Page 4 - Et-Taraf Hayvanat Bahçesi

1. "Et-Taraf": on first pass "Et" reads as the Turkish word for meat, and "taraf" as "side". The hyphenated name reads as an ordinary Turkish phrase, and I am unsure how to pronounce it.
2. The location is stated three times in a row: the lede ("Hüfuf'un doğusundaki Et-Taraf kasabasında"), then "Et-Taraf kasabası, Hüfuf'un doğusunda...", then the "Bölge" field. "Kasabada Et-Taraf Hayvanat Bahçesi bulunur." repeats the title and lede. It reads as padding.
3. "aile gezi noktasıdır": "gezi noktası" ("excursion point") is a calque. A Turkish writer would say "gezilecek yer" or just "aileler için bir gezi yeri".
4. "çocukların çeşitli hayvanları yakından görebildiği": which animals? "Çeşitli" says nothing. Nothing says whether you can touch or feed them.
5. "Bölgenin sade ve telaşsız aile gezilerinden biridir": the subject is unclear. After the previous sentence it could be the zoo or the town, and "Bölgenin" could mean the town, the oasis or the region. I had to reread.
6. "sade ve telaşsız": "sade" ("plain") is faintly negative next to the praise in the rest of the sentence, and the register is soft brochure.
7. "yakındaki Et-Taraf kırsalını ve vahanın güney kesimlerini ziyaret etmekle kolayca birleştirilebilir": a long passive chain ("combined by visiting") that I had to read twice. The verb comes at the very end. Also "Et-Taraf kırsalı" ("the Et-Taraf countryside") is not a destination I can picture.
8. Spatial tension: the town is east of Hüfuf, but the "nearby" places to combine it with are "the southern parts of the oasis". I cannot place that, and "yakındaki" ("nearby") does not fit "güney kesimleri".
9. "Geziyi planlamadan önce yerinde teyit edin.": "yerinde" means on the spot, but you cannot confirm on the spot before planning the trip. It also does not say what to confirm.
10. The advice is contradicted by the box below, which says "ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin" (confirm with the operator). One says on site, the other with the operator. No operator is named.
11. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir": no fees are given, so I do not know whether entry is paid.
12. "Her gün 15.30–22.30": identical to the Es-Sevsen hours on the previous page. I notice it but cannot judge it.

## Page 5 - El-Hamidiye Çarşısı

1. Lede: "Hüfuf'un merkezindeki geleneksel çarşı binası, tarihî şehir merkezini geliştirme çalışmaları kapsamında restore edildi." Officialese. The chain "tarihî şehir merkezini geliştirme çalışmaları kapsamında" is a stack of nouns before the verb, and "kapsamında" is bureaucratic.
2. In the same sentence "merkezindeki" and "şehir merkezini" repeat "merkez". It sounds heavy.
3. "bugün binada Şark Restoranı hizmet veriyor": "Şark" is an Ottoman-bookish word for "the East/Orient", so I cannot tell whether it is the restaurant's proper name or a description ("an Eastern restaurant"). "Hizmet veriyor" is bureaucratic, and "faaliyet gösteriyor" or "var" would be plainer.
4. "El-Hamidiye Çarşısı, Hüfuf'un merkezinde bir binadır.": the "X is a building" construction is wooden, a calque. The next sentence ("Bina, Kuzey er-Rifa mahallesinde...") gives the same location a third time.
5. "Kuzey er-Rifa mahallesinde": "er" reads as the Turkish word for a private soldier, and I cannot tell whether "Kuzey" is part of the name or a direction. The Turkish "Kuzey" plus Arabic "er-Rifa" in one name is jarring.
6. "El-Hamidiye": to a Turkish reader "Hamidiye" immediately suggests Sultan Abdülhamid and Ottoman-era buildings. The page never explains the name or says whether there is any connection. A question is raised and never answered.
7. "restore edildi" for El-Hamidiye but "yeniden inşa edildi" (rebuilt) for El-Kaysariye: restored versus rebuilt is a big difference and the page does not explain it. "Restore edildi" also uses a foreign verb where "onarıldı" would be natural.
8. "El-Kaysariye Çarşısı": "Kaysariye" looks like "Kayseri" and is hard to place.
9. "Zanaatkârlar Çarşısı da kuruldu": "Zanaatkârlar Çarşısı" is stiff and bookish, as if from a translation. "Kuruldu" ("founded") for a market building is odd, and I wonder whether it is new or old.
10. "Böylece bina, şehrin kalbindeki geleneksel çarşı binaları kümesine katıldı.": "kümesine katıldı" is a calque ("joined the cluster"). "Şehrin kalbi" is brochure language. It also contradicts the page: a building that is "no longer a market" joins the "cluster of traditional market buildings".
11. "Günümüzde bina çalışan bir çarşı değil, tarihî bir yapıdır: artık halk çarşısı olarak çalışmıyor; içinde Şark Restoranı hizmet veriyor.": the register shifts mid-sentence from formal "-dır" to the plain "-yor". The same fact ("it is not a market any more") is stated twice inside one sentence and appears four more times on the page.
12. "halk çarşısı": a Turkish reader says "halk pazarı" for a neighbourhood market. "Halk çarşısı" ("people's market") is unfamiliar. I could not tell what kind of market is meant.
13. "Binaya ve tarihine dair daha fazla ayrıntı, burada yayımlanmadan önce güvenilir kaynaklardan teyit edilmeyi bekliyor.": this is an editor's note shown to the reader. The page admits it is unfinished, and the passive "teyit edilmeyi bekliyor" is bureaucratic. I could not tell what is missing, only that something is.
14. The building is called "tarihî" (historic) several times, but the page gives no date, age or history at all. The claim hangs in the air.
15. I cannot tell what I would do there. Under "Ziyaret bilgileri" there are no hours, no fee and no restaurant details, only "Bölge" and "Güncel durum". Is it worth visiting just to look at, or only to eat?
16. FAQ: "El-Hamidiye Çarşısı'nın çevresindeki geleneksel çarşılarla bağlantısı nedir?" ("What is its connection with the traditional markets around it?") is stiff and bureaucratic. The answer does not give a connection. It repeats the restoration programme and says nothing about what is "around" it.
17. In that FAQ "çevresindeki" (surrounding) implies that El-Kaysariye and Zanaatkârlar are next to it. The page never says so, and I could not tell whether the other two markets are still operating.
18. The restoration sentence ("Hüfuf'un tarihî şehir merkezini geliştirme çalışmaları kapsamında restore edildi...") appears almost word for word three times (lede, body and FAQ). The location sentence and the "not a market" sentence are repeated in the same way. The page feels like one fact said many times.
19. Nothing on this page, or on the ones before it, says how "Hüfuf" relates to "El-Ahsa". As a reader I only guess that Hüfuf is a city in the oasis.
