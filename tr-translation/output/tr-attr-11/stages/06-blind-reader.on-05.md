# Blind reader notes (Turkish reader), on-05

## Page 1 - El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği

1. "Ana sayfa / Çarşılar ve parklar / El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği Çiftlikler El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği" - Misreads on first pass. A lemon farm sits under "Çarşılar ve parklar" (markets and parks). I expected a market or a park, and only the glued-on word "Çiftlikler" corrects that. The title, the category and the title again run together with no separator, so I read the line twice.
2. "Bir narenciye çiftliği; ziyaretçiler ünlü El-Ahsa misket limonunu (lomi) mevsiminde burada toplar." - This sentence appears twice, directly under the title and again under the buttons. The second time it reads like a copy-paste error.
3. "(lomi)" in the same sentence - Misreads. I could not place "lomi". It looks like a typo for "limon", and nothing explains it. The parenthesis implies lomi equals misket limonu, but I cannot tell whether it is a local name or a different product. The page does not say, so this is missing.
4. "ziyaretçiler ... burada toplar" - Unclear. The habitual "toplar" tells me visitors pick the fruit, but not whether I, as a visitor, may do it, or whether it is just what happens here. "toplayabilir" or "toplama imkânı" would have been clearer. "ünlü" is also a little brochure-like.
5. "Haziran ortasından ağustos ayına kadar, misket limonu toplama mevsimi." against "Her gün 16.00–21.30 (yetişme mevsimlerinde)" - Contradiction. "En iyi zaman" gives one season (mid-June to August), but the hours line says "yetişme mevsimlerinde" in the plural. I cannot tell whether there is one season or several, or whether the farm is closed outside them. "Yetişme mevsimi" for opening periods also sounds foreign; "sezon" or "toplama mevsimi" would be more natural. It is also unclear whether August is included in "ağustos ayına kadar".
6. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir — ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin." - Left hanging. No fee appears anywhere on the page, yet it warns that fees can change, so I do not know whether there is a fee. "İşletmeci" is not identified, and no phone number or other way to contact them is given. "Teyit edin" is bureaucratic and bookish; "doğrulayın" or "sorun" is more natural, and "işletmeciyle" is more natural than "işletmeci ile".
7. "[button] Benden ne kadar uzak?" - Foreign. It is understandable, but a Turkish site would say something like "Bana uzaklığı" or "Buraya ne kadar uzaktayım?". More importantly, the page never says where the farm is (no area, no town), so the button has nothing to anchor to. That information is missing.
8. "[button] + Bu yeri kaydedin [button] Paylaşın" - Register. The polite plural imperative is stiff for buttons, and "Bu yeri" is flat. Turkish apps use short forms: "Kaydet", "Paylaş".
9. Browser tab "El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği — Visit Al-Ahsa" - The tab mixes "El-Ahsa" (Turkish rendering) with "Al-Ahsa" (English), so the same place has two spellings on one line.

## Page 2 - Çilek Çiftliği

10. "Ana sayfa / Çarşılar ve parklar / Çilek Çiftliği Çiftlikler Çilek Çiftliği" - Same stumble as item 1: a farm filed under markets and parks, with the category label glued into the title.
11. "Ukayr yolu üzerinde bir çilek çiftliği" - Misreads. I could not tell whether "Ukayr" is a town, a road name or a district, or whether the road is "the road to Ukayr" or a road called Ukayr. "Ukayr" is also hard to pronounce, and the page does not say where it is (no region, no city). That is missing. This sentence also appears twice on the page.
12. "mevsimlik çilek toplama imkânı sunar" - Register. "İmkânı sunar" is brochure and bureaucratic language. "Mevsimlik" reads as "seasonal" but could also be read as "for a season". Something like "mevsiminde çilek toplanabilir" would be more natural.
13. "Kış ve ilkbahar mevsiminde 15.30–22.30" (the text reads 15.30–21.30) - Stumbles and is left hanging. "Mevsiminde" is singular but two seasons are named; "kış ve ilkbahar aylarında" would be natural. Unlike page 1, no days are given, so I cannot tell if it is open every day. There is also no "best time" and no statement of what happens in summer or autumn.
14. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir..." - Same hanging issue as item 6. No fee is shown, and strawberry picking is the one activity where I would want to know whether it is paid by weight.
15. The lede is repeated verbatim under the buttons - Same duplication as item 2.

## Page 3 - Es-Sevsen Kuş Bahçeleri

16. "El-Ahsa vahasının ortamında kuşların sergilendiği bir bahçe." - Stumbles and sounds foreign. "Ortamında" means "in the environment/medium of", not "in the middle of". I assumed "ortasında" was meant, but I had to guess. The whole sentence is a long participial chain ("kuşların sergilendiği") and the verb is missing, so it is a fragment.
17. "kuşların sergilendiği" - Sounds foreign. "Sergilenmek" is for objects in an exhibition. For live birds I cannot tell whether this means aviaries, a zoo-like display, or free-flying birds. What kind of birds is missing.
18. "Es-Sevsen" - Misreads. "Sevsen" reads as the Turkish verb form "sevsen" ("if you loved/liked"), and "Es-" in front looks like a clipped prefix. Only the comparison with other "Es-/El-" names on the site told me it is a name.
19. Title "Kuş Bahçeleri" (plural) against "bir bahçe" (one garden) - Contradiction. I cannot tell whether it is one garden or several.
20. The whole page - Unclear and left hanging. There is only one sentence about the place, so I do not know what I would actually do there, how long a visit takes, whether there is a fee, or where it is. Unlike page 4, there is no "Bölge" line.

## Page 4 - Et-Taraf Hayvanat Bahçesi

21. "Et-Taraf" - Misreads. On first pass "Et" is the Turkish word for meat and "taraf" is "side", so the name reads as a Turkish phrase. It took the context of Hüfuf to realise it is a place name.
22. "Kasabada Et-Taraf Hayvanat Bahçesi bulunur." - Stumbles. The sentence only restates the title and the lede, and "Et-Taraf" appears six times in the paragraph. The paragraph feels padded.
23. "bir aile gezi noktasıdır" - Sounds foreign. "Gezi noktası" is a calque; Turkish writers would say "gezilecek yer" or "aile gezisi için uygun bir yer". "Çocukların çeşitli hayvanları yakından görebildiği" is also a heavy participial chain before the noun arrives.
24. "çeşitli hayvanları" - Unclear. Which animals, and whether I can touch or feed them, is missing. That information is not given.
25. "Bölgenin sade ve telaşsız aile gezilerinden biridir" - Stumbles and sounds off in register. "Bölgenin" refers to what? The earlier sentences named the town and the oasis. "Sade ve telaşsız" is literary, and "aile gezilerinden biri" is vague.
26. "yakındaki Et-Taraf kırsalını ve vahanın güney kesimlerini ziyaret etmekle kolayca birleştirilebilir" - Stumbles and contradicts. The sentence is long and the verb arrives last. "Et-Taraf kırsalı" is odd, since the zoo is already in Et-Taraf. The page also says Et-Taraf is east of Hüfuf but then sends me to "the southern parts of the oasis" without explaining. What is there to see in these places is missing.
27. "Geziyi planlamadan önce yerinde teyit edin." - Contradicts itself. I cannot confirm "on the spot" before planning a trip; I would be planning from home. It also does not say what to confirm. The next line gives a different instruction ("işletmeci ile teyit edin"), so there are two different ways to confirm, one vague and one without any contact. "Teyit" is again bookish.
28. "Bölge / Et-Taraf — Hüfuf'un doğusu" - Redundant. The same location is repeated from the lede and the body, and the label "Bölge" is used for a town.

## Page 5 - El-Hamidiye Çarşısı

29. Title "El-Hamidiye Çarşısı" with the lede "Artık çarşı olarak çalışmıyor" - Misreads on first pass. The title promises a market and the tab, breadcrumb and heading all say "Çarşısı", yet the place is not a market any more. I only learned this from the second sentence of the lede, so I would have planned for the wrong thing. "Hamidiye" also suggests an Ottoman building, but no date or history is given.
30. "Hüfuf'un merkezindeki geleneksel çarşı binası, tarihî şehir merkezini geliştirme çalışmaları kapsamında restore edildi." - Stumbles and register. A noun chain ("tarihî şehir merkezini geliştirme çalışmaları") plus "kapsamında" reads like a project report. "Kapsamında" appears three times on the page.
31. "bugün binada Şark Restoranı hizmet veriyor" - Misreads. "Şark" is a bookish, Ottoman word for "the East", so I read it as "an oriental restaurant", and I could not tell if it is the actual name or a kind of cuisine. What kind of food it serves, and its hours, are missing.
32. "Bina, Kuzey er-Rifa mahallesinde, Kral Abdülaziz Yolu üzerindedir." - Misreads. In "er-Rifa", "er" is also the Turkish word for a private soldier, and "Kuzey" in front mixes Turkish with Arabic. "Kral Abdülaziz" also recalls the Ottoman sultan. A Turkish writer would more likely say "Kral Abdülaziz Caddesi".
33. "Bu çalışmalarla birlikte El-Kaysariye Çarşısı yeniden inşa edildi, Zanaatkârlar Çarşısı da kuruldu." - Unclear. Two new names are introduced without explanation. "El-Kaysariye" looks like "Kayseri". "Restore edildi" for one and "yeniden inşa edildi" for another suggests different things, and I cannot tell whether "kuruldu" means newly created. Where they are in relation to the Hamidiye is not stated.
34. "şehrin geleneksel çarşı binaları kümesine katıldı" (the text reads "şehrin kalbindeki geleneksel çarşı binaları kümesine katıldı") - Sounds foreign. "Kümesine" brings to mind "kümes" (chicken coop) before "küme" (cluster). "Katıldı" is also odd: the lede already called it a "geleneksel çarşı binası", so it was already one. "Şehrin kalbi" is a travel-brochure phrase.
35. "Günümüzde bina çalışan bir çarşı değil, tarihî bir yapıdır. Bina artık halk çarşısı olarak çalışmıyor; içinde bugün Şark Restoranı hizmet veriyor." - Contradicts and repeats. The fact that it is not a market is stated here twice in a row, and again in the lede, the status field and the FAQ, so the same fact appears five or six times. The page reads as if it were afraid I would miss it.
36. "halk çarşısı" - Sounds foreign. "Halk pazarı" is a Turkish street market, and "halk çarşısı" reads as "folk bazaar". The distinction between a "halk çarşısı" and a plain "çarşı" is never explained.
37. "Binaya ve tarihine dair daha fazla ayrıntı, burada yayımlanmadan önce güvenilir kaynaklardan teyit edilmeyi bekliyor." - Register and left hanging. This is an internal editorial note shown to readers ("not yet verified before publication"), and the sentence is a long chain. It promises history ("tarihine dair") when no history is given anywhere. It also undermines the reliability of every other fact on the page.
38. "Güncel durum / Tarihî bir yapı — artık çarşı olarak çalışmıyor; içinde Şark Restoranı hizmet veriyor" - Stumbles and left hanging. "Güncel durum" is a clumsy label, and the same fact is repeated. There are no hours or fees for the building, unlike the other four pages. If the point of a visit is the restaurant, there is no information about it, and if it is the building, it is not said whether I can go in. That is missing.
39. "El-Hamidiye Çarşısı'nın çevresindeki geleneksel çarşılarla bağlantısı nedir?" - Stumbles. "Bağlantısı nedir" is bureaucratic, and the question assumes the other markets are "çevresindeki" (around it). The answer only says all three were part of the same restoration programme, never that they are nearby.
40. FAQ order and wording - Sounds off in register. The three questions repeat the body text almost word for word, so the FAQ adds nothing new for a reader who has already read the page.
