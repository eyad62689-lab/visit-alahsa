## 3. p3#1: «El-Ahsa vahasının havasında kuşların sergilendiği bir bahçe.»

Ruled a source defect with A11-sawsan-word standing. Four readers on four wordings (ortamında, atmosferi içinde, atmosferinde, havasında) gave one residual complaint: the sentence promises an "atmosphere" and nothing concrete. That is the English's and the Arabic's shape ("A garden for displaying birds amid the atmosphere of the Al-Ahsa oasis"; «وسط أجواء واحة الأحساء»), on a page whose whole body is this one sentence. Owner question Q-A11-1 (enrich the three one-sentence pages) already covers it.

TDK's own sense of «hava» (İçinde yaşanılan ve etkisinde kalınan ortam; atmosfer), quoted in the round-3 proofread, backs «havasında». The momentary "air" parse yields no wrong fact and resolves inside the sentence. I see no wording that removes the ambiguity without a new fact. «vahanın havasını yansıtan» or «vaha ortamındaki» would restructure the sentence or say "reflecting" (a claim); «vahanın ortasında» drops "atmosphere". A fifth rewrite of one line for a source vagueness is not warranted.
