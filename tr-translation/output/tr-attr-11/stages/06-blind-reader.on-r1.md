# Blind reader notes (Turkish reader), on-r1

## Page 1 - El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği

1. Breadcrumb: "Ana sayfa / Çarşılar ve parklar / El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği Çiftlikler El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği". The trail runs together with no separators, so the farm name appears twice with "Çiftlikler" wedged between. I also expected a "Çarşılar ve parklar" section to hold bazaars and parks, not a lime farm. Misfit: the section label does not match what the page is.
2. "Bir narenciye çiftliği; ziyaretçiler ünlü El-Ahsa misket limonunu (lomi) mevsiminde burada toplar." I stumbled on "(lomi)". It is a word I could not place. It reads like a brand or a foreign word, and the page never says what it is. "Misket limonu" is a lime to me, so what the parenthesis adds is unclear. It is not explained, and I am not adding an explanation.
3. Same sentence: "narenciye" is the trade or old-fashioned word for citrus, and "ünlü" is a slightly brochure-style claim. Both are small register issues. "Turunçgil" would be the everyday word, but "narenciye" is understood.
4. The same sentence appears twice in a row (under the title and again as the first block), and on pages 2 and 3 as well. I read it twice thinking I had missed something. It looks like a duplicated block.
5. "Haziran ortasından ağustos ayına kadar, misket limonu toplama mevsimi." It is ambiguous whether "ağustos ayına kadar" includes the whole of August. The sentence is also a fragment with no verb: "Haziran ortasından ağustos sonuna kadar" would be clearer, but I cannot tell whether "end" is intended, so I am only flagging it.
6. "Her gün 16.00–21.30 (yetişme mevsimlerinde)". "Mevsimlerinde" is plural, while the line above names one season (mid-June to August). I could not tell which seasons are meant or what happens outside them (closed? open at other hours?). "Yetişme mevsimi" (growing season) also sounds odd. I would have expected "hasat" or "toplama mevsimi", as in the line above. Hours outside the season are missing.
7. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir — ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin." "Teyit edin" is bureaucratic, and "doğrulayın" or "sorun" would be more natural. More importantly, no operator, phone number or contact appears anywhere, so I have no way to confirm. The line also mentions "ücretler", but no fee is given on the page. Both are left hanging.
8. Nowhere on the page does it say where the farm is (which part of the oasis, which town). I only see the "Benden ne kadar uzak?" button. The location is missing, not something to fill in.
9. Buttons: "Bu yeri kaydedin" and "Paylaşın" use the polite plural imperative, while "Benden ne kadar uzak?" is a first-person question. "Benden uzak" is understandable, but "Bana ne kadar uzak?" or "Uzaklığım ne kadar?" would sound more natural. The register is slightly inconsistent across the three buttons.

## Page 2 - Çilek Çiftliği

1. Breadcrumb: "Ana sayfa / Çarşılar ve parklar / Çilek Çiftliği Çiftlikler Çilek Çiftliği". Same problem as page 1: a farm is filed under "Çarşılar ve parklar", and the trail is run together with the name doubled.
2. "Ukayr yolu üzerinde bir çilek çiftliği". I read "Ukayr yolu" as a road called Ukayr and had no idea what or where Ukayr is. I do not know from where the road starts, how far along it, or whether Ukayr is a town. Nothing else on the page tells me. The location is left hanging.
3. "mevsimlik çilek toplama imkânı sunar". "İmkânı sunar" is brochure and bureaucratic language, and "mevsiminde çilek toplayabilirsiniz" or "çilek toplanabilir" would be more natural. "Mevsimlik" also does not say which season, which is only answered later in the hours line.
4. "Kış ve ilkbahar mevsimlerinde 15.30–21.30". There is no "Her gün" or any day information, unlike pages 1 and 3, so I do not know whether it is open daily. I also cannot tell what happens in summer and autumn (closed, or different hours). Unlike page 1, there is no separate "En iyi zaman" line.

## Page 3 - Es-Sevsen Kuş Bahçeleri

1. "Es-Sevsen". I misread it at first as the Turkish verb form "sevsen" ("if you loved"), with an odd "Es-" in front. As a place name it looks like a pun or a typo. It is hard to tell where the word break is and how to pronounce it. No transliteration help is given.
2. The title is plural ("Kuş Bahçeleri"), but the lead says "bir bahçe". Is it one garden or several? The page contradicts itself on the name.
3. "El-Ahsa vahasının atmosferi içinde kuşların sergilendiği bir bahçe." "Atmosferi içinde" is a foreign-sounding calque and I read it twice. "Vahanın havası içinde" or "vahanın ortasında" would sound more like Turkish, but I cannot tell which is meant.
4. Same sentence: "kuşların sergilendiği" means the birds are "exhibited". It reads like captive display behind glass, and I cannot tell what I would actually do there: walk among free birds, look at cages, or watch birds in the wild. What is being promised is unclear.
5. The page has no location, no sense of which birds, and no fee, even though the disclaimer below mentions "ücretler". These are missing, not something to fill in.

## Page 4 - Et-Taraf Hayvanat Bahçesi

1. "Et-Taraf". "Taraf" is an ordinary Turkish word (side, party), so "Et-Taraf kasabası" reads to me as "the side town" or "the Taraf side". It did not parse as a place name at first. The page gives no hint.
2. "Hüfuf'un doğusundaki". I would write the city "Hufuf" or "Hofuf". "Hüfuf" with ü looks odd, and I was unsure how to pronounce it. It is used consistently, but it looks unusual.
3. "Et-Taraf kasabası, Hüfuf'un doğusunda, vahanın köyleri arasında yer alır. Kasabada Et-Taraf Hayvanat Bahçesi bulunur." The name "Et-Taraf" appears four times in two short sentences, and it feels repetitive and choppy. Also, "vahanın köyleri arasında" is unclear: a "kasaba" lying among the oasis's villages is a confusing picture.
4. "çocukların çeşitli hayvanları yakından görebildiği bir aile gezi noktası". "Aile gezi noktası" is a calque and sounds foreign ("gezi noktası" is tourism jargon). "Çeşitli hayvanlar" says nothing about which animals, which is missing.
5. "Bölgenin sade ve telaşsız aile gezilerinden biridir". It says a place "is one of the family outings". A place is not an outing, so the noun does not match. "Sade ve telaşsız" is bookish and reads like a translation. It is also a claim without anything behind it.
6. "yakındaki Et-Taraf kırsalını ve vahanın güney kesimlerini ziyaret etmekle kolayca birleştirilebilir." This is a long passive sentence that I lost track of before the verb. It reads bureaucratically. "Yakındaki Et-Taraf kırsalı" repeats the town's own name as if it were another place, and I do not know what there is to visit in the "kırsal" or the "south parts of the oasis". "Kolayca" is claimed with no distance given. Earlier the text said the town is east of Hufuf, and now it speaks of the oasis's south. I did not understand how those fit.
7. "Geziyi planlamadan önce yerinde teyit edin." "Yerinde" means on the spot, so "confirm on the spot before planning the trip" is contradictory: I cannot confirm on the spot before planning. It also does not say what to confirm. The wording differs from the standard disclaimer below ("işletmeci ile teyit edin"), so there are two different instructions, and it is left hanging.
8. The line "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir" mentions fees again, but no fee is given on this page either.

## Page 5 - El-Hamidiye Çarşısı

1. "El-Hamidiye". To a Turkish reader "Hamidiye" immediately means Sultan Abdülhamid and Ottoman-era buildings. The page never says whether there is any link, so I was left wondering. This is a misreading that the page does not resolve.
2. The title and navigation say "Çarşısı" (bazaar), and the page sits under "Gezilecek yerler / Tarihî yerler", while pages 1–4 use "Çarşılar ve parklar". The headline sells a market that the lead then says is no longer a market. The title and the content pull in different directions.
3. Lead: "Hüfuf'un merkezindeki geleneksel çarşı binası, tarihî şehir merkezini geliştirme çalışmaları kapsamında restore edildi." This is heavy: "tarihî şehir merkezini geliştirme çalışmaları kapsamında" is a bureaucratic noun chain ("within the scope of the works of developing the historic centre") and I read it twice. "Geliştirme çalışmaları" is a calque of "development works". "Kapsamında" sounds like an official notice.
4. "bugün binada Şark Restoranı hizmet veriyor." "Şark" is an old-fashioned Turkish word for "the East/Orient", with an Ottoman-bookish feel. I could not tell whether "Şark Restoranı" is a proper name or a description (an Eastern restaurant). "Hizmet veriyor" (serves/provides service) is also bureaucratic for a restaurant. "İçinde Şark Restoranı bulunuyor" or "var" would be more natural.
5. "Bina, Kuzey er-Rifa mahallesinde, Kral Abdülaziz Yolu üzerindedir." "Kuzey er-Rifa" mixes a lowercase Arabic article with "Kuzey", while elsewhere "El-" is capitalised ("El-Kaysariye", "El-Hamidiye"). The capitalisation is inconsistent. I also read "Kuzey" as a separate word at first. "Abdülaziz" is also an Ottoman sultan's name in Turkish, and only "Kral" tells me it is another person. For a city street I would expect "Caddesi" rather than "Yolu", which sounds foreign.
6. "Bu çalışmalarla birlikte El-Kaysariye Çarşısı yeniden inşa edildi, Zanaatkârlar Çarşısı da kuruldu." "El-Kaysariye" reads to me like "Kayseri", the Turkish city, with an odd ending. I stumbled on it. The spelling and pronunciation are unclear. "Yeniden inşa edildi" (rebuilt) and "kuruldu" (founded) are different actions, and the sentence does not say where these two markets are relative to this building.
7. "Böylece bina, şehrin kalbindeki geleneksel çarşı binaları kümesine katıldı." I briefly misread "kümesine" as "kümes" (a chicken coop) before I saw "küme" (cluster). "Küme" with a possessive suffix is awkward here and the whole line is overwritten ("şehrin kalbindeki"). "Böylece ... katıldı" claims a causal link: the building "joined" the group because of the works, yet it was already described as a traditional bazaar building. If it was a traditional bazaar building already, what did it join? The logic does not fit.
8. "Günümüzde bina çalışan bir çarşı değil, tarihî bir yapıdır: artık halk çarşısı olarak çalışmıyor; içinde bugün Şark Restoranı hizmet veriyor." This is the third time the same fact is stated (lead, body, "Güncel durum", FAQ), and within this sentence it is said twice ("çalışan bir çarşı değil" and "artık ... çalışmıyor"). "Günümüzde ... artık ... bugün" stacks three time words. "Halk çarşısı" is a strange phrase: "halk pazarı" is the Turkish term for a neighbourhood market, so I could not tell what "halk çarşısı" separates from an ordinary "çarşı". The phrase sounds foreign.
9. "Binaya ve tarihine dair daha fazla ayrıntı, burada yayımlanmadan önce güvenilir kaynaklardan teyit edilmeyi bekliyor." This reads like an internal editorial note that leaked into the page. "Teyit edilmeyi bekliyor" is a calque and sounds bureaucratic. It raises questions the page does not answer: which details, and why should a traveller care? The page also claims "tarihî" without giving any date or history, so the claim has no support. What is missing is any date or history; I will not guess.
10. "Güncel durum: Tarihî bir yapı — artık çarşı olarak çalışmıyor; içinde Şark Restoranı hizmet veriyor". There are no opening hours for the restaurant, and no "Ziyaret saatleri" or disclaimer line like pages 1–4. If I wanted to go and eat, or just look at the building, I would not know whether it is open. Whether the building can be visited as a sight, or only as a restaurant, is not said.
11. FAQ question: "El-Hamidiye Çarşısı'nın çevresindeki geleneksel çarşılarla bağlantısı nedir?" This is a heavy genitive-possessive chain ("Çarşısı'nın çevresindeki ... çarşılarla bağlantısı") and I had to read it twice. The question is also a bit odd and not something a traveller would ask.
12. The answer to that question ("Çarşı, ... restore edildi. Bu çalışmalarla birlikte ... yeniden inşa edildi, ... kuruldu.") does not answer it: "bağlantısı" and "çevresindeki" (nearby) suggest location or physical relation, but the answer only says they were part of the same works. How close the other markets are is missing. The restore/rebuild/found chain is also repeated verbatim from the body.
13. FAQ: "El-Hamidiye Çarşısı hâlâ çalışan bir çarşı mı?" followed by "Hayır. Çarşı tarihî bir yapıdır". The answer calls it "Çarşı" and in the next sentence "Binasında" as if the market and building were different things, so the voice switches between "the market" and "the building". Small, but it contributed to my confusion about whether the place still exists as a market.
14. The FAQ says "Hüfuf'un merkezinde, Kuzey er-Rifa mahallesinde bulunur; Kral Abdülaziz Yolu üzerindedir." This repeats the body text word for word. It offers me no landmark or way to find the place, so "where it is" stays vague even after reading it twice.
