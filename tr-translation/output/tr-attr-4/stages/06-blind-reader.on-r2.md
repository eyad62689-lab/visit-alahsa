# Blind reader notes (Turkish reader), round on-r2

## Page 1 - Et-Tahimiye Doğu Camii

1. "Köyde Et-Tahimiye Doğu Camii bulunur; cami Et-Tahimiye Birinci Camii adıyla da bilinir, eskiden ise Ebu en-Nuuş Camii denirdi." Three names in one sentence; I had to reread to see they are all the same mosque. "Doğu" and "Birinci" are never explained (east of what? first of what?). "Ebu en-Nuuş" at first looked like a person's name rather than an old name of the mosque. Not fixable without adding information; a shorter split into two sentences would read easier.
2. "Cenazeler yakındaki mezarlığa defnedilmeden önce cenaze namazı burada kılınırdı." Parses, but "Cenazeler ... defnedilmeden" with a bare nominative subject is heavy; I lost the thread before "kılınırdı". Possible: "Ölüler yakındaki mezarlığa defnedilmeden önce cenaze namazı burada kılınırdı." Also "yakındaki mezarlık" has no antecedent, so I wondered which one.
3. "Caminin tarihi yüzyıllar öncesine uzanır." On first pass "tarihi" read as the adjective (tarihî) hanging, then it resolved as "its history". Also vague: no date or century, and nothing says whether "yüzyıllar" means two or six.
4. "Köy eskiden “kırk caminin köyü” olarak ünlüydü; bugün bu camilerden ancak on bir kadarı ayakta." Terse, verbless ending ("ayakta"), a little colloquial against the surrounding register. "ancak on bir kadarı" is an odd pairing of "ancak" with "kadar". Also "eskiden" appears again, after the previous "eskiden ise" in the second sentence.
5. "Cami, iç avlunun çevresinde üç revaktan oluşan mütevazı bir çamur yapıdır." "çamur yapı" sounds foreign; for a building made of mud a Turkish reader expects "kerpiç" or "çamurdan yapılmış". "Dış duvar" in the next sentence also appears with no earlier mention of a wall.
6. "Eski Ez-Züheyri deresinin kıyısında inşa edilmiştir; Cebel el-Kare'nin yamaçları hemen yakındadır." "Eski" first read as part of the name, then as "old stream"; "Ez-Züheyri" could be a person. "Cebel el-Kare" is introduced with no label (mountain? hill? place?) except the word "yamaçları" later. Also "inşa edilmiştir" needed me to go back to find the subject (cami).
7. "2017'de cami, El-Ahsa'nın resmen tescil edilmiş tarihî camileri arasına alındı." "resmen tescil edilmiş ... arasına alındı" is redundant, and by whom (which authority) is missing. I would not fill it in, but the sentence feels incomplete.
8. "Cebel el-Kare'ye yakın konumu, camiyi bölgeyi gezenler için uğraması kolay bir durak yapar." Repeats the Cebel el-Kare proximity already given two sentences earlier; tour-brochure register compared with the rest; "uğraması kolay" is a loose phrase.
9. Missing: nothing says whether visitors may enter, or when. The lead promises a place to visit, and the body only says it is an easy stop.

## Page 2 - Ukayr Limanı Camii

1. Lead: "Kral Abdülaziz döneminde geliştirilen liman tesislerinden biri." For a moment "Abdülaziz" read as the Ottoman sultan; "Kral" corrects it, but Saudi context only arrives in the next sentence. Also "geliştirilen" here vs "inşa edilen ve geliştirilen" in the body (see 6).
2. "El-Ahsa'nın doğusundaki Arap Körfezi kıyısındadır" "Arap Körfezi" sounds unfamiliar to a Turkish reader, who normally meets "Basra Körfezi". I paused to check it was the same body of water (and not the Arabian Sea or the Gulf of Aden).
3. "Suudi Arabistan'ın doğu kıyısındaki ilk deniz limanıdır." "deniz limanı" is redundant (a liman is already a sea port here), and "ilk" is unqualified: first ever, first modern, first of what era? Left hanging.
4. "Limanın yapıları arasında tarihî bir cami bulunur." Repeats the lead almost word for word; I read it as padding.
5. "Necid'in denize açılan eski kapısında denizciler, tüccarlar ve yolcular bir zamanlar bu camide namaz kılardı." "kapısında" has no clear referent: the port is not named in this sentence, so I had to infer that "Necid'in denize açılan eski kapısı" is the port. Necid is also unexplained and far from the El-Ahsa coast mentioned just before, so the jump is abrupt.
6. "Cami, Kral Abdülaziz döneminde inşa edilen ve geliştirilen liman tesisleri arasında sayılır; kale, han, emirlik binası ve gümrük binası da bu tesislerdendir." Unclear whether the mosque was built in his time or only developed (the lead says only "geliştirilen"; the body says "inşa edilen ve geliştirilen", and it is unclear what belongs to what). "emirlik binası" reads as an "emirate building"; a governor's/administration building is what I guess, but only guess. "Cami ... sayılır; ... da bu tesislerdendir" is clumsy: "da" suggests the mosque was on the list already, then it lists other things.
7. "Ukayr, o günlerde El-Ahsa'nın ticaret damarıydı." "ticaret damarı" is a calque; I understood "artery" but it sounds foreign in Turkish ("ticaret kapısı", "ana ticaret yolu"). "o günlerde" points to no date at all. Also the shift from "Necid'in kapısı" to "El-Ahsa'nın ticaret damarı" made me wonder whose port it was.
8. "Cami, tarihî camilerin restorasyon programı kapsamında restore edildi." "restorasyon ... restore" repeats the stem. The program is unnamed and undated, and I could not tell if it is the same as the one named on other pages of the site (not on this page; I do not add that, just that the program has no name here).
9. "Bu çalışmayı Cübeyl ve Yenbu Kraliyet Komisyonu destekledi." Understandable, but I wondered why an industrial-city commission funds a mosque; the text does not say how ("destekledi" is vague: paid? carried out?). Not fillable by me.
10. "Cami, programın kapsadığı El-Ahsa camilerinden biriydi." Says again what the previous two sentences said, and the past tense "biriydi" made me think it no longer is.
11. "Eski liman, ziyaretçilerine bütün hikâyesini anlatır; cami bu hikâyenin bir parçasıdır." Personification and promotional tone; "bütün hikâyesini" overclaims, and "hikâye" was never told in the text, so the sentence hangs. "ziyaretçilerine" suggests the port is open to visitors, which is not said.
12. Missing: no date for the mosque or the restoration, no word on whether one can visit it, and no description of the building itself.

## Page 3 - Şeyh Muhammed bin Ebu Bekir el-Mulla Camii

1. Tab title: "Şeyh Muhammed bin Ebu Bekir el-Mulla Camii". "el-Mulla" first read as the ordinary word "molla" (a cleric) rather than a family name; only the lead's "El-Mulla ailesi" resolves it. Also the capitalisation differs: lowercase "el-Mulla" in the full name, capital "El-Mulla" as the family name.
2. Lead: "El-Kut mahallesinin kalbinde, yaklaşık üç yüzyıllık tarihî bir cami; ilim ehli El-Mulla ailesiyle bağlantılı." "El-Kut" first made me think of "Kut" (Kut ül-Amare) as a word; harmless. "bağlantılı" is vague (what kind of link?), though the body answers it later. "ilim ehli" is a correct but slightly bookish register.
3. "Caminin yakınında El-Mulla ailesinin evi ve dinî okulları vardır." The possessive is only on the last noun, so I read "evi ve dinî okulları" two ways (the family's house and the family's schools, or the house and some religious schools). "dinî okul" sounds like a modern religious school; for a historic setting I expected a different word. Also unclear whether they still exist ("vardır").
4. "Cami Doğu Camii adıyla da bilinir." This is the same name as the first page's mosque (Et-Tahimiye Doğu Camii); a reader of both pages will mix them up. "Doğu" is not explained here either.
5. "Çamur, çakıl ve hurma ağacı gövdelerinden yapılan cami, uzun süre bir ilim merkezi olarak görev yaptı; burada fıkıh, Kur'an tefsiri ve dil bilgisi dersleri verilirdi." Long subject before the verb; "uzun süre" gives no span; "dil bilgisi" does not say which language. Otherwise it reads.
6. "Caminin imamları kuşaktan kuşağa ilim ehli El-Mulla ailesinden geldi." "ilim ehli" is repeated from the lead, so it felt like a formula. The sentence is otherwise clear.
7. "Cami, “Prens Muhammed bin Selman Tarihî Camileri Geliştirme Projesi” kapsamında, projenin ilk aşamasında rehabilite edildi; bu aşama Hicri 1441 (2020) yılında tamamlandı." "rehabilite edildi" sounds foreign: in Turkish "rehabilite" is for people or the medical/social sense, not buildings ("onarıldı", "restore edildi"). The name stacks "Muhammed" again right after "Şeyh Muhammed", so I briefly lost track of who was who. "Hicri 1441 (2020)" parses, but the parenthesis reads like an equation and 1441 does not map to a single year.
8. "Geleneksel karakteri korundu; camide yaklaşık 125 kişi namaz kılabilir." "Geleneksel karakteri" has no stated owner: after the project sentence, "karakteri" could belong to the project or the neighbourhood before I settled on the mosque. "yaklaşık 125" looks falsely precise, and the figure is in digits while page 1 spells out "elli".
9. Missing: no word on whether visitors can enter or when; no hint of what the building looks like beyond its materials.
