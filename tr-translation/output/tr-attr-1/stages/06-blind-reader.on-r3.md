Observations (Turkish reader, single read):

1. "Burası, halkı İslam'ı ilk kabul edenler arasında olan bir vahadır."
   After standing before the arches and walls, "Burası" first read as the mosque; the predicate "bir vahadır" then forced me to re-resolve it to the oasis. Brief re-read, no misunderstanding of a word.

2. "Kemerlerinin ve duvarlarının önünde durduğunuzda..."
   The possessive has no explicit owner in the sentence; the previous sentence's subject is "geriye kalanlar", so for a moment the antecedent was unclear. It resolves to the mosque in context. Very minor.

3. Last sentence: "...El-Ahsa'daki El-Calaniye Camii, Şeyh Muhammed bin Ebu Bekir el-Mulla Camii ve El-Hubeyş Camii ile birlikte restore edildi."
   Long list with three names before the verb; I had to hold the subject (Cüvasa Camii) across it. Readable, but slightly heavy.

No misread words otherwise.
