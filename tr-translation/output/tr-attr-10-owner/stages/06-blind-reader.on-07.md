# Blind reader notes (Turkish reader), on-07

## Page 1 - El-Kaysariye Çarşısı

1. Breadcrumb: "Ana sayfa / Çarşılar ve parklar / El-Kaysariye Çarşısı Geleneksel çarşılar El-Kaysariye Çarşısı". The trail runs straight into a category label ("Geleneksel çarşılar") and then repeats the page name, so I could not tell where the trail ends and the heading begins. The section is "Çarşılar ve parklar" but the category is "Geleneksel çarşılar"; two different labels for what looks like the same thing.

2. Browser tab: "El-Kaysariye Çarşısı — Visit Al-Ahsa". The site name is English and spelled "Al-Ahsa", while the whole page says "El-Ahsa". Same spelling clash on page 2's tab.

3. Subtitle: "İki yüzyıldan eski tarihî bir çarşı; El-Ahsa'nın en eski halk ve geleneksel çarşılarından biri, zanaat ve yerel ürünler için bir gezi noktası." Sounds foreign. "halk ve geleneksel çarşılar" reads as a calque: "halk çarşısı" does not mean what is intended, and I had to read it twice to see that "halk" and "geleneksel" are two adjectives. "bir gezi noktası" is also not how a Turkish writer would say it. The sentence is a stack of three appositions with no verb.

4. "UNESCO Dünya Mirası alanının bileşenlerinden biri · 1563-005". "bileşen" is bureaucratic here. Which UNESCO site it is part of is not said (missing). "1563-005" is a bare code with no label; it parses like a date or a phone fragment, and I cannot tell what it is.

5. Buttons "+ Bu yeri kaydedin" and "Paylaşın". Grammatical, but the formal imperative sounds stiff, and "yeri kaydedin" does not say what is saved or where. The "+" glued to the text reads oddly.

6. Names on first pass. "Kaysariye" reads as "Kayseri" before anything else. "El-" opens almost every name (El-Ahsa, El-Kaysariye, El-Umran), and in Turkish "el" is "hand", so the eye keeps catching on it. "Er-Rifa" begins with "er" (soldier/man). "Hüfuf" is pronounceable but looks like a typo for "Hufuf". None of these is explained or glossed.

7. "Hüfuf'taki El-Kaysariye Çarşısı, Arap Körfezi bölgesinin en eski ve en köklü çarşılarından biridir; geçmişi iki yüzyıldan fazla geriye uzanır." The scale shifts. The subtitle says one of El-Ahsa's oldest, here it is one of the oldest in the whole Gulf region, and the only support is "two centuries". "en eski ve en köklü" with "iki yüzyıl" feels inflated, and the claim is left hanging. Also "iki yüzyıldan eski" in the subtitle against "iki yüzyıldan fazla" here are the same claim in two wordings.

8. "Çarşının mimarisi kendine özgüdür." Raises a question the page never answers: what is distinctive about it? The next sentences describe shops and smells, not architecture.

9. "Sıkı sıkıya dizilmiş dükkânlar sizi geçitlerden geçirir." Sounds foreign. "sıkı sıkıya" suggests holding tight (as in "sıkı sıkıya sarılmak"), and "sizi geçitlerden geçirir" is a causative that makes the shops the active subject, which is an unnatural Turkish image. I had to read it twice.

10. "Bu geçitler baharat ve parfüm kokar, bakırla parıldar." Readable, but "bakırla parıldar" introduces copper, and copper is never mentioned again among the things you can buy. Left hanging. "parfüm" is also a slightly modern word for a souq.

11. "El-Ahsa'da geleneksel alışverişin kalbi burada atar." Brochure cliché; register jumps from descriptive to promotional.

12. "Çarşı, ünlü El-Ahsa biştini, yani itibarın ve büyük günlerin pelerinini satın almak için ideal bir yerdir." Several problems. "bişt" is a word I could not place; the gloss "pelerin" gives me a cape, not a formal cloak. "itibarın ve büyük günlerin pelerini" is a literal-sounding epithet that no Turkish writer would produce; it is also bookish. "ideal bir yer" is brochure-shouty. "bişt" also resembles "pişt" (dialect for "cooked"), so the spelling is a trap for pronunciation. The word appears in three forms (biştini / Biştin / bişti) and each reads slightly differently.

13. "“El-Ahsa dokuması”, altın ya da gümüş kaplamalı sırma ipliklerle yapılan el işlemesinin titizliğiyle ayırt edilir." Stumbles: the subject is "dokuma" (weaving), but what is described is "el işlemesi" (embroidery), so I could not tell if it is a weave or an embroidery. "kaplamalı sırma" is redundant (sırma is already metal thread). The long chain of genitives before the verb "ayırt edilir" made me lose the thread. It is also unclear whether "El-Ahsa dokuması" is the same thing as the bişt just mentioned.

14. "Bu zanaatı El-Ahsa aileleri kuşaktan kuşağa aktarmıştır." "Bu zanaat" has an unclear antecedent: the weaving, the embroidery, or the bişt-making. Otherwise fluent.

15. "Biştin yanı sıra hurma yaprağı ve çömlek işi yerel zanaatlar, her çeşit hurma, kahve ve baharat bulabilirsiniz." Starting a sentence with "Biştin" (reads like an odd standalone word) is jarring. "hurma yaprağı ve çömlek işi yerel zanaatlar" is a noun pile that I read twice: is it palm leaves as an item, or palm-leaf work? The whole list is held up until the final verb.

16. Heading "El-Kaysariye Çarşısı ziyaret saatleri" followed by "Ziyaret saatleri ve ücretler aşağıdaki ziyaret bilgileri kartında yer alır; çarşının en iyi saatleri sabah ve akşamdır." The heading promises hours and the paragraph only points to a card below. "aşağıdaki ziyaret bilgileri kartında yer alır" is bureaucratic; "çarşının en iyi saatleri" is awkward (the best time of day, not hours of the souq).

17. Card label "Bölge" with "Hüfuf, Er-Rifa mahallesi". The label says region, but the value is a town and a neighbourhood. Minor mismatch; "Konum" would have read more naturally.

18. "08.00–12.00 ve 15.30–22.00 (Cuma sabah ve ikindi sonrası)". The parenthesis is unclear: is Friday different, and if so how? "sabah ve ikindi sonrası" repeats the normal pattern, so I cannot tell what the exception is. "ikindi sonrası" is also vague when the afternoon opening is given as 15.30. What happens at Friday midday is not said (missing).

19. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir — ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin." "işletmeci" for a souq made me ask who the operator is; nothing on the page says. "teyit edin" is bookish; "doğrulayın" or "sorun" is what I would expect.

20. Button "Benden ne kadar uzak?". Oddly casual against the rest of the page, and I could not tell what it does (needs my location?). Nothing on the page explains it.

21. FAQ: "08.00'den 12.00'ye ve 15.30'dan 22.00'ye kadar açıktır; Cuma günleri sabah ve ikindi sonrası açıktır." The second clause adds nothing: it says Fridays are open in the morning and after ikindi, which is what the first clause already says. The Friday question from item 18 is not answered.

22. FAQ: "Ünlü El-Ahsa bişti, hurma yaprağı ve çömlek işi yerel zanaatlar, her çeşit hurma, kahve ve baharat." A fragment with no verb. "El-Ahsa bişti" now reads as "bişti" (a verb-like form, "pişti"), a third spelling of the same word.

23. FAQ: "El-Kaysariye Çarşısı kaç yıllıktır?" The question asks for a number; the answer only says "iki yüzyıldan fazla", with no date or founding period. It also restates "en eski ve en köklü" a third time.

24. Footer link "Tüm çarşılar ve parklar →" and the category "Çarşılar ve parklar". A traditional market in a "parklar" section is a mismatch I noticed immediately; nothing explains the grouping.

## Page 2 - Medeniyetler Diyarı

1. Name "Cebel el-Kare". "Kare" reads as the Turkish word for "square" (as in "kare"), so "Cebel el-Kare'nin yanında" first parsed as "next to the square". "Cebel" is also unfamiliar and is never glossed as "mountain" (missing). The suffixes "Kare'nin / Kare'deki / Kare'yi" keep reinforcing the ordinary-word reading.

2. Title "Medeniyetler Diyarı". "Diyar" is Ottoman-bookish and "Medeniyetler" is abstract and grand. I could not tell if this is a proper name or a descriptive title, and I cannot tell what is actually inside.

3. Tab title: "Medeniyetler Diyarı — Visit Al-Ahsa". English site name and "Al-Ahsa" spelling, against the page's "El-Ahsa".

4. Breadcrumb "Çarşılar ve parklar / Medeniyetler Diyarı Parklar ve bahçeler Medeniyetler Diyarı". Same run-together category label and repeated name as on page 1. Category "Parklar ve bahçeler" does not fit a heritage village and exhibition, and the footer link "Tüm çarşılar ve parklar →" repeats the mismatch.

5. Subtitle: "Cebel el-Kare'nin yanında, bölgenin medeniyet aşamalarını gözler önüne seren bir miras köyü ve sergi." Sounds foreign. "miras köyü" is a calque of "heritage village" (a Turkish writer would say something like "kültürel miras köyü" or "geleneksel köy"). "medeniyet aşamalarını gözler önüne seren" is brochure rhetoric and bookish. "sergi" with no article or qualifier: a permanent exhibition? Which period or stages? Not said (missing).

6. "Medeniyetler Diyarı, Cebel el-Kare'nin eteğinde bir miras köyü ve sergidir." The subtitle said "yanında" (beside); this says "eteğinde" (at the foot); the card says "yanı" and the FAQ "yanındadır". The wording drifts between "beside" and "at the foot of", and I cannot tell whether those are the same position.

7. "Köy ve sergi, bölgenin medeniyet aşamalarının izini sürer." A noun chain of genitives ("bölgenin medeniyet aşamalarının izini") that I had to reread. "izini sürer" suggests tracking/hunting rather than presenting. Is the exhibition inside the village or a separate thing? Not clear. The previous sentence says the same thing again.

8. "Köy, Cebel el-Kare'yi ve mağaralarını ziyaret etme deneyimiyle bütünleşmiştir." This stumbled: "ziyaret etme deneyimiyle bütünleşmiştir" is a long nominal chain before the verb and sounds translated. "mağaralarını" introduces caves for the first time here without any setup.

9. "Bu bütünleşmeyi rotalar, tesisler ve manzara noktaları sağlar." Abstract. Which routes, which facilities, which viewpoints? I cannot picture what I would actually do there. "Bu bütünleşmeyi" refers back to a vague idea.

10. "Ziyaret saatleri ve ücretler aşağıdaki ziyaret bilgileri kartında yer alır; biletler Cebel el-Kare'deki mağara deneyimini de kapsar." Same bureaucratic pointer as page 1. "mağara deneyimi" is a calque. The ticket covers the "cave experience" here, "Cebel el-Kare'yi" on the card, so it is unclear whether the ticket gets me into a cave, the mountain, or a guided visit.

11. Card "Bölge": "El-Umran şehri, Hüfuf'un doğusu — Cebel el-Kare'nin yanı". "Umran" is an ordinary (old) Turkish word meaning "prosperity/civilisation", so "El-Umran şehri" first read as a descriptive phrase, not a place. The value is a noun fragment joined by a dash rather than a location in a sentence. Label "Bölge" again for what is a town.

12. "Her gün 08.00–23.00 (tatillerde uzatılabilir)". "tatillerde" - which holidays: public, school, religious? Not said. "uzatılabilir" is a bureaucratic, non-committal passive, and there is no hint by how much. Left hanging.

13. "Yetişkinler için 50 Suudi riyali (SAR), çocuklar için 10 SAR — tek bilet Medeniyetler Diyarı'nı ve Cebel el-Kare'yi kapsar". "SAR" is an abbreviation I had to parse. The age limit for "çocuklar" is missing. Dash plus long clause in a price field reads heavy.

14. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir — ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin." Same as page 1: who is the "işletmeci"? Not said. "teyit edin" is bookish.

15. Button "Benden ne kadar uzak?". Same casual register as page 1; no hint what happens when I click.

16. FAQ "Medeniyetler Diyarı nedir?" Answer: "Cebel el-Kare'nin eteğinde bir miras köyü ve sergidir; bölgenin medeniyet aşamalarının izini sürer." This is the page's own subtitle repeated almost word for word, so after reading the whole page I still cannot say what I would see or do there.

17. FAQ "Medeniyetler Diyarı'na giriş ücreti ne kadar?" Answer: "...biletler Cebel el-Kare'deki mağara deneyimini de kapsar." Third repetition of the ticket claim; the same item-10 ambiguity (cave vs mountain) remains.
