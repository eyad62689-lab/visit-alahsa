# Blind reader notes (Turkish reader), on-r3

## Page 1 - El-Ahsa gezi rehberi: dünyanın en büyük vahasında yapılacaklar

1. Map compass "N ↑" — sounds foreign. A Turkish map says "K" (Kuzey). "N" made me stop for a second. Suggest "K ↑".
2. "Cebel el-Kare" (title, map, many headings) — misreads on first pass: "Kare" is the ordinary Turkish word for "square", so the first time I read it as "Square Mountain". The hyphenated form helps a bit, but the word is still there. Nothing to fix in the text; I just note that it misread.
3. "Ukayr — Suudi Arabistan'ın doğu kıyısındaki ilk deniz limanı" — unclear. "İlk" can mean the historically first port, or the first one you reach on the coast. The page never says which. The same phrase comes back as a route heading in Chapter 6, so it matters more there.
4. "Ayn en-Necm Parkı ... bu pınar, El-Ahsa'nın şifalı kükürtlü pınarlarının en ünlüsüdür." — slight stumble. The sentence starts with the park, then "bu pınar" switches to the spring, so I had to look back to see what "bu" points to. The page also moves between "artezyen kaynağı" (stats) and "pınar" (here and in the sources) with no explanation; I could not tell whether they are the same thing.
5. "El-Kaysariye Çarşısı — 200 yılı aşkın geçmiş / İki yüzyıldan eski tarihî bir çarşı; El-Ahsa'nın en eski halk ve geleneksel çarşılarından biri, zanaat ve yerel ürünler için bir gezi noktası." — repetitive. The label and the first clause say the same age twice, and "tarihî" and "eski" pile up. "halk ve geleneksel çarşıları" sounds translated; "halk çarşısı" is not a category a Turkish writer would use next to "geleneksel". "bir gezi noktası" at the end is flat.
6. "Hicri beşinci yüzyıl / El-Calaniye Camii ... Uyuniler Devleti dönemine ait bir cami" — I can place "Hicri beşinci yüzyıl", but most readers will want a rough Gregorian century next to it, and none is given. I could not place "Uyuniler Devleti" at all; it is a name I had never heard and the page does not say what or when it was.
7. Section 05 (Kasr İbrahim, Cüvasa Camii, Ukayr) repeats the map texts from section 04 word for word. As a reader I noticed I was reading the same three paragraphs a second time.
8. "Toprağın ve çarşının hafızası" — a bit bookish and foreign ("the memory of the soil and of the market"). The singular "çarşının" also clashes with the plural "çarşılar" in the next sentence.
9. "Suudi Arabistan'ın Doğu Bölgesi'nde: kumun altında su, mağaralarla dolu bir dağ ve nesillerdir süregelen çarşılar." — a verbless fragment after a colon. It reads fine as a brochure line, but it is a little shouty next to the calmer sentences after it.
10. "Kayıtlı yerleriniz: 0 Bu yerlerden rota oluşturun" — left hanging. With zero saved places, the button asks me to build a route "from these places", and there are none.
11. "Cüvasa Camii ... Medine'den sonra cuma namazının kılındığı ilk yer." — clear, but "yer" after "cami" is a little loose. "ilk cami" would read more naturally. Minor.

## Chapter 6 routes

1. "Bir gün: Hüfuf'un kaleleri ile doğunun en eski camileri arasında" — unclear and misreads. This is the first time the page mentions "Hüfuf", and nothing tells me what it is (a town? the old city?). "Kaleleri" is plural, but the page has only shown me one fort (Kasr İbrahim). I could not tell what "doğunun" means: the east of what, Al-Ahsa, Arabia, or the Islamic world?
2. Every route shows "[Rota] ..." items (Tarihî Hüfuf Rotası, Cebel el-Kare ve Cüvasa Rotası, ...) that the page never explains. On the one-day plan, I get two whole "Rota" items in a single day, and I cannot tell how long each one takes or whether both fit in a day.
3. "İki gün: eski şehir, sonra doğu köyleri" — unclear. "Eski şehir" is never tied to a name. I guess it is Hüfuf, but the page does not say so. The only village named anywhere is El-Battaliye (section 05), so "doğu köyleri" plural promises more than the list shows.
4. "Şeyh Muhammed bin Ebu Bekir el-Mulla Camii" — a long name, but it reads correctly. No problem. Next to it, "El-Cebri Camii" appears here for the first time with no description.
5. "Üç gün: son durak Suudi Arabistan'ın doğu kıyısındaki ilk deniz limanı" — stumbles. The heading uses a description instead of the name. I had to wait for "Ukayr Kıyı Rotası" under Day 3 to understand it was Ukayr. The "ilk" ambiguity from the map also comes back (first ever, or first along the coast?).
6. "El-Asfar Gölü ve Millî Park Rotası" — "Millî Park" with no name. Which national park? The page has not mentioned one before.
7. "Göle ulaşmak için dört çekerli araç gerekir." — a useful and clear sentence, but its placement left it hanging. In the three-day plan it comes after Day 3 (Ukayr), so it reads as if it belonged to the coast day and not the lake day. Placing it under Day 2 would avoid that.
8. "Bir gün: Hüfuf'un tarihî çarşılarında" — fine. But El-Kaysariye Çarşısı, which the page presented as a highlight, is not listed by name in the market routes. I wondered whether it is part of "Tarihî Hüfuf Rotası" or not.
9. "[Gezilecek yer] Kadınlar Çarşısı" — unclear for a visitor. "Women's Market": can men or mixed groups go in? The page does not say.
10. "İki gün: çarşılar, sonra Cebel el-Kare'de çömlekçilik" — contradicts or is left hanging. Pottery is mentioned nowhere else on the page; the Cebel el-Kare texts only talk about caves and temperature. I could not tell whether I would watch potters, make pottery or shop. The route item under Day 2 ("Cebel el-Kare ve Cüvasa Rotası") does not mention pottery either.
11. "El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği" — reads naturally; "misket limonu" is the right Turkish word. No problem.
12. Headings "Bir gün: / İki gün: / Üç gün:" plus a phrase — the pattern is consistent and easy to scan. No problem.

## Companion paragraphs

1. [family] "Gezinize bir aile parkı ekleyin: Cüvasa Camii'nin yanındaki Cüvasa Parkı ya da yürüyüş ve gezinti için Kral Abdullah Çevre Parkı." — "aile parkı" sounds a bit like a category label. "yürüyüş ve gezinti" are near-synonyms, so the pair adds nothing. "Çevre Parkı" sounds bureaucratic ("Environment Park") but I accept it as a name. When this shows under a route that does not include Cüvasa (the nature routes), "Cüvasa Camii'nin yanındaki" points to a place I am not visiting.
2. [friends] "Çöl gezilerini sevenler için: Hüfuf'un güneydoğusundaki Cebel el-Erba bir kamp ve gezinti noktasıdır." — slight stumble. "...için:" sets up a recommendation, but then a full descriptive sentence follows. Either "Çöl gezilerini sevenler için Hüfuf'un güneydoğusundaki Cebel el-Erba iyi bir kamp ve gezinti noktası." or drop the colon. "gezinti noktası" repeats the flat "gezi noktası" phrasing from the market text.
3. [solo] "El-Ahsa Müzesi daha derinlemesine bilgi edinmek isteyenlere bölgenin tarihini ve eski eserlerini sunar." — heavy word order: the subject, then a long dative phrase, then the object. I lost the thread until "sunar". "Daha derinlemesine" compared with what? Also, nothing connects it to travelling alone. Suggest "Daha derinlemesine bilgi edinmek isteyenler için El-Ahsa Müzesi, bölgenin tarihini ve eski eserlerini sunuyor."
4. [solo, Hüfuf] "Tarihî Hüfuf Rotası'nda kale ile çarşı arasını yürüyerek gezersiniz." — unclear: which fort and which market? Both are singular and unnamed. Earlier the heading said "kaleleri" (plural). The aorist "gezersiniz" reads as a plain statement of fact rather than a suggestion. That is fine, but it shifts register from the imperatives around it.
