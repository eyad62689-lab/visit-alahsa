## The deciding question

Did the reader stumble on the round-1 edit itself? No.

- **Sentence 3 («Parkın hikâyesi Hicri 1382 (1962) yılında bir projeyle başladı»).** The round-1 stumble (r1 p2#6) was the long relative
  clause before «başladı». r2 #7 quotes the shortened sentence and loses no thread and no subject. What it says is about the date style
  («Hicri 1382 (1962)» parses oddly; «Hicri 1382 (Miladi 1962)» suggested) and that «Parkın hikâyesi ... başladı» is somewhat literary.
  The first is the open P11 and the same remark as r1 p2#6 (the form is the calibration form; the suggestion would settle P11, so it is
  not applied); the second is the English's «a story that began». Not a misread.
- **Sentence 4 («Proje, ... aitti ve ... amaçlıyordu»).** r2 #8 reads «Proje» as the subject of both verbs («A project that “belonged”
  then “aimed”»), understands «belonged to the ministry», and understands the aim (the sands, the villages). Its complaints are «aitti» is an
  odd way to say the ministry ran the project (r1 p2#7, ruled), the genitive chain «Suudi Arabistan'ın Tarım Bakanlığına» (A7-ministry:
  country before the ministry, A6-ministry), and a «thin join with ve» (taste: one subject, two past predicates). Its remedy
  «yürütülüyordu» adds a verb the English lacks («as a Ministry of Agriculture project»; the Arabic «launched» is not the English).
  Not a misread; the edit stands.
- The rest of the page (sentences 1, 2, 5–10) is unchanged from stage 05; every stumble on it repeats an r1 ruling, is the English's
  figure, or is frame.

The precedent (tr-attr-6 r2 addendum; tr-attr-3, -4, -5) closes the round with an addendum and no edit. No `07-judge.r2.json` is written.
