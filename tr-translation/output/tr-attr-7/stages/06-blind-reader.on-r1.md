## Page 1 - El-Ahsa Millî Parkı

1. Tab title «El-Ahsa Millî Parkı — Visit Al-Ahsa»: the page says «El-Ahsa» but the site name beside it says «Al-Ahsa». I took them for two different spellings of the same place, which looks careless on a Turkish page. I would not know whether they are the same site or place. Nothing to suggest, only the mismatch.

2. «Ana sayfa / Çarşılar ve parklar / El-Ahsa Millî Parkı Parklar ve bahçeler El-Ahsa Millî Parkı»: the line runs on without a break, and the category appears twice under two different names («Çarşılar ve parklar» and «Parklar ve bahçeler»). I did not know which one this park belongs to. The title is also repeated twice in a row. A park under «Çarşılar» (bazaars) also reads oddly at first.

3. Lead: «Kumları durdurmak için 1962'de başlatılan bir projeden doğan yapay orman; milyonlarca ılgın ağacı, gölgelikli oturma alanları ve yürüyüş yolları.» There is no verb. It is a list after a semicolon, and I took a moment to see that the second half lists what the park contains. It also says 1962, while the body says «Hicri 1382 (1962)». That is consistent, but I read the date twice.

4. «Millî Parkı» in the title: for a Turkish reader «Millî Park» means a protected natural area. The page then calls it an artificial forest with family facilities. I expected something else, but the page does not explain the name. I can only note the mismatch.

5. «El-Ahsa Millî Parkı, vahanın kuzeydoğusunda, El-Umran şehrinde uzanır.» It reads fine. «El-Umran şehrinde» is a bit bare, with no hint of where it is in relation to anything I know, but it does not stumble.

6. «Park, Cebel el-Kare'ye yakındır.» The sentence is abrupt and short after a long one. I do not know what Cebel el-Kare is, and the page does not say. It is not clear whether it is a mountain, a site or a town, and «yakın» gives no distance. This is left hanging and missing information; I will not guess. As it stands it adds nothing for planning.

7. «Parkın hikâyesi Hicri 1382 (1962) yılında bir projeyle başladı.» «Hicri 1382 (1962)» parses oddly: I had to stop and work out that the bracket is the Gregorian year. «Hicri 1382 (Miladi 1962)» would have worked. «Parkın hikâyesi ... başladı» is also somewhat literary compared with the plain sentences around it.

8. «Proje, Suudi Arabistan'ın Tarım Bakanlığına aitti ve vahadaki köylere doğru ilerleyen kumları durdurmayı amaçlıyordu.» «Tarım Bakanlığına aitti» (belonged to the ministry) is an odd way to say the ministry ran or carried out the project, and the genitive chain «Suudi Arabistan'ın Tarım Bakanlığına» is clumsy. A project that «belonged» then «aimed» also has a thin join with «ve». Suggestion: «Proje, Suudi Arabistan Tarım Bakanlığı'nca yürütülüyordu ve ... amaçlıyordu.»

9. «Yaklaşık 4.500 hektarlık bir alana milyonlarca ağaç dikildi; bunların çoğu ılgın ağacıydı.» Clean. The «4.500» with a dot is read correctly as four thousand five hundred.

10. «Alanda yirmi kilometre boyunca uzanan bir kuşak da bulunur.» I lost the thread here. A belt of what? Trees, dunes, a road? The «da» says it is in addition to the trees, but the noun «kuşak» has no content, and I could not tell whether it is part of the 4.500 hectares. The next sentence then says «kum bariyerleri», which I guess refers to this belt, but the page never says so. Missing information; I cannot fill it in.

11. «Zamanla kum bariyerleri önce gür, yapay bir ormana, sonra gölgelikli oturma alanları, yürüyüş yolları ve aile tesisleri olan halka açık bir parka dönüştü.» Long, and the verb comes at the very end after two comma lists; I lost the thread before «dönüştü». «Kum bariyerleri» appears here for the first time and had not been introduced (see 10). «aile tesisleri» sounds like a literal rendering (facilities of a family?); «ailelere yönelik tesisler» would read more naturally. Splitting into two sentences would help.

12. «Park bugün Millî Bitki Örtüsünü Geliştirme ve Çölleşmeyle Mücadele Merkezinin gözetimindedir.» The long institution name runs straight into the possessive suffix with no apostrophe, so I could not see where the name ends and the sentence begins: «Merkezinin» should be «Merkezi'nin». The name is heavy and sounds like a translated title rather than an established Turkish body. The sentence also makes me look for the verb at the end.

13. «Merkez, parkın rehabilitasyon projelerini sürdürüyor.» The tense switches to present continuous, while the rest uses the aorist (-ır) and the past, so the register wobbles; «sürdürür» or «yürütür» would match. «Rehabilitasyon» sounds foreign and technical here; «iyileştirme» or «onarım» would be plainer. Also «Merkez» is short for the long name two sentences back, which is fine but takes a moment.

14. «Park sınırları içinde, tuza dayanıklı yerli türlerden iki milyon ağaçla Cüvasa Ormanı hayata geçirildi.» Several stumbles. «Cüvasa Ormanı» could be read as an ordinary phrase; I took a moment to see that it is a name. «hayata geçirildi» is used for projects and plans, not for a forest, and sounds like a calque; «oluşturuldu» would work. It is a passive with no agent, so I could not tell whether the Merkez did it, and there is no date, which is odd after «bugün». The phrase «tuza dayanıklı yerli türlerden iki milyon ağaçla» is a long stretch before the verb. It is also not clear whether the two million trees are part of the «milyonlarca» above.

15. Visit card, «Bölge: El-Umran — vahanın kuzeydoğusu»: reads fine. It only repeats the opening of the body.

16. Visit card, «Ziyaret saatleri: Her gün 06.00–18.00» and «Ücretler: Giriş ücretsiz»: clean. The label «Ücretler» is plural for a single fact, a small oddity only.

17. «Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir — ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin.» «İşletmeci» (operator) was never introduced; the body names a Merkez that supervises, so I did not know whom to ask, and the page gives no contact. «teyit edin» is a bit stiff/foreign, while «doğrulayın» would be plainer. Missing: how to reach them.

18. «[button] + Bu yeri kaydedin [button] Paylaşın»: the «+» and the polite plural imperative are fine, but «kaydedin» does not say where it saves to. Minor.

19. «[button] Benden ne kadar uzak?»: this is spoken in the first person, while the other buttons are imperatives addressed to me, so the voice changes. «Uzak» is also unclear because the subject (the park) is not named. Suggestion: «Parka ne kadar uzaktayım?» or «Bana uzaklığı».

20. Back link «Tüm çarşılar ve parklar →»: consistent with the breadcrumb but not with the second category name «Parklar ve bahçeler» (see 2). Otherwise clean.

21. Overall: the facts hold together without contradiction, but the page reads as a translation in several places (hayata geçirildi, aile tesisleri, rehabilitasyon, aitti). The two open ends I could not resolve are the «kuşak» (10) and the closeness to Cebel el-Kare (6).
