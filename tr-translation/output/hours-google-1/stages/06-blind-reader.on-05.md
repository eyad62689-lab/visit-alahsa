Blind reader observations (Turkish reader), snippets 1-12

1. Snippet 3, "En iyi zaman akşam saatleridir: dükkânlar açılır ve çarşı canlanır." Unclear. The sentence before says the market is already open 08.00-11.30, so "dükkânlar açılır" (shops open) makes it sound as if the shops are shut in the morning, or that the evening is when they open. I read it twice. No wording suggested; the fact behind it is not in the text (whether the morning shops are closed or just quiet).

2. Snippet 3, "Giriş ücretsizdir." Hangs a little. Nothing in snippets 1-2 mentions an entrance fee for a market, so it sounds like a stray fact. Also it is the only place a fee is mentioned. It is not wrong, just unanchored.

3. Snippet 3, "08.00–11.30, akşamları 16.00–23.00 saatleri arasında açıktır". "Arasında" with a dash range is doubled (the dash already means "between"). Also 16.00 is called "akşam"; to a Turkish ear 16.00 is still öğleden sonra/ikindi, so "akşam" feels early. The next clause, "Cuma günleri ise yalnızca akşam 16.00–23.00 saatleri arasında açılır", repeats it. Mild.

4. Snippet 3, "açıktır ... açılır". Verb switches within one sentence (state, then event) for the same fact. Not wrong, but the first-pass reading is "is it open or does it open".

5. Snippets 1-3, "Cumartesi–Perşembe". Parses, but a Turkish reader thinks Monday-first, so the range "Saturday-Thursday" briefly looks like it wraps oddly before I work out it means six days, Friday excluded. The en dash gives no help. Fine once you notice Cuma is listed separately.

6. Snippets 6 and 7, "Geçici olarak kapalı" under the label "Saatler". Left hanging: no hours, no date, no reason, no idea of when to check again. The card reads as a hours line that contains no hours. Missing information, not a wording problem.

7. Snippet 8, "Rotada Osmanlı garnizonunun konuşlandığı bir kale, El-Ahsa halkının Kral Abdülaziz'e biat ettiği ev, El-Ahsa'nın ilk devlet okulu ve hâlâ bütün canlılığıyla yaşayan üstü kapalı bir çarşı var." Long chain of three relative clauses before the list closes; I lost the thread by "okul". "Hâlâ bütün canlılığıyla yaşayan" is brochure-ish and a bit florid ("bütün canlılığıyla" + "yaşayan"). The stops are not named here (kale, ev, okul), so the reader cannot match them to names until later.

8. Snippet 8, "Hüfuf'un eski şehrinde ... El-Ahsa halkının". Hüfuf and El-Ahsa appear as two names with no link; I do not know whether Hüfuf is a town inside El-Ahsa or a different place. Not explained in any of the snippets.

9. Snippet 8, "en iyisi sabah ya da öğleden sonranın geç saatlerinde yürüyerek gezmektir". Contradicts snippets 4-5: Kasr İbrahim closes at 16.00, so "öğleden sonranın geç saatleri" (late afternoon) means the fortress is shut. It also does not fit Friday. "Öğleden sonranın geç saatlerinde" is also clumsy (two genitive chains).

10. Snippet 8, "Cuma günleri ise Kasr İbrahim kapalıdır, El-Kaysariye Çarşısı da yalnızca akşam açılır; bu yüzden çarşıyı o gün akşama bırakın." Clear in meaning. "Ise" has nothing to contrast with in the sentence before, and "akşama bırakın" is slightly colloquial next to the formal "kapalıdır". The "da yalnızca" reads a bit like "also only". Acceptable.

11. Snippet 8, last sentence, "Beyt el-Biat ile El-Emiriye Okulu şu anda geçici olarak kapalıdır." Raises the question I cannot answer: with two of the four stops closed and the fortress closed on Friday, what do I actually do on this route? Nothing offered. "Şu anda" on a website has no date, so I do not know how old the claim is. "Beyt el-Biat": "biat" is an ordinary Turkish word, so the name reads fine and even tells me what the house is.

12. Snippet 9, "Kalenin hemen yakınındaki". "Hemen yakınındaki" is redundant ("yakınındaki" or "hemen yanındaki"). Snippet 12 has just "Kalenin yakınındaki", so the two articles differ.

13. Snippet 9, "Kale Cuma günleri açılmaz." / snippet 11 same. "Açılmaz" is a door verb and sounds like "never opens" (habitual) rather than "is closed on Fridays". Elsewhere (4, 5, 8) "kapalıdır" is used. Different register for the same fact, mild.

14. Snippet 9 vs 11, "Öğleye doğru ışık kalenin mimarisini ve iç avlusunu en iyi hâliyle gösterir" vs "Mimariyi ve iç avluyu en iyi gündüz görürsünüz". Two different pieces of advice about the same fortress: toward noon vs simply daytime. The second is empty (the fortress is open only in the daytime anyway). The first is slightly literary ("ışık ... gösterir"). Not a contradiction but the reader cannot tell which to follow.

15. Snippet 9/12, "Burası El-Mulla ailesinin evidir. Evi El-Ahsa kadısı Şeyh Abdurrahman bin Ömer el-Mulla Hicri 1203 (1788) yılında yaptırdı." "Evidir ... Evi" is a repetition. "El-Mulla" with capital after "aile" and "el-Mulla" lower-case inside the full name looks like an inconsistency to a reader. The long name chain "El-Ahsa kadısı Şeyh Abdurrahman bin Ömer el-Mulla" with no commas was heavy; I had to read it twice to see who is the judge and who the sheikh. "Hicri 1203 (1788) yılında" is fine.

16. Snippet 9/12, "El-Ahsa halkı 1913'te burada Kral Abdülaziz'e biat edip Suudi devletine katıldı." "Burada" plus "biat edip katıldı" is understandable but dense; "Suudi devleti" in 1913 is a slightly odd label for a reader (which state, what is "joined"). I would not stumble in a museum text, but here it is unexplained.

17. Snippet 9/12, "Ev bugün bir müzedir; ancak şu anda geçici olarak kapalıdır." "Bugün" and "şu anda" stack, and "bugün" can be read as "today it is a museum, only today closed". "Geçici olarak" and "şu anda" say the same twice. No end date.

18. Snippet 12, "Ardından mahalleden doğuya ilerleyin; sıradaki durak El-Emiriye Okulu." Direction "doğuya" is given with no distance or landmark; "mahalleden" (from the quarter) is vague as a starting point. Missing, not wrong.

19. Snippet 12, "Tarihî binası bugün kültür evi olarak hizmet veriyor; okul da şu anda geçici olarak kapalıdır." Self-contradiction on first pass: it serves as a cultural house, yet is closed. "Okul da" is also confusing: the school is a historic building that is now a cultural house, so who is "okul"? Also the shift from "hizmet veriyor" to "kapalıdır" (colloquial to formal). I read it twice.

20. Snippet 12, "1937'de açılan bu okul El-Ahsa'da örgün eğitim veren ilk devlet okuludur." Fine, and consistent with snippet 8 ("ilk devlet okulu"). "Örgün eğitim veren" is a bit bureaucratic but clear.

21. Snippet 10, "iki günlük gezi planına (48 saat) göz atın". The "(48 saat)" is odd: two days = 48 hours is self-evident, and no Turkish writer would add it; it reads like a label or SEO tag. Also "iki günlük plan" repeated in the next sentence.

22. Snippet 10, "İki günlük plan buna mevsiminde El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği'ni ve Ukayr kıyısını ekler." Stumbles: "buna" has no clear antecedent (this one-day plan?), "mevsiminde" floats (the season of what? which season? it could belong to "ekler" or to the farm), and I cannot tell whether "El-Ahsa Misket Limonu Çiftliği" is a proper name or a description. "Ukayr kıyısı" is a name I have never seen; there is no hint it is a place on the sea. Missing: what Ukayr is, which season.

23. Snippet 11, "Bu kalenin duvarları çamurdan, çatıları hurma ağacı gövdelerindendir." The suffix chain "gövdelerindendir" is heavy; I had to read it again to understand "roofs are made of palm trunks". Also "Bu kalenin" right after "Kasr İbrahim ile başlayın" is fine, but "Hüfuf'un merkezinde Kasr İbrahim ile başlayın" reads as if the fortress is the starting point of the whole route; consistent with snippet 8 which says the route runs between El-Kut and the market, not necessarily starting at the fortress.

24. Snippets 4-5, "Kasr İbrahim". "Kasr" may be read for a moment as a misspelling of "kasır"; after the first mention it is fine. The Turkish order "İbrahim Kasrı" would be more native but I understand it. Minor.

25. Cross-snippet: the market (16.00) opens exactly when the fortress closes (16.00). Nobody says so; for a Friday-minded reader this is fine, but the route intro does not tell me how to combine the two on a normal day (fortress morning, market after 16.00). Missing, not wrong.

26. Cross-snippet: snippets 1-3 give the market's hours three times in nearly the same form; snippet 1 uses a dot as separator ("·") and the middle dot reads fine. No problem found there.
