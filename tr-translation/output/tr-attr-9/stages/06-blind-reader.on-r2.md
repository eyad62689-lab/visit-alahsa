# Blind reader notes (Turkish reader), on-r2

## Page 1 - El-Ahsa Evi Müzesi

1. "Ana sayfa / Gezilecek yerler / El-Ahsa Evi Müzesi Müzeler El-Ahsa Evi Müzesi" - The breadcrumb runs straight into the category and the title with no separator, so the museum name appears twice in a row and reads like a stutter. It is probably layout, but as text it is confusing.
2. "“En-Naasil” müzesi" - I did not know how to pronounce "Naasil" (double a: long vowel? two syllables?). The word is also never explained. It appears in the lede and again as "En-Naasil Müzesi adıyla da bilinir", so I expected a meaning and got none. If the meaning is not known, a plain statement of the alternative name is enough.
3. "meclis salonları" / "Erkeklerin meclis salonunda ... kadınların meclis salonunda" - To a Turk "meclis" is parliament or assembly. I first pictured parliament halls inside a house. Only the context (coffee pots, furniture) corrected me. It is also never said what these rooms were for, so the reader has to guess. Something like "oturma/misafir salonu" would work, or a one-time explanation.
4. Lede vs. body - The lede calls the museum "eski bir El-Ahsa evi", but the body says the founder built it "kendi evinin dış avlusunda". I could not tell whether the museum is an old house, or a new building or room set in the courtyard of the founder's own house. The sentence "eski ev yalnızca sergilenmekle kalmaz" at the end again suggests it is the old house itself, so the page contradicts itself or is left hanging here.
5. "kendi evinin dış avlusunda kurdu" - "dış avlu" sounds like a calque ("outer courtyard"). No Turkish writer would say it that way, and "kendi evinin" is unclear: is that house the museum or a different one?
6. "Odalar geleneksel yaşamın düzenini izler." - Abstract and empty. "Follows the order of traditional life" gave me no picture and I read it twice. It sounds translated.
7. "El-Ahsa kahve ibrikleri" - I could not tell what makes the pots "El-Ahsa" ones (a type? a make?). It reads as if I should already know.
8. "Müzenin alanı yaklaşık 180 metrekaredir." - It lands out of nowhere between the founding sentence and the room sentence. It is unclear whether the 180 m² includes the courtyard or the house.
9. "böylece eski ev yalnızca sergilenmekle kalmaz, yaşatılır" - Brochure slogan. It is a sudden jump in register from the flat inventory above it. "Sergilenmek" for a house is odd, and it repeats "yeniden canlandırır" from the first sentence. It also raises a question the page does not answer: which events, and when?
10. "Hüfuf" (repeated) - I read "Hüfuf" twice before realising it is a place name. Turkish texts usually write Hofuf or Hufuf, so it did not register as a known city on the first read. The page never says that Hüfuf is the main town of El-Ahsa.
11. "Saatler ve ücretler değişebilir" - No fee is shown on this page, so the sentence mentions something that is not there. I could not tell whether entry is free or whether the fee is simply missing.
12. "ziyaretten önce işletmeci ile teyit edin" - "teyit" and "işletmeci" are bureaucratic and bookish ("doğrulayın" would be plainer). For a museum run by the founder's family, "işletmeci" is also odd. No phone, address or other way to reach the "işletmeci" is given, so I could not tell how I would confirm anything.

## Page 2 - El-Halife Miras Müzesi

1. Lede, "halk çarşısı biçiminde kurulmuş özel müze" - "halk çarşısı" is unclear. A "people's bazaar"? A folk-culture bazaar? "Özel müze" is also ambiguous: private or special? I took it as private only from the body text.
2. Lede, "geleneksel sergileri El-Ahsa'nın eski zanaatlarını ve gündelik yaşamını kapsıyor" - "geleneksel sergileri" is an odd phrase (traditional exhibits?). The present continuous "-yor" (also "kapsıyor") is out of step with the aorist "-ir" used in the body, and "kapsıyor" is abstract.
3. "El-Kaysariye Çarşısı" - I first read "Kaysariye" as "Kayseri" or a Kayseri-style covered bazaar, and I took "Kaysariye" for a Turkish word. It is not explained what the model bazaar is or where it is, yet the whole museum is defined by it, three times.
4. "Eş-Şube ile El-Kureyn köyleri" / "Eş-Şurufiye mahallesi" - "Şube" reads as the Turkish word "şube" (branch, office), so "Eş-Şube" looked like "the branch". "Eş-Şurufiye" is heavy to read and to pronounce. Neither is a problem if the reader knows them, but I did not.
5. "Müberrez'in kuzeyinde" - Müberrez is never introduced. I could not tell if it is a town, a village or a district. The page assumes I know it.
6. Location contradiction - The first sentence puts the museum north of Müberrez, between two villages. Later: "Ev, Müberrez'in Eş-Şurufiye mahallesindedir." So is the museum in Müberrez or north of it? Is the house the museum or a different place? The page raises this and never answers.
7. "En öne çıkan özel müzelerinden biridir ve ... Müzeler Kurumundan ruhsat almıştır" - "En öne çıkan" is brochure puffery and unsupported. "Ruhsat" is bureaucratic. "Müzeler Kurumundan" reads like a missing apostrophe or a half-proper name (I could not tell if it is a formal institution name).
8. "evinin bahçesinde kurmaya başladı" - "Kurmaya başladı" (began to set up) and then "müze bu sürede büyüyerek bugünkü biçimini aldı" - I could not tell whether the museum is still in the garden, or whether it moved or grew into something bigger.
9. Repetition - The bazaar claim is made in the lede, in the body ("El-Kaysariye Çarşısı'nın eski hâlini andıran bir halk çarşısı") and again on page 4's "başka miras müzeleri" list. The same words come back almost unchanged.
10. "yirmiden fazla küçük dükkân" - It is unclear whether these shops are display set-ups (a model street) or working shops. "Yeniden canlandıran" suggests living history, but I could not tell what I would do there: watch, buy, or just walk through?
11. "Müzede silahlar, kahve ibrikleri, ..., kadın giysileri ve takıları, eski öğretim gereçleri sergilenir. Müzede ayrıca ..." - Two sentences in a row start with "Müzede", and the list is long, with "ve ... ve" chains in the second one. "Kadın giysileri ve takıları" was read twice.
12. "Aramco dönemine ayrılmış bir köşe" - I did not know what "Aramco dönemi" refers to, and "köşe" (corner) sits oddly with "bölümler". The page does not say what the period is.
13. "Bir meclis salonu da geleneksel El-Ahsa tarzında düzenlenmiştir." - Again "meclis salonu" reads as parliament hall first. The sentence is also tacked on at the end, so it feels like an afterthought.
14. Footer - Hours are given, but there is no fee, and the page still warns that "ücretler değişebilir". "İşletmeci ile teyit edin" has the same bureaucratic register as on the other pages and no contact.

## Page 3 - Abdürrezzak el-Arab Müzesi

1. Lede, "eski okul müfredatlarından oluşan nadir koleksiyonuyla" - "Müfredat" is already a plural-sounding Arabic word, and "müfredatlar" sounds foreign. More importantly, I could not tell what a "collection of curricula" physically is (textbooks? syllabi? notebooks?). I read it twice. The body says "okul müfredatlarından ve öğretim gereçlerinden", which is again vague.
2. "Müze, belgelenmiş uzmanlık alanıyla en çok tanınır: Suudi Arabistan'da eğitimin tarihi." - "Belgelenmiş uzmanlık alanı" is a calque ("documented specialty") that no Turkish writer would put this way, and I did not know what it means. Documented by whom?
3. Lede vs. body - The lede is only about the school curricula. The body first says it brings together the folk-culture heritage of El-Ahsa, then says its specialty is the history of education, and only at the end returns to folk culture. I did not know what the museum mainly is.
4. "Müzenin koleksiyonu resmî ilgi gördü." - Bookish and foreign ("received official attention"). I could not tell what "resmî ilgi" is; it sounds like an invented phrase. The next sentence appears to explain it.
5. "Suudi Arabistan'ın turizm ve millî miras otoritesinin El-Ahsa ofisi" - A stack of genitives; I lost the thread before the verb. "Otorite" and "ofis" are foreign-sounding and a little casual next to "millî". It was not clear whether this is one body or a tourism authority plus a heritage authority.
6. "Projede 150'den fazla eski müfredat, yaklaşık 750 saatlik çalışmayla sayısallaştırıldı." - The number parsed oddly. I took "750 saatlik çalışma" as the total effort, but it can also be read as a duration. "Sayısallaştırıldı" is textbook-TDK; in everyday Turkish people say "dijitalleştirildi". And it is again unclear what a "müfredat" is as an object.
7. "eğitim" / "öğrenim" / "öğretim" - Three near-synonyms (eğitimin tarihi, öğrenimin nasıl başladığı, öğretim gereçleri, eğitim koleksiyonu) with no clear logic. I was not sure if the museum covers schooling, learning or teaching.
8. "Bu da müzeyi, Suudi Arabistan'da öğrenimin nasıl başladığının izini sürmek isteyen ziyaretçiler için kendine özgü bir durak yapar." - A very long chain: "öğrenimin nasıl başladığının izini sürmek isteyen ziyaretçiler" stacks several genitives and possessives. I read it twice. "Nasıl başladığı" also promises an origin story the page has not told: nothing dated is given.
9. Hours, "Akşamları 16.30–21.30" - "Akşam" (evening) does not fit 16.30, which is mid-afternoon for a Turkish reader. "Akşamları" and the times seem to contradict each other.
10. "önceden randevuyla" - How to get an appointment is missing. There is no contact on the page, and I could not tell if a walk-in visit is possible at all.
11. Footer - No fee given, but "ücretler değişebilir" is stated. Same bureaucratic "teyit / işletmeci" wording.
12. Name, "Abdürrezzak el-Arab" - "El-Arab" first read as "Arap" (the people), and I was not sure if it is a surname or a descriptor. The Ottoman-style "Abdürrezzak" is fine but is a register shift from the more modern "Suudi Arabistan".

## Page 4 - Dar et-Turas

1. Title, "Dar et-Turas" - "Dar" reads as the Turkish word for "narrow", so I first took it as "narrow [something]". The meaning ("Miras Evi") is given only once, in parentheses in the body, not in the title or the lede.
2. Lede, "El-Ahsa'nın eski üslubuyla yapılmış bir ev; ... barındırıyor" - "Üslup" is bookish, and the present continuous "-yor" (also "barındırıyor") is again out of step with the "-ir" of the body. The page calls it "bir ev" in the lede and "özel bir müzedir" in the body, so I was not sure which it is.
3. "Bunu, ailesinden devraldığı kültürel miras biriktirme geleneğini sürdürerek yaptı." - A noun stack: "kültürel miras biriktirme geleneği" plus a participial "devraldığı". I read it twice. "Bunu ... yaptı" is a weak link to the sentence before.
4. "Ev, geliştirilmiş hâliyle Mayıs 2017'de açıldı." - Unclear. Does "geliştirilmiş hâliyle" mean it was open before and was upgraded, or that it opened already developed? The page raises the question and does not answer it.
5. "bazılarının geçmişi üç yüz yıldan fazla geriye uzanır" - "Geçmişi ... geriye uzanır" is redundant (past + back). "Bazılarının" has an unclear referent: the swords, the helmets, or all the weapons? "Üç yüz yıldan fazla" is also not anchored to any date.
6. "Koleksiyona fosilleşmiş hayvanlar da dâhildir." - "Fosilleşmiş hayvanlar" sounds foreign (a Turk says "hayvan fosilleri") and it made me smile. It is also dropped in after weapons with no connection. "Dâhildir" with the circumflex is bookish.
7. "Binanın kendisi de sergiden sayılır; eski yerel üslubuyla yeşilliklerin arasında durur." - "Sergiden sayılır" is stiff and "durur" is vague. "Eski yerel üslubuyla" repeats "eski üslup" from the lede and body, so the same idea is made three times.
8. Section "El-Ahsa'daki başka miras müzeleri" - It arrives after the description without any link to Dar et-Turas. The first sentence ("El-Ahsa'da başka miras müzeleri de vardır.") is filler. Each museum appears as a bare description with no hours, no distance and no sense of whether I should go.
9. "Hüfuf'taki El-Ahsa Müzesi ise vahanın resmî devlet müzesidir" - "Resmî devlet müzesi" is redundant. "El-Ahsa Müzesi" and "El-Ahsa Evi Müzesi" (the previous page) look almost the same, and I confused them on the first pass. The sentence also makes a claim about a museum that has no page here.
10. Footer, "Kişi başı 20 Suudi riyali (SAR)" - "(SAR)" in parentheses is an abbreviation Turks would not usually write, and it parses like a code. The fee is given "kişi başı" here but not on the next page (see Page 5 point 12). There is no mention of children or discounts, so I could not tell if the 20 riyals applies to everyone.

## Page 5 - Salih ez-Zafer Müzesi

1. Title, "ez-Zafer" - "Zafer" is a common Turkish word and name (victory), so "ez-Zafer" first read as a Turkish word with a strange prefix. It also means "Salih" reads as a Turkish first name followed by a mistake.
2. Lede, "koleksiyon Suudi Arabistan'da türünün ilki" - After the semicolon this is a verbless fragment ("the collection [is] the first of its kind in Saudi Arabia"). It sounds abrupt and foreign.
3. Lede, "benzersiz izcilik koleksiyonuyla ünlü" - "Benzersiz" is brochure puffery. "Ünlü" is also hard to believe from an outside reader's point of view.
4. "Müzeyi farklı kılan şey ünlü izcilik müzesidir. Bu izcilik müzesi, ..." - It is unclear whether the scouting "museum" is a separate museum inside this one, a hall, or a collection. The lede and the last sentence say "koleksiyon", so the page switches between "müze" and "koleksiyon".
5. "Müzeyi farklı kılan şey" - A calque of "what sets the museum apart". No Turkish writer would phrase it this way.
6. "Suudi Arabistan'da türünün ilki olarak tanımlanır" - Described by whom? The passive hides the source, so "first of its kind" reads as an unsupported claim and I could not tell how much to trust it.
7. "Koleksiyondaki nadir parçalar şu bölümlere ayrılmıştır:" - Bureaucratic ("ayrılmıştır"), and the colon list that follows ("konuk ağırlama ve kahve, tarım ve sulama, ...") is fine on its own but dry.
8. "belgesel fotoğraflar" - In Turkish "belgesel" almost always means documentary film, so I paused. I took it to mean documentary photographs.
9. "izcilik hareketinin onlarca yıllık geçmişini anlatır" - Vague: which decades, and which scouting movement? Nothing dated is given, so the sentence says little.
10. "Halk kültürü ve izcilik koleksiyonları birlikte müzeyi El-Ahsa'nın en kendine özgü özel müzelerinden biri yapar." - "Özgü" and "özel" next to each other jingle, and "özel müze" can be read as "special museum". "Birlikte ... yapar" is a literal construction. This is the third "one of the most ..." claim in a row (compare Page 2), so it is repetitive.
11. Location, "Hüfuf'un güneyinde" / "Hüfuf'un güneyi — El-Ahsa" - Only a direction, with no village, street or landmark. I could not tell where in the south, so I would not know how to plan getting there.
12. Hours, "Akşamları, önceden rezervasyon ve randevuyla" - No times at all (Page 3 gives times for a similar arrangement). "Rezervasyon ve randevu" is redundant, and how to book is missing: no phone or booking channel.
13. Fee, "15 Suudi riyali (SAR)" - It does not say per person, per group, or per what, unlike Page 4 ("Kişi başı"). "(SAR)" in parentheses is the same stiff abbreviation as on Page 4. The same "teyit / işletmeci" footer follows.
