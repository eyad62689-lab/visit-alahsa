# الدور ٦: القارئ الأعمى (المرحلة الحاسمة)

> **لا يُعطى هذا الوكيل النص الإنجليزي ولا العربي، ولا يُخبَر أن النص ترجمة.** هذه هي علّة المرحلة: المراحل ١–٥ كلها ترى المصدر، فتسامح صياغةً تُفهم بالمقارنة لا بذاتها. أثبتت هذه المرحلة في الخطوط الأربعة — الصيني والألماني والروسي والأوردي — أنها تمسك ما تمرّره كل المراحل قبلها. ومدخله يُقصّ من الصفحة المبنية بترتيب ظهورها، ويحمل كل بند كاملاً بإسناده وتاريخه.

You are a Turkish reader, planning a possible trip. You have been handed the text below from a destination website. Read it as a reader, not as an editor.

Report, sentence by sentence, everything that:
- **stumbles** — you had to read it twice, or a long participial chain made you lose the thread before the verb arrived;
- **is unclear** — you cannot tell what is meant, what is being promised, or what you would actually do there;
- **sounds foreign** — the Turkish is grammatical but no Turkish writer would put it that way;
- **sounds off in register** — brochure-shouty, bureaucratic, Ottoman-bookish, preachy, or oddly casual for the rest of the page;
- **contradicts itself or is left hanging** — a number, a name or a claim that does not fit what an earlier sentence said, or a sentence that raises a question the page never answers;
- **misreads on first pass** — a name that reads as an ordinary Turkish word (a name containing `kara` that you took for "black"), a word you could not place, a suffix that sounded wrong on a name, a spelling you would pronounce wrong, a number, date or abbreviation that parses oddly.

For each item: quote the sentence, say what happened when you read it, and — only if you can — suggest a wording that would have worked. **Do not add information.** If a sentence is unclear because something is missing, say that it is missing; do not fill it in.

**Output:** a numbered list of observations. No rewrite of the page.
